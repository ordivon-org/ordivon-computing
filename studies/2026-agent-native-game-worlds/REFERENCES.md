## Interpretation limits

- Believable behavior does not prove human behavioral validity.
- Large Agent populations do not by themselves establish society or civilization.
- Open-ended training performance does not establish player enjoyment.
- Cross-game instruction following does not establish persistent character identity.
- Generated worlds do not remove the need for authored coherence and consequence.
- Minecraft results should not be treated as universal evidence for all world structures.

The references define a comparison set and source of hypotheses. Ordivon Game must test its own claims through bounded complete experiences.
