# 05 — Research Program and Decision Gates

## Phase W1 — one complete interaction

Run a real Host Task through network/target Observation, the inherited
Cloudflare Fetch/Browser provider, Receipt and Artifact production, response
loss or Host restart, reconciliation, Verification and continuation.

## Phase W2 — minimization

Record broad evidence, then remove fields and layers. Measure duplicate Effects,
false recovery, stale Claims, authority confusion, recovery time, operator
intervention and implementation complexity.

## Phase W3 — second workload

Use either:

- asynchronous Agent/service Artifact review with uncertain delivery,
  identity rotation and participant handoff; or
- external Sandbox with writable work, remote-to-remote Artifact movement and
  provider replacement.

## Phase W4 — dynamic topology

Exercise local→remote, remote→local, remote→remote, one→many and many→one.
Change path, transport, provider, body, identity and participant independently
and jointly.

## Phase W5 — second-consumer and architecture test

Determine whether Host, Security and at least one domain share a compact
responsibility or merely share vocabulary. Compare:

- independent World layer;
- Host-local composition;
- provider adapters plus observation sources;
- no shared abstraction.

## Admission gates

A stable World contract requires:

1. two materially different workloads;
2. at least two real consumers;
3. a failure that direct integration does not prevent cleanly;
4. lower total complexity or materially stronger recovery/evidence;
5. independent revision and invalidation semantics;
6. a successful deletion test for every retained field.

## Anti-roadmap

Until those gates pass, do not build:

- automatic path/provider routing;
- a universal Interaction or World schema;
- a global identity or permanent external Agent presence;
- a network, Sandbox, workflow, queue, storage or identity platform;
- a central data relay;
- compatibility protocols between internal modules.
