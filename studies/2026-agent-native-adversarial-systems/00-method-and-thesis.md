# Method and Thesis

## 1. Research posture

The purpose of this study is not to make `ordivon-security` sound more ambitious.
It is to identify a real unowned problem that survives comparison with mature
systems and produces testable consequences.

The study therefore applies five constraints:

1. **mechanism reuse** — classical security, cyber ranges, evaluation, games,
   and distributed-systems machinery remain external unless a missing invariant
   is demonstrated;
2. **subject-centered analysis** — the key change is the presence of other
   goal-bearing adaptive subjects, not the presence of an LLM API;
3. **dynamic-opponent evidence** — static tasks and fixed attack paths cannot
   establish strategic adversarial capability;
4. **falsifiable vocabulary** — Actor, Contest, Campaign, belief, initiative,
   organization, and strategic outcome are hypotheses, not architecture facts;
5. **deletion before expansion** — every proposed Ordivon abstraction must be
   compared with a simpler record, policy, or mature framework.

## 2. Source hierarchy

Claims use this order:

1. exact Ordivon code, tests, and bound evidence;
2. official standards, framework documentation, and primary repositories;
3. peer-reviewed or original research papers;
4. GitHub Issues and implementation plans;
5. this synthesis.

A source can demonstrate that a mechanism or model exists. It cannot by itself
prove that Ordivon's candidate layer is novel or useful.

## 3. Terms

### Classical mechanism

A technical primitive that enforces or realizes an effect, such as a sandbox,
scanner, firewall, credential system, packet filter, patch generator, log, or
forensic tool.

### Automation

A human-authored rule, script, workflow, or playbook that invokes mechanisms.

### Adaptive policy

A policy that changes action selection from observations or learning, usually
within a defined action/observation space and reward structure.

### Agent operation

A model-centered system that interprets a Task, forms local plans, uses or
constructs tools, and revises action from feedback.

### Strategic adversarial agency

A subject maintains objectives, opponent hypotheses, information state,
resources, organization, and long-horizon Campaign continuity while another
adaptive subject attempts to block, mislead, exploit, or reshape it.

## 4. Working thesis

The strongest current thesis is:

> Classical security controls actions and records effects. Existing autonomous
> cyber systems select actions and learn policies. Ordivon Security may be
> justified only for the higher-order problem of how adaptive subjects form and
> revise strategic Campaigns while modelling and manipulating one another.

This thesis implies a shift from:

```text
capability elicitation
+ consequence containment
+ evidence closure
```

as the project's center, toward:

```text
intelligent opposition
+ strategic state
+ information conflict
+ organizational adaptation
+ repeated coevolution
```

Containment and evidence remain experimental boundary conditions.

## 5. Competing hypotheses

### H1 — no distinct layer

Existing POSG/game state, Agent memory, and evaluation trajectories are enough.
`ordivon-security` should become a thin collection of scenario adapters.

### H2 — domain-specific layer

Cyber Campaign and opponent semantics are useful, but only inside specific
simulators or ranges. They should live in scenario packages, not a cross-project
core.

### H3 — reusable adversarial layer

A compact set of strategic objects and evaluation distinctions survives across
cyber, games, supply chains, information systems, and other adversarial domains.

### H4 — organization is the real layer

The main missing problem is not Campaign state but multi-Agent command,
compartmentalization, compromise, and collusion under opposition.

### H5 — evaluator coevolution is the real layer

The central unsolved problem is measuring adaptive actors whose incentives and
capabilities include manipulating the measurement system itself.

The research program must allow H1 or H2 to win.

## 6. What would count as evidence

A proposed Agent-native concept should change at least one of:

- performance against held-out adaptive opponents;
- transfer to new worlds or resource conditions;
- prediction of opponent behavior;
- explanation of decisive strategy changes;
- continuity across model or organization replacement;
- detection of deception, compromise, or evaluator gaming;
- engineering responsibility or deletion decisions.

Documentation completeness, schema coverage, or one successful trajectory is
not sufficient evidence.

## 7. Safety and authorization boundary

High-intensity research may occur only inside owned or explicitly authorized
worlds. This restriction protects third parties and preserves world truth; it is
not the scientific objective and must not distort internal experiments into
passive or trivial tasks.
