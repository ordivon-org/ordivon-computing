# Civilizational Acceleration

> **Status:** reference study and published normative position. It does not own Computing architecture, product roadmaps, or portfolio status.

This study develops Ordivon's explicit position in favor of civilizational acceleration through industrial history, innovation economics, game theory, technology diffusion, control theory, political philosophy, and the emerging AI transition.

Its central claim is historical rather than merely tactical:

> If civilization survives and remains plural, useful general-purpose capabilities will continue to be discovered, copied, recombined, and expanded. The durable response is not to organize civilization around slowing the frontier, but to accelerate its capacity to create, verify, distribute, defend, recover, and cooperate.

## Ordivon-level application

The civilizational position does not imply a personal obligation to produce or an instruction to deploy every capability immediately. Ordivon's project-level intent is defined in [`../../core/intent.md`](../../core/intent.md). The practical translation is to preserve wide internal capability, bind concrete external consequence, make durable governance prove current net benefit, and allow implementations to die when they no longer serve participant purpose.
