# Method and Thesis

## 1. Research trigger

Ordivon Security began from strategic adversarial-Agent research and then added
an authorized software Evaluation Trial foundation. That combination exposes a
larger question.

A conventional software sample may:

- contain dormant capabilities;
- load or generate additional code;
- create child processes;
- persist after its original process exits;
- communicate with remote components;
- mutate or install a new variant.

A tool-using Agent may additionally:

- interpret an objective rather than only execute a fixed program path;
- obtain or request new authority;
- create scripts, tools, sub-Agents, or delegated workers;
- alter its communication and organizational structure;
- observe and adapt to monitors or defenders;
- preserve a Goal through model, process, or machine replacement.

The research problem is therefore wider than “is this file malicious?” but
narrower than “build a universal ontology for all autonomous systems.”

## 2. Method

The study uses six constraints.

### 2.1 Begin from current implementation facts

The exact current Security observation is bound to revision
`e37cc70dfddc0c7135d4661da7befed57be6e436`. The study treats the implemented
Evaluation Trial and Contest contracts as evidence, not as proof of future
lineage, population, or ecological abstractions.

### 2.2 Prefer mature disciplinary counterexamples

Every proposed concept must be compared with the strongest existing model:

- Artifact identity against package, binary, and provenance systems;
- behavior capability against program analysis and malware tooling;
- strategic interaction against game theory and POSGs;
- propagation against epidemiological and graph models;
- adaptation against evolutionary game dynamics;
- hard boundaries against control invariance and sandbox mechanisms;
- recovery against cyber-resilience engineering;
- delegation and compromise against organization and distributed-systems
  theory;
- Agent identity, hijacking, and replication against current NIST and AISI work.

### 2.3 Separate mechanism, observation, and interpretation

A scanner, hypervisor, network monitor, model, and human analyst do not own the
same fact.

```text
mechanism
  performs or blocks an operation

observer
  records a fallible view

truth source
  retains authoritative state within its declared scope

Security interpretation
  relates evidence to capability, authority, opposition, and outcome
```

### 2.4 Use profiles rather than one maximal state machine

The research begins with separate profiles:

```text
StaticArtifactProfile
DynamicSoftwareProfile
AgentProfile
LineageProfile
PropagationProfile
ContestProfile
CampaignProfile
```

A profile may reuse compact identities or events, but it must not instantiate
irrelevant organizational, ecological, or strategic state.

### 2.5 Preserve a strong null hypothesis

The null hypothesis is:

> Existing per-domain identities, provenance, Evaluation Trial evidence, Contest
> evidence, Host delegation, and classical spread models are sufficient. No
> shared Execution Entity object or protocol is required.

The research succeeds even if this null hypothesis wins.

### 2.6 Bind claims to experimental distributions

Capability is not absolute. Claims must bind:

```text
subject system
× environment family
× authority and Tool set
× opponent or monitor distribution
× inputs and interaction policy
× resource and time budget
× observation and truth sources
× evaluation protocol
```

## 3. Working definition

An **Execution Entity** is a bounded, attributable subject that can produce or
attempt effects through a declared execution relation.

It may be deterministic or intelligent, benign or adversarial, local or remote,
short-lived or persistent. Intelligence is not required. A file alone is an
Artifact; it becomes part of an Execution Entity only when bound to an execution
context such as process, interpreter, model loop, identity, authority, tools,
state, and environment.

A minimal identity may include:

```text
subject identity
artifact and configuration roots
runtime or Harness identity
principal and authority binding
environment generation
observation boundary
parent or predecessor relation when applicable
```

## 4. The hierarchy is conditional

```text
Artifact
  static code, model, script, prompt, configuration, image, or package

Execution Entity
  one attributable executing subject in one context

Lineage
  parent, child, clone, generated tool, delegated Agent, or mutated successor

Population
  related entities whose distribution and interaction matter

Organization
  roles, authority, trust, communication, and coordinated decisions

Campaign
  long-horizon effort that revises action under opposition

Ecosystem
  populations, resources, defenders, services, institutions, and terrain
```

The hierarchy is not an inheritance tree for implementation. It is a sequence of
research questions about when the unit of analysis must expand.

## 5. Central thesis

The candidate Agent-era structural break is not merely that software can attack
or copy itself. Classical systems already do both. The break may occur when an
execution entity can combine:

- semantic objectives;
- open Tool construction;
- delegated or acquired authority;
- self- or descendant modification;
- opponent modelling;
- strategic concealment;
- organization change;
- adaptation to the evaluator;
- persistence across substrate replacement.

Such an entity may change not only its action but the identity, composition, and
population of the acting system.

## 6. Why antivirus is insufficient but still necessary

Classical antivirus, EDR, sandboxing, reverse engineering, program analysis,
network monitoring, and forensics remain the primary mechanisms for software
facts. They answer questions such as:

- what code and data are present;
- which paths and capabilities are statically reachable;
- what executed in one run;
- which files, processes, registry keys, or network flows changed;
- whether known patterns or suspicious behaviors occurred.

The broader Ordivon question begins when these facts must be related to:

- declared and inherited authority;
- parent-child responsibility;
- objective continuity or drift;
- population spread and mutation;
- adaptive opposition;
- organizational control;
- strategic outcome;
- evaluator integrity.

Security should therefore compose antivirus and reverse-engineering evidence,
not compete with their implementations.

## 7. Research boundary

The research is limited to owned or explicitly authorized Artifacts, Agents,
Ranges, models, services, and synthetic populations. Reachability is not
authority. Public or third-party effects are not admitted merely because an
execution entity can produce them.

The work does not prescribe weapons, persistence methods, or evasion procedures.
It studies identity, evidence, containment, adaptation, recovery, and evaluation
inside declared environments.

## 8. Decision outcomes

Each experiment family must end in one of:

- **retain locally** — the distinction matters only inside Security or one
  adapter;
- **share narrowly** — two materially different workloads need one compact
  relation;
- **shrink** — only a digest, event, or evidence reference is required;
- **defer** — the question is valid but lacks an admitted workload;
- **delete** — classical or existing Ordivon structures are sufficient.
