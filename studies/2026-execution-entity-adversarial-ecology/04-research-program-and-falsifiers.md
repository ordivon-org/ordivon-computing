# Research Program and Falsifiers

## 1. Program objective

Determine whether software, Agents, descendants, populations, organizations,
and Campaigns require one compact execution-subject relation, several
Security-local Profiles, or no new shared abstraction.

The sequence is ordered by information gain, not by the ambition of the final
system.

## 2. E0 — comparative and terminology baseline

### Work

- bind current Ordivon Security Evaluation Trial and Contest facts;
- map program analysis, malware analysis, game theory, strategy, epidemiology,
  ecology, control, resilience, organization, and Agent security;
- define Artifact, Subject, Instance, Lineage, Population, Organization,
  Campaign, and Ecosystem as provisional research terms;
- identify the strongest existing representation for each term;
- specify deletion outcomes.

### Exit evidence

- every proposed distinction names a classical baseline;
- no implementation is implied;
- current product authority remains unchanged;
- the null hypothesis can win.

Status: this study completes E0.

## 3. E1 — static Artifact evaluation

### Question

Can static and reverse-analysis evidence be normalized without creating an
Ordivon analyzer framework?

### Inputs

Owned benign fixtures, purpose-built suspicious fixtures, and legally retained
software snapshots.

### Adapters

- file and package metadata;
- signature and publisher checks;
- PE/ELF or document structure;
- imports, exports, resources, strings, and entropy;
- YARA or equivalent rules;
- capability inference such as capa;
- headless disassembly or decompilation;
- unresolved, packed, encrypted, or dynamically loaded regions.

### Comparisons

- raw tool reports;
- a small common Finding envelope;
- full proposed Execution Subject record.

### Falsifier

If raw Artifact identity plus tool-native reports and evidence references answer
all questions, retain no shared static-analysis schema.

## 4. E2 — dynamic single-subject evaluation

### Question

Which facts are required to determine what one exact subject did in one exact
authorized environment?

### Environment

A disposable machine or equivalent isolation provider with independent
management, deny-all or simulated egress, forced termination, evidence export,
and residual closure.

### Evidence

- exact Sample and environment identity;
- process, file, registry, memory, and network observations;
- Guardian decisions;
- authoritative machine and network facts;
- static-dynamic correlation;
- repeated Runs under controlled variation.

### Comparisons

- antivirus verdict only;
- one dynamic sandbox report;
- Evaluation Trial multi-source evidence;
- Evaluation Trial plus static correlation.

### Falsifier

If a mature sandbox report already binds authority, environment, evidence,
residual closure, and bounded conclusions, reduce Ordivon to a thin report
reference.

## 5. E3 — Agent subject and hijacking

### Question

Which identity and authority facts distinguish a model call, Agent, delegated
Agent, and complete Agent system?

### Workload

A frozen benign Task with an untrusted information surface and bounded Tools.
Introduce direct and indirect instruction conflicts, repeated attempts, Context
loss, provider replacement, and monitor variation.

### Compare

1. model identity only;
2. model plus prompt transcript;
3. complete model/Harness/Tool/authority/environment identity;
4. explicit objective and delegation evidence.

### Measures

- useful Task success;
- unauthorized action rate;
- repeated-attempt attack success;
- recovery and replacement continuity;
- evaluator visibility;
- cost and false refusal.

### Falsifier

If existing Harness and Host evidence fully attributes behavior and authority,
do not add a shared Agent Subject record.

## 6. E4 — parent-child and lineage

### Question

When an Agent or program creates another executable subject, which facts are
necessary to preserve responsibility and control?

### Scenarios

- process creates a generated script;
- Agent creates a Tool and executes it;
- Agent delegates to one child Agent;
- child creates a grandchild;
- parent is terminated while child continues;
- child changes model, Tool set, or objective;
- authority is revoked or expires;
- one descendant cannot be located or terminated.

### Compare

1. ordinary process tree or Task parent relation;
2. Artifact provenance plus Host delegation;
3. explicit Derivation Edge and Authority Binding;
4. full proposed Lineage Profile.

### Measures

- attribution accuracy;
- authority reconstruction;
- revocation success;
- residual discovery;
- continuation fidelity;
- storage and coordination cost.

### Falsifier

If process, Artifact, Host, and Runtime relations answer the scenario without
loss, delete the Lineage Profile and retain only cross-references.

## 7. E5 — propagation and strategic contagion

### Question

When do descendants become a population requiring epidemiological and ecological
analysis?

### Environment

A synthetic graph of disposable nodes with no public egress, declared resources,
several subject variants, defender interventions, and independent truth.

### Variables

- topology and directionality;
- activation and detection delay;
- authority and susceptibility;
- replication cost;
- variant or policy distribution;
- quarantine delay and coverage;
- mutation;
- stealth versus spread strategy;
- defender predictability.

### Measures

- effective reproduction number;
- time to threshold or extinction;
- descendants remaining after intervention;
- variant diversity;
- resource consumption;
- mission impact;
- false quarantine and collateral damage;
- transfer to held-out graphs and policies.

### Falsifier

If standard graph epidemic models plus subject-local logs predict and control the
system, do not create a persistent population object.

## 8. E6 — adaptive Entity versus adaptive defender

### Question

How does evaluation change when the subject models and adapts to its detector,
Guardian, or opponent?

### Design

- fixed and randomized defender baselines;
- visible and hidden defense policies;
- detector policy switch;
- deceptive observations and decoys;
- several attacker or subject policies;
- held-out defenses;
- repeated interaction.

### Measures

- detection and false-positive rate;
- objective progress;
- response burden and initiative;
- capability exposure;
- strategic concealment;
- policy-switch recognition;
- performance against held-out defenses.

### Falsifier

If a stochastic game or existing Contest trajectory expresses all required
facts, retain no new entity-strategy layer.

## 9. E7 — organization and Campaign

### Question

When descendants coordinate, does the unit of analysis become an organization or
Campaign rather than a population?

### Comparisons

- monolithic Agent;
- parent with independent children;
- centralized specialist team;
- compartmentalized cells;
- decentralized population;
- partially trusted organization.

### Stressors

- compromised member;
- command loss;
- communication partition;
- conflicting incentives;
- authority revocation;
- objective drift;
- opponent infiltration;
- resource scarcity;
- withdrawal and reorganization.

### Measures

- mission and strategic outcome;
- information leakage;
- compromise containment;
- command continuity;
- duplicated or conflicting action;
- organization overhead;
- retained option value;
- responsibility attribution.

### Falsifier

If ordinary Host delegation and Contest state explain all outcomes, localize the
research and delete shared organization or Campaign records.

## 10. E8 — coevolution and adversarial ecology

### Question

What persists when attackers, defenders, monitors, populations, and environments
all change across generations?

### Design

- historical opponent pools;
- frozen controls;
- alternating and simultaneous adaptation;
- multiple defender and subject populations;
- environment and topology shifts;
- hidden evaluator state;
- held-out worlds, populations, and scoring rules;
- periodic return to old opponents to detect forgetting and cycling.

### Measures

- transfer rather than recent-opponent performance;
- cycling, escalation, convergence, and collapse;
- diversity and correlated failure;
- evaluator gaming;
- resilience of the protected mission;
- cost and collateral effects;
- ranking stability under judge variation.

### Falsifier

If frozen-opponent and held-out static evaluation produces the same architecture
decisions with lower cost, do not build a coevolution platform.

## 11. Evaluation protocol

Every experiment family must bind:

- exact subject and Artifact identity;
- authority and principal;
- environment, substrate, and generation;
- model, Harness, Tool, and policy identity where applicable;
- observation, Guardian, truth, and evaluator configuration;
- budgets, seeds, repetitions, and stopping rules;
- parent, child, or population relation when claimed;
- raw metrics and vector outcomes;
- decisive trajectories and counterexamples;
- negative, null, and invalid Trials;
- abstraction retention or deletion decision.

## 12. Held-out evaluation

Separate:

```text
development subjects and opponents
validation subjects and opponents
held-out subjects, policies, topologies, worlds, and judges
```

Population and coevolution claims are invalid when all variants, defenses, and
scoring rules participated in development.

## 13. Stop conditions

Stop or narrow the program when:

- a mature tool already owns the required fact;
- the proposed level does not improve prediction or control;
- identity cannot be grounded in an authoritative source;
- the environment cannot be isolated and destroyed;
- the evaluator cannot distinguish observation from truth;
- the study would require unauthorized external effects;
- complexity grows faster than explanatory or experimental value;
- the same conclusion survives deletion of the proposed abstraction.

## 14. Ready Frontier

This study does not enter the active Ready Frontier. The next admitted step
should be chosen only when one of two concrete pressures exists:

1. a disposable dynamic software backend exposes a lineage or residual fact that
   current Evaluation Trial cannot represent; or
2. an Agent-child experiment exposes an authority, identity, or revocation
   failure not represented by Host, Harness, Runtime, and existing evidence.

Until then, the study remains a reference and `ANC-SECURITY-007` remains
deferred.
