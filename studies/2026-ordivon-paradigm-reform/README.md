# Ordivon Paradigm Reform

> **Status:** closed historical audit and reform sequence. This directory does not own current architecture, repository state, portfolio status, or a permanent governance process.

## Result summary

The audit found a verification and representation overhang: multiple schemas, receipts, generated mirrors, audits, closeouts, and cross-repository checks were describing or re-proving the system faster than they improved product capability, research judgment, or recovery. The resulting reduction round applied Core A11 to validation machinery itself.

The durable conclusions were incorporated elsewhere:

- current shared purpose and architecture remain in [`../../core/`](../../core/) and [`../../docs/authority.md`](../../docs/authority.md);
- mutable research status remains only in [`../../research/portfolio.json`](../../research/portfolio.json);
- product implementation and operations remain in owning repositories;
- tests, evidence, protocols, audits, and generated views must retain a current consumer and positive recurring value;
- no retention registry, governance service, or universal research-object schema was admitted.
