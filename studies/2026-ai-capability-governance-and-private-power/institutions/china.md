# China

Observed through: 2026-08-01

## Administrative structure

China governs public-facing generative AI through a combination of cybersecurity,
data, personal-information, algorithm-recommendation, deep-synthesis, generative-
AI, content, app-distribution, security-assessment, and filing/registration rules.

Services with public-opinion or social-mobilization attributes must complete
filing or registration processes. Public announcements require deployed products
to display the model name and filing or registration identifier.

By 2026-04-30, official reporting stated that 868 generative-AI services had
completed filing and 530 applications or functions using filed models had
completed registration. These counts describe administrative scale, not safety,
quality, freedom, or enforcement error.

## 2026 anthropomorphic-interaction rules

Rules effective 2026-07-15 add safety assessment, algorithm filing and annual
verification, app-store duties, official inspection/cooperation, corrective
orders, registration suspension, service suspension, and fines for covered
anthropomorphic interaction services.

## Power analysis

China's model makes state administrative approval and Provider/platform operations
closely coupled. Providers retain implementation and account power, while public
agencies exercise filing, inspection, content, data, distribution, and service-
continuation authority.

The public filing lists improve service identity legibility. Independent merits
appeal, technical evidence access, and user-level contestability are less visible
in the reviewed public materials.
