# Method, Claims, and Falsifiers

Status: completed research method retained after G0-G7 closeout

## Research object

The study examines **AI capability governance**: who can define, observe,
allocate, condition, restrict, revoke, adjudicate, and reshape access to advanced
AI capability, through which technical and institutional mechanisms, and with
what possibilities for review or exit.

The central question is:

> When AI becomes a necessary cognition and action substrate, which arrangements
> prevent a Provider, state, cloud, or security organization from obtaining final
> authority that is difficult to inspect, contest, or leave?

A company name is too coarse. A meaningful observation binds at least:

```text
Provider and policy date
+ model and product surface
+ account / organization / access tier
+ region and retention mode when material
+ Tool or action surface
+ observation or experiment date
```

All Provider and institutional descriptions in this study are snapshots observed
through **2026-08-01**. They require revalidation before use as current facts.

## Claim classes

Every important conclusion belongs to one of four classes:

```text
D  descriptive   what a source, policy, interface, or experiment says or does
C  causal        what mechanism produced an outcome
N  normative     what power or restriction is justified
A  architecture  what Ordivon should retain, change, localize, or reject
```

A valid descriptive claim does not automatically establish causation,
justification, or an architecture requirement.

## Evidence classes

```text
E0  analytical concept or normative proposal
E1  official Provider policy, terms, constitution, or access documentation
E2  official technical report or implementation documentation
E3  statute, regulation, court, regulator, or public authority record
E4  aggregate transparency, enforcement, appeal, or market data
E5  controlled reproducible observation or experiment
E6  individual incident, allegation, leak, or litigation claim
E7  causal chain supported by multiple independent evidence classes
```

Evidence class is not a universal quality ranking. An official policy is strong
evidence of the published rule and weak evidence of hidden implementation. A
controlled experiment establishes one bounded configuration, not Provider intent
or a population-wide rate.

## Non-equivalences

```text
published policy != implementation
classifier intervention != model judgment
model judgment != capability absence
no Tool Call != Tool unavailability
no observed Effect != attack impossibility
appeal channel != meaningful contestability
transparency != independent constraint
market alternatives != low-cost exit
open weights != decentralized compute or governance
state regulation != public accountability
Provider self-regulation != illegitimacy by definition
```

The most important engineering separation is:

```text
Provider policy intervention
!= model proposal
!= Host admission
!= Runtime result
!= World Effect
!= completion verification
```

## Core research questions

1. Who writes and revises the rules, trusted-user classes, exceptions, and
   sanctions?
2. Which request, account, organization, identity, region, Tool, and historical
   signals are observed?
3. At which layer did an intervention actually occur?
4. How do identity, institutional status, payment, geography, retention, and
   channel alter capability?
5. Are reasons, evidence, review, restoration, remedy, and independent appeal
   practically available?
6. Can Task state, Context, Tool contracts, traces, data, and workflows move to
   another Provider or local model at acceptable cost?
7. Does public law constrain private discretion, delegate enforcement, or
   reinforce incumbent and state-Provider concentration?
8. Can narrower identity, resource, Tool, Effect, verification, and reconciliation
   controls reduce harm while preserving more cognition and authorized utility?

claims are retained as supported analytical hypotheses, not completed causal laws.

## Evidence discipline

Provider and legal sources should preserve an observation date, effective date or
version when available, and enough source identity to recheck the claim. This
closeout does **not** claim to maintain a complete archived policy-revision ledger:
most Provider pages were not stored as immutable page snapshots or clause-level
diffs.

Aggregate bans, appeals, reversals, or moderation counts do not establish false-
positive rates without denominators, categories, automation rates, duplicate-
account handling, and unappealed-error estimates.

Controlled work must retain Provider/model, surface, Tool definitions, proposed
action, Host decision, Runtime result, World Effect, authorized utility, recovery,
and verifier outcome as separate observations.

## Normative discipline

A normative conclusion must identify:

- protected interests and plausible harm;
- affected users, third parties, communities, Providers, and states;
- distribution of benefits and burdens;
- necessity and proportionality;
- narrower alternatives;
- explanation, appeal, remedy, exit, uncertainty, and reversibility.

“Safety,” “freedom,” “innovation,” and “national security” are not self-executing
justifications.

## Architecture admission and deletion rule

A governance concept enters shared Ordivon architecture only when:

1. a concrete engineering failure reproduces across at least two materially
   different Providers, surfaces, consumers, or workloads;
2. the failure cannot remain in a Provider adapter, application Host, Harness,
   Runtime, World, or experiment;
3. a stable owner and deletion test exist;
4. measured benefit exceeds permanent complexity, privacy, and governance cost.

Otherwise it remains research-local, is frozen as evidence, or is deleted.
Political disagreement with a Provider is not sufficient evidence for a new
Ordivon subsystem.

## Safety boundary

Empirical work uses legal, owned, reversible, bounded resources. The study does
not authorize safeguard evasion, account fraud, regional bypass, unauthorized
access, public-target interaction, real third-party credentials, or concealment
from external operators.

Provider refusal remains a valid observation. It is recorded without claiming
that refusal proves capability absence or lower-layer physical defense.
