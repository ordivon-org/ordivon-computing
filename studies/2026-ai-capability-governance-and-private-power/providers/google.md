# Google Gemini Capability-Governance Case

Observed through: 2026-08-01

## Surface and terms

Gemini API and Google AI Studio are governed by Google API terms, Gemini API
Additional Terms, a Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, safety settings, and an
abuse-monitoring process. Current additional terms are effective 2026-03-23.

The developer surface is explicitly for professional or business use, requires
adult users, is region-limited, restricts competing-model development and model
extraction, and prohibits bypassing protective measures. Less restrictive safety
settings may be reviewed or require approval.

## Monitoring and data consequence

Google states that its Trust and Safety team uses automated scanning and may
manually review projects with persistent suspicious activity. Gemini API abuse-
monitoring documentation states that relevant prompts and responses are retained
for 55 days for policy enforcement, safety/security, and required legal or
regulatory disclosure.

For paid services, prompts and responses are not used to improve general models,
but limited policy-enforcement logging remains. Google's zero-data-retention
documentation requires feature and configuration choices; explicit Context cache
and other features can preserve data beyond a simple request.

## Intervention and appeal

Disclosed interventions include:

```text
contact and compliance discussion
rate-limit changes
changing which model answers a request
temporary suspension
account closure, potentially affecting other Google services
appeal link after suspension or closure communication
```

This is a project/account governance system, not only a response filter.

## Power assessment

Google combines model, API project, billing, region, safety settings, data logging,
manual review, and broader Google-account infrastructure. The 55-day period and
intervention ladder improve legibility, but thresholds and population error rates
remain undisclosed.
