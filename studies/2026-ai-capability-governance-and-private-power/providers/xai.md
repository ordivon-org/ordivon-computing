# xAI Capability-Governance Case

Observed through: 2026-08-01

## Public position and contract

xAI's Acceptable Use Policy effective 2026-06-26 says it aims to maximize user
control while requiring lawful, responsible use and respect for guardrails. The
same policy prohibits jailbreak, adversarial prompting, prompt injection,
protective-measure bypass, model distillation, scraping, unauthorized service
access, hacking, fraud, and a broad set of harmful uses.

Consumer and enterprise terms reserve service modification, suspension, or
termination. Enterprise terms state that xAI may monitor accounts and take
corrective action including immediate termination; they also prohibit probing,
scanning, penetration, and benchmarking of the service.

## Agentic actions and responsibility

Consumer terms explicitly recognize autonomous actions such as browsing, code
execution, communications, file modification, Tool invocation, data processing,
and third-party interactions. They place responsibility and liability for such
Agentic Actions primarily on the user and disclaim broad xAI responsibility.

This is a notable allocation of Effect responsibility: xAI controls the hosted
model and service while contractually shifting downstream action consequences to
the user.

## Data and integration

Consumer content may be used for model improvement unless the user selects
available controls or Private Chat; enterprise/API content receives different
treatment. Integration with X or other third parties can add identity, history,
location, and platform dependency.

## Assessment

xAI differs rhetorically from more precautionary Providers but remains a strong
private-ordering system. Its public materials disclose less quantitative appeal
and enforcement information than Anthropic and less concrete abuse-retention
information than Google.
