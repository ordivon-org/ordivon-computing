# AI Capability Governance and Private Power

Status: G0-G2 foundational research completed; provider cases and experiments pending

## Purpose

This study investigates who can define, observe, allocate, condition, restrict,
revoke, adjudicate, and reshape access to advanced AI capabilities, and how those
powers are produced jointly by model providers, cloud and compute infrastructure,
contracts, classifiers, identity systems, markets, and states.

The governing question is not whether one company is good or evil. It is:

> When AI becomes a necessary cognition and action substrate, which institutional
> arrangements prevent any provider, state, cloud, or security organization from
> acquiring final power that is difficult to inspect, contest, or exit?

## G0-G2 result

G0-G2 establishes a reusable research substrate:

1. scope and claim boundaries that separate description, causation, normative
   judgment, and architecture implications;
2. research questions, hypotheses, falsifiers, evidence levels, and revision
   discipline;
3. a cross-disciplinary theory map spanning political power, governmentality,
   private ordering, digital constitutionalism, organizational risk, structural
   and infrastructure power, epistemic injustice, exit/voice, polycentric
   governance, and security engineering;
4. a typed research grammar and local JSON Schemas for future provider cases,
   policy revisions, enforcement events, and controlled observations.

The study does **not** yet rank providers, estimate false-positive rates, infer
intent from policy text, or justify a shared Ordivon governance service.

## Analytical separation

```text
Provider refusal
!= model incapability
!= Host denial
!= Tool denial
!= Runtime rejection
!= World-effect prevention
!= justified governance
```

The study treats safety interventions as observable mechanisms with possible
benefits, errors, distributional consequences, and institutional alternatives.
It neither assumes that every restriction is oppression nor that a restriction
is justified merely because it is described as safety.

## Current research disposition

Retain as an active study, but do not consume another portfolio active line.
G3 should build exact, revision-bound OpenAI and Anthropic cases from official
policies and technical evidence. G4-G6 must add materially different providers,
regulatory interactions, and controlled observations before any provider-power
index or Ordivon architecture change is admitted.

## Architecture non-decision

G0-G2 earns no new runtime service, policy engine, provider reputation score,
identity registry, global telemetry store, or centralized appeal system. Future
Ordivon feedback should remain narrow:

```text
Provider supplies cognition
Host owns durable Task and authority
Harness owns the bounded model-Tool loop
Runtime owns physical execution evidence
World/provider adapters preserve native observations
Verifier decides consequence
User retains Provider exit
```
