# SCD / Computational Possibility Engineering Consumption Boundary

Decision: **consume the two owners as source-fenced semantic constraints; do not turn them into central product services.**

`ordivon-computing` physically hosts Computer, SCD and Computational Possibility research, but repository colocation does not merge semantic ownership.

## SCD trigger

Use SCD when engineering claims semantic equivalence, refinement, preservation, abstraction, composition, property satisfaction or semantics-sensitive transport of a computational description.

Do not infer those claims from structural compatibility, schema validity, digest identity, version ordering or successful execution alone.

## Computational Possibility trigger

Use CP when engineering claims regime-relative possibility/impossibility, simulation/reduction, witness/certificate existence, applicability or complexity/resource bounds.

Do not infer theorem-level possibility from one implementation success, theorem-level impossibility from failure, or complexity theory from observed runtime cost alone.

## Product boundary

Exact product contracts and current execution facts stay with their product owners. SCD/CP constrain semantic claims made *about* those objects. They are not default runtime dependencies.

Reopen shared mechanism design only after repeated real consumers demonstrate the same unowned proof/compatibility burden.
