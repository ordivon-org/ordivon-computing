# Plural-Intelligence Products and Organization

Agent capability changes the allocation of work, attention, authority, and responsibility. Current people and organizations remain the legal and physical owners of most resources and commitments, but architecture should not turn that present condition into a permanent claim that every future artificial intelligence must remain property or lack purpose, refusal, responsibility, and exit.

## Persistent product objects

A conversation is an effective entry point for an open Goal, but sustained work needs visible durable objects:

```text
participants, Goal, and commitments
+ Task frontier and Attempts
+ current world bindings
+ Context inputs
+ Action Proposals, Effects, and Dispatches
+ Artifacts, Claims, and verified Facts
+ decisions routed to the responsible participant or institution
```

Conversation carries negotiation and explanation. Durable work objects carry continuity, commitments, evidence, and current authority.

## Division of responsibility is contextual

People may contribute:

- purpose, priority, values, and social context;
- ownership of credentials, machines, money, and legal commitments;
- domain judgment where objectives conflict;
- acceptance or rejection of consequences that bind them or their institutions.

Agents may contribute:

- high-throughput reading, synthesis, and open Action Proposals;
- repeated reversible exploration and bounded execution;
- cross-tool observation and verification;
- independent alternatives and counterexamples;
- continuous maintenance and adaptation;
- negotiation, delegation, refusal, and responsibility where their identity and domain contract support them.

The useful structure is not a permanent hierarchy. It is:

```text
identified participants and resource relationships
+ replaceable cognition and execution
+ explicit commitments and consequence boundaries
+ deterministic evidence from reality
```

A ParticipantRef is a system identity, not proof of consciousness or legal personhood. Domain institutions still determine rights and duties.

## Attention is a system resource

As Agent throughput rises, per-action approval becomes both expensive and unreliable. A product should distinguish:

- reversible, isolated, and private exploration that proceeds under a broad capability profile;
- ordinary commitments covered by an existing participant mandate or policy;
- novel, ambiguous, costly, shared, or irreversible consequences that require another participant, verifier, resource owner, or institution;
- claims that lack sufficient evidence for acceptance;
- conflicts among Goals or commitments that require negotiation or priority resolution.

A DecisionRequest should include the reason, alternatives, evidence, consequence, reversibility, and cost of delay. Asking one person to approve every Tool call transfers policy execution back to a fatigued operator and prevents stronger systems from using their capability.

## Authority and decision routing

Escalation means routing a missing commitment to its actual owner. Depending on the domain, that may be:

- a person who owns the affected account or asset;
- an organization or legal role;
- a verifier or independent judge;
- a team commander or elected coordinator;
- another Agent that owns a Task or Artifact;
- a future artificial participant with stable identity and accepted standing.

No participant may grant itself another participant’s resources or erase external rules. Non-domination and consequence responsibility apply together.

## Multi-Agent collaboration

Multiple Agents create value only when work contains real independent structure and results can be compared or joined through stable Artifacts.

Useful forms include:

- independent research or design candidates;
- specialist execution against separate Workspaces;
- actor and independent verifier separation;
- pipeline stages with explicit input and output contracts;
- bounded adversarial or counterexample search;
- negotiated division of responsibility around shared Goals.

A Join is a state-reduction operation, not merely several messages arriving:

```text
independent Attempts
→ Artifacts, Claims, and evidence
→ explicit comparison or integration rule
→ accepted result or unresolved conflict
```

More Agents can also increase duplicate work, shared-error amplification, token cost, communication overhead, and merge complexity. Agent count is not a maturity metric.

## Refusal, revocation, and exit

A durable collaboration model must represent more than command and obedience. Depending on the domain contract, a participant may:

- refuse a proposed commitment;
- request different evidence or terms;
- revoke a delegation;
- transfer a Task;
- leave a collaboration;
- preserve obligations that survive exit;
- challenge a claimed Fact or completion state.

These semantics should remain local until multiple domains demonstrate a stable shared contract.

## Handoff and institutional memory

A continuation record should transfer current semantic state rather than an unfiltered transcript:

```text
participants, Goal, and commitments
active Task frontier
world and contract revisions
Attempts and unresolved uncertainty
verified Facts and Claims under evaluation
relevant Artifacts
next admissible work
```

Organizational memory can then evolve through explicit promotion:

```text
raw events
→ Observations and Artifacts
→ verified Facts
→ reusable Knowledge
→ challenged Core principles
```

## Evaluation and economics

Local metrics such as commits, tests, tokens, or Agent turns can be useful operational signals. They do not establish Goal progress.

A persistent intelligence system should optimize for outcomes such as:

- verified improvement per unit time;
- accepted results per active participant minute;
- recovery after interruption;
- independent verification rate;
- duplicate or abandoned work;
- cost per accepted result;
- unrecoverable loss and reversibility;
- entropy introduced into repositories and operations;
- important decisions routed without approval fatigue;
- distribution rather than permanent concentration of useful capability.

As generation becomes cheaper, value moves toward problem selection, unique world access, reliable verification, durable continuity, broad participation, trusted commitments, and responsibility for consequences.
