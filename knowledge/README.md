# Knowledge Base

The knowledge base stores reusable explanations, conceptual models, comparisons, and cases that help reconstruct and apply the core.

## Relationship to the rest of the repository

```text
Studies  → preserve learning and derivation
Knowledge → preserve reusable understanding
Core      → preserve the smallest generative theory
Research  → preserve open questions and experiments
Projects  → preserve real construction in their own repositories
```

A topic belongs here when it is already coherent enough to reuse, but still benefits from explanation, examples, historical context, or implementation detail.

## Promotion path

```text
raw observation or learning
→ Study or Research note
→ reusable Knowledge page
→ repeated cross-project validation
→ compact Core principle or primitive
```

Promotion is based on explanatory and generative power, not perceived importance alone. Detailed knowledge remains valuable even when it never becomes part of the core.

## Writing shape

A strong knowledge page usually contains:

1. a precise concept;
2. a first-principles explanation;
3. an example and a contrasting case;
4. its relationship to other layers;
5. practical implications for Agent-native systems;
6. links to deeper studies, research questions, or real projects.
