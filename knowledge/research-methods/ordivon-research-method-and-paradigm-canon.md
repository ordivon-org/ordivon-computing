# Ordivon Research Method and Paradigm Canon

Status: reusable Knowledge synthesis. This page compresses methods repeatedly supported across Ordivon owner research and Research-System dogfood. It is **not** domain truth, a Research-System authority surface, or a universal ontology.

## Responsibility boundary

Computer owns the reusable comparison and world-model-revision method:

```text
owner-native question / practice / evidence
        ↓
owner-local research result
        ↓
cross-owner comparison and falsification
        ↓
retain / narrow / split / repair / reject a shared claim
        ↓
project-specific reform question
        ↓
independent owner re-test
```

Project truth remains with the project. ResearchRef/currentness/closeout/publication/Atlas/Observatory machinery remains with the Research System. A cross-owner synthesis does not mint semantic ownership.

## 1. Agent-first is a stress regime, not an ontology

Agent-era systems are unusually strong falsifier generators because cloning, handoff, long-lived Tasks, short-lived Runs, model routing, tool calls, external Effects, dynamic Context, shared memory, and multi-Agent composition make hidden assumptions observable.

Use Agent-first to pressure-test claims such as:

- Agent identity = process identity;
- Task = Run = Attempt;
- Tool intent = physical effect = semantic success;
- one coordination phenomenon requires one central coordinator;
- caller-visible Context exhausts relevant durable history;
- current representation implies current owner truth.

Do **not** rewrite World, Finance, Network, Human, Media, or other domains into Agent-centric ontologies merely because Agent-era cases exposed a flaw.

## 2. Responsibility placement precedes architecture

Before selecting a service, schema, repository, protocol, or subsystem, ask which owner can preserve the invariant and which facts are merely imported.

Distinguish:

```text
owner-native truth
cross-owner bridge
reusable Computer synthesis
Research-System institutional state
physical implementation / consumer
```

Broad Computing's decomposition into Semantics of Computational Descriptions and Computational Possibility is the canonical positive case: a broad repository/topic boundary was not a valid semantic-owner boundary.

## 3. Foundations-first without numbering inertia

A Foundation is admitted only after referent reconstruction and destructive pressure, not because the next integer is available.

Preferred sequence:

1. reconstruct the referent and current use;
2. separate overloaded terms;
3. identify hidden assumptions and rival accounts;
4. search counterexamples and cross-regime pressure;
5. test deletion-essentiality and owner absorbability;
6. admit only the irreducible responsibility that survives.

A mature result may remain a derived theory, operational protocol, architecture, or bridge. Computational Possibility and Ordivon Normative both demonstrate that `NumberedFoundationCount = 0` can be a positive mature architecture.

## 3A. Start from an informed baseline; subtraction does not delete usable knowledge

A knowledge need does not imply a rediscovery need. Before opening or closing a research route, recover the strongest applicable current internal **and external** standing that is already available, and treat admitted mature results as positive solution-domain content rather than merely as obstacles to an Ordivon novelty claim.

```text
NeedKnowledge != NeedToRediscoverKnowledge
Originality != TruthUseAdmission
PriorArtAbsorbed != ReferentResolved
SubtractFromNovelty != SubtractFromSolutionDomain
SolvedSubspace != DeletedProblemDomain
```

For an imported result, distinguish five questions instead of re-proving it by default:

1. is the source/result sufficiently established for the claim being used?
2. do its scope and assumptions transport to the current target?
3. does composition with other imported or owner-local standings require an additional bridge?
4. is any local implementation faithful to those assumptions?
5. does Ordivon need new architecture/materialization, or can existing owners/consumers use the result directly?

These gates are independent. External prior art may eliminate an originality claim while leaving the referent question open; conversely, mature theory may resolve the current referent and justify `NO_CHANGE` or `STOP`. Do not continue research merely to make an imported solution Ordivon-origin.

At closeout, preserve any load-bearing imported solution components, scope/applicability conditions, competing accounts and composition assumptions needed by future consumers. A `REDUCED`, `ABSORBED`, `NO_NEW_*`, prior-art, or owner-sufficient verdict must not erase the positive knowledge that made that verdict possible.

This is a research-practice guard, not a global external-knowledge registry, consumer graph, or automatic authority transfer. Source/owner authority and currentness remain claim-native.

## 4. Fresh whole-space search beats residual inertia

A residual list is historical evidence, not a roadmap.

```text
Map != Roadmap
Residual != NextRoute
Unexplored != RecommendedNext
```

After a local closeout, rebuild the candidate space from the whole referent, subtract established coverage, compare marginal information gain and falsification value, and include a no-new-route control. This prevents HF24, FDF54, NDF6, HaF62, HDF44, new SCDF, or similar numbering from appearing by inertia.

## 5. Destructive falsification must be prebound

Before result visibility, freeze the proposition/rival, scope, discriminating method, falsifier, evaluator/oracle, stop condition, and material evidence bindings when those determine interpretation.

A mechanically successful execution can legitimately produce:

```text
PROCESS_EXIT_ZERO
ResearchResult = FALSIFIED_IN_SCOPE
```

Execution failure is not research falsification unless the contract declared it discriminative in advance.

## 6. ResearchInvestigation is not Runtime Run

Research is not inherently execution-shaped. Conceptual/source reading, formal reasoning, observational work, human investigation, destructive fixtures, and cross-owner recovery may use zero Runtime Runs.

Execution-backed research still requires:

```text
Run/Attempt
→ Observation
→ Evidence
→ Evaluation
→ Interpretation
→ Research Result
```

Runtime owns physical execution truth; it does not decide the semantic research verdict.

## 7. Preserve epistemic axes instead of collapsing them

The following distinctions are repeatedly deletion-essential:

```text
Evidence != Conclusion
EpistemicVerdict != EvidenceScope
Standing != Currentness
HistoricalValidity != Currentness
Artifact/Representation != Truth
SchemaValidity != SemanticConsistency
SemanticConsistency != OwnerTruthAdmission
OwnerTruthAdmission != DownstreamCommitment
```

A strong verdict over a narrow scope is not a universal result. A frozen historical claim can remain valid evidence while no longer current.

## 8. Typed negative memory

One `NEGATIVE` bucket destroys future control value. Preserve at least the semantic difference among:

- `FALSIFIED` / `FALSIFIED_IN_SCOPE`;
- `BASELINE_EQUIVALENT_WITHIN_BOUND`;
- `NO_DETECTABLE_EFFECT_UNDER_DECLARED_SENSITIVITY`;
- `UNDERDETERMINED` / insufficient discrimination;
- `NOT_ADMITTED`;
- `REDUCED` / absorbed by an existing owner;
- `REPAIR_REQUIRED`;
- execution-invalid / execution-failed;
- owner/container failure with surviving content.

This prevents resurrection of bad routes while preserving useful survivors.

## 9. Minimal repair and non-monotonic history

When a counterexample defeats an over-broad scope:

1. preserve the old claim as historical evidence;
2. retain any surviving specialization;
3. repair only the failing carrier or predicate;
4. propagate only dependency-essential repairs;
5. refreeze under explicit reopen rules.

Network's `Communicability → InterLocusCapability` repair and induced NDF1 repair are the canonical case. Admission → reopen → reclassification is valid research history; do not rewrite history as if the final result had always been known.

## 10. Closure is scoped

```text
StageClosed != WholeDomainExhausted
LocalArchitecturalSaturation != LogicalExhaustion
TaskCompleted != ResearchClosed
```

Close what the evidence supports. Reopen on a concrete falsifier, materially new regime, owner-boundary change, or deletion-essential residual—not because a programme can continue numerically.

## 11. Removal-first existence testing

A shared mechanism, service, protocol, schema, active responsibility, or model-context burden must defeat removal and mature classical baselines.

Prefer Git, SQLite, DuckDB/Parquet, `jq`, owner-native receipts, ordinary files, and OpenTelemetry-style equipment for deterministic storage, counting, filtering, diffing, replay bookkeeping, and aggregation. Add an Ordivon mechanism only when repeated real consumers prove that composition of mature substrate cannot preserve the needed semantics.

```text
shared distinction != shared service
cross-project convergence != centralized infrastructure
```

## 12. Structure the control boundary, not the thought process

Structure data where silent mutation/deletion changes research meaning: identity, owner/authority, scope, proposition/rival disposition, evidence binding, falsifier/evaluator, result standing, currentness, residual/reopen, and recovery.

Keep interpretation, brainstorming, provisional derivation, and explanatory reasoning free-form unless a specific control failure proves otherwise.

## 13. Reproducibility is graded

Use the least capability that preserves the claim:

- R0 reasoning recoverable;
- R1 evidence reconstructable;
- R2 investigation repeatable;
- R3 execution replayable;
- R4 environment reproducible.

Universal R4/environment capture is ceremony and still cannot guarantee identical live-world outcomes.

## 14. Evaluator authority must be explicit

```text
candidate != evaluator
evaluator independence != domain truth authority
```

A benchmark or model grader cannot invent a project-local truth rule. Cross-owner evaluation must bind its oracle to owner-native semantics or an explicit counterfactual law frozen as part of the investigation.

## 15. Currentness requires source fences

An exact historical observation may remain valid after its owner advances. Therefore a current cross-project claim must compare the retained observation fence with the selected current owner source and classify advancement/divergence/unknown rather than infer currentness from recency.

This is the epistemic principle. The concrete `CURRENT` publication, AuthorityVersionRef, Atlas ProjectionHealth, and closeout machinery are owned by the Research System and owner corpora, not by Computer.

## 16. Recovery preserves admitted consequence identity

Once an exact Effect/request/consequence identity is durably admitted, recovery should preserve and reconcile that identity rather than create a fresh consequence merely because execution was interrupted or the observer restarted.

The concrete mechanisms belong to Runtime/Host/Harness. Computer retains only the reusable responsibility distinction.

## 17. Fresh synthesis context is a bounded research operator

When an evidence-acquisition Agent exhibits search inertia after decision-relevant dependency coverage, a fresh synthesis context can be useful. But freeze an unambiguous semantic question and oracle before attributing failure to context order or representation.

Canonicalized bytes or reordered evidence are mechanical conveniences, not evidence of improved semantic tractability by themselves.

## 18. Cross-project convergence is evidence, not automatic standardization

When the same distinction independently survives in multiple owners, Computer may compress it into Knowledge or challenge Core. Implementation should remain owner-local unless genuine multi-consumer pressure earns a shared mechanism.

A shared reform must return to independent projects for re-test. One project accepting an implementation choice does not make that choice universal.

## 19. Promotion ladder and bounded closeout destination test

```text
Research
→ reusable Knowledge
→ repeated heterogeneous owner validation
→ deletion/classical-baseline survival
→ compact Core responsibility
```

Importance alone is insufficient. Many valuable architectures should remain project-local or Knowledge-level indefinitely.

Closing a Task or rejecting a new theory does not itself decide where the surviving result should live. Before a materially changed research line is closed, perform one bounded **destination adjudication** over the surviving content; the answer may be split across destinations and may legitimately be `no new durable materialization`.

```text
TaskCloseout != DestinationAdjudicated
NoNewTheory != NoReusableKnowledge
ReusableKnowledge != CoreAuthority
PromotionDecision != MaterializationOrDelivery
```

Use the smallest truthful destination:

- **owner-local truth / current domain semantics** → owning project or owner-native research/knowledge surface;
- **still-open conditional claim / unresolved falsifier** → Research;
- **stable reusable explanation, method, cross-owner comparison, or imported-solution composition** → Knowledge, preserving source/applicability boundaries;
- **compact stable cross-workload responsibility** → Core only after stronger deletion, independent-consumer, and classical-baseline gates;
- **implementation-only invariant** → implementation/project docs or tests that own it;
- **failed, superseded, or negative route with future control value** → typed history/negative memory;
- **nothing with material downstream reuse value** → no additional materialization.

Do not require every closeout to create a Knowledge page, and do not use recurrence/test count alone as promotion authority. The purpose is to prevent both forms of Promotion Debt: under-promotion that causes rediscovery and over-promotion that turns local success into doctrine. Source-neutral imported knowledge can earn a Knowledge destination when its reusable composition/application is the result; it does not need Ordivon-origin novelty.

### 19A. Sedimentation is stronger than preservation

For audit purposes, distinguish these stages without turning them into a new global lifecycle schema:

```text
S0 Result exists
→ S1 durable Standing
→ S2 canonical Representation
→ S3 discoverable / first-look reachable
→ S4 bound to a real future consumer
→ S5 changes a default, routine, interface, admission rule or responsibility placement
→ S6 future consequence is independently observed or a justified NO_CHANGE is demonstrated
```

The non-equivalences are load-bearing:

```text
Preserved != Discoverable
Discoverable != Consumed
Consumed != Environmentalized
Environmentalized != Beneficial
HistoricallyIntegrated != CurrentlyInhabited
```

Not every result should progress to S5/S6. Before claiming that a reusable standing has become part of Ordivon's persistent environment, ask:

1. which current owner/source represents it canonically?
2. which ordinary first-look or recovery path makes it reachable when relevant?
3. which concrete future consumer is supposed to use it?
4. should it change default Context, Tool exposure, routine, admission, responsibility placement, test, or nothing at all?
5. what challenge/re-grounding path prevents the sedimented default from becoming shadow authority?
6. what future observation would show benefit, harm, stale use, or justified `NO_CHANGE`?

A document, Host checkpoint, Atlas pointer, or successful promotion decision proves only its own stage. Do not infer later stages from earlier ones.

## 20. Canonical case library

The current strongest method cases include:

- **Network** — Communicability over-scope falsified by Bell/joint relational-resource capability; minimal NDF0/NDF1 repair.
- **Computing** — broad owner decomposition into SCD + Computational Possibility.
- **Computational Possibility** — zero numbered Foundations with mature Derived-Theory Basis; historical AlgF0 withdrawn after definitional eliminability.
- **Host** — Primitive/Universal/Central Host ontology falsified; durable continuity retained while universal coordination ownership was rejected.
- **Post-Host** — PHR3/PHR4 show owner failure does not imply reality/content failure.
- **Research-System fresh dogfood** — conceptual research with zero Runtime Runs; bounded equivalence and destructive falsification both under successful execution; Host→Runtime pointer-only recovery without authority copying.
- **Computer contraction / RSI Lab** — removal-first survival, mature deterministic substrate, and thin scientific instruments outperform permanent architecture seats where semantics do not require them.
