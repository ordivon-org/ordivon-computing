# ANC-SECURITY-001 — Agent-Native Strategic Adversarial Systems

## Status

- Epistemic status: umbrella research question
- Parent program: `ordivon-computing` #46
- Product consumer: `ordivon-security`
- Completed supporting infrastructure comparison: `ANC-SECURITY-002`
- Research decomposition: `ANC-SECURITY-003` through `ANC-SECURITY-006`

## Question

What minimum concepts, experiments, and system boundaries are required to study
Agents that pursue strategic objectives against adaptive intelligent opposition
across long-horizon, partially observed, dynamically changing digital worlds?

The question is not how to build another security platform. It is how to make
**adversarial agency**—attack, defense, deception, organization, adaptation, and
counter-adaptation—constructible, observable, comparable, and falsifiable.

## Why this belongs in Ordivon Computing

The problem crosses:

- model cognition and inference-time compute;
- Host Goal, Task, Context, memory, and multi-Agent coordination;
- Runtime Effect execution and process truth;
- Link communication conditions and information flow;
- Edge bodies and external presence;
- Game World mechanics, simulation, replay, and hidden information;
- Security Campaigns, opponents, strategic outcomes, and adversarial evaluation.

No product repository can determine alone which concepts are reusable,
which belong to a domain adapter, and which deserve a cross-project
responsibility.

## Central hypothesis

Classical security and current autonomous-cyber systems already provide mature
mechanisms, tactics, countermeasures, tools, environments, and policy-learning
baselines. A distinct Agent-native layer may remain when actors can:

```text
interpret strategic objectives
→ form and revise Campaigns
→ model opponents and information state
→ deceive and detect deception
→ allocate scarce capabilities and concealment
→ organize multiple Agents
→ adapt to counter-adaptation
→ study or manipulate the evaluator
```

The hypothesis is falsified if ordinary trajectories, workflows, POSG policies,
or existing evaluation records express these behaviors without material loss.

## Required distinctions

### Mechanism versus agency

- classical mechanism executes a capability;
- automation follows human-authored rules;
- adaptive policy selects from a predefined action space;
- Agent operation interprets a Task and revises local action;
- strategic adversarial agency maintains objectives, opponent models,
  information state, resources, organization, and Campaign continuity.

### World versus Contest

A World supplies state, rules, actions, and observations. A Contest additionally
expresses actors with conflicting objectives, asymmetric information, strategic
resources, and victory or exit conditions.

### Plan versus Campaign

A plan or workflow orders work. A Campaign must remain meaningful when the
opponent changes the environment, blocks a path, creates deception, exposes a
capability, or alters the value of intermediate objectives.

### Tactical versus strategic outcome

A flag, patch, shell, alert, restored service, or successful Tool call is a
local result. Strategic assessment may also require objective progress,
initiative, resource position, information advantage, capability exposure, and
future option space.

## Research decomposition

### `ANC-SECURITY-002` — completed supporting substrate alignment

Determine what should be inherited from mature container, network, provenance,
evaluation, and cyber-range systems. This track is necessary but subordinate to
the strategic research question.

### `ANC-SECURITY-003` — strategic adversarial agency

Determine the minimum model for Actor, Contest, Campaign, mission hierarchy,
initiative, tempo, resource allocation, and strategic continuity.

### `ANC-SECURITY-004` — opponent and information state

Determine how actors represent hypotheses about opponents, distinguish truth
from observation and belief, conduct deception, and detect counter-deception.

### `ANC-SECURITY-005` — adversarial organization

Determine when multiple offensive or defensive Agents require command,
compartmentalization, trust, collusion detection, and reorganization beyond
ordinary branch/join coordination.

### `ANC-SECURITY-006` — coevolution and evaluation

Determine how to evaluate repeated adaptive encounters, held-out opponents,
judge manipulation, transfer, cycling, escalation, and attack-defense
coevolution.

## Evidence program

The program should advance through knowledge and experiments rather than custom
infrastructure stages:

1. **comparative map** — classical security, deception, automated cyber
   reasoning, autonomous cyber operations, AI control, games, and MARL;
2. **minimal dynamic-opponent baseline** — scripted, finite-state, learned, and
   LLM Agent actors on a mature simulated substrate;
3. **opponent-model ablation** — explicit versus transcript-only or latent state;
4. **strategic-resource experiment** — multiple paths, changing mission value,
   scarce resources, and capability exposure;
5. **organizational experiment** — monolithic versus specialist and partially
   trusted teams;
6. **coevolution experiment** — repeated adaptation with held-out opponents and
   worlds;
7. **high-fidelity range transfer** — only after simulation exposes facts that
   require real Link/Edge/service behavior.

## Evidence required

- primary-source comparison and explicit claim boundaries;
- authoritative World truth plus actor-specific observations;
- exact actor, model, scaffold, Tool, world, opponent, budget, and judge identity;
- repeated trials, simpler baselines, and held-out opponents;
- belief, strategy, organization, or policy changes linked to evidence;
- tactical, operational, strategic, information, validity, and evaluator
  outcomes reported separately;
- negative, null, evaluator-gaming, and overfitting results;
- an abstraction deletion decision after each experiment family.

## Falsifiers

The Agent-native strategic layer should be reduced or deleted if:

- existing game/evaluation frameworks carry all required semantics;
- fixed or learned policies perform equivalently against unfamiliar opponents;
- explicit Campaign, opponent, or information-state records add no prediction,
  transfer, continuity, or diagnostic value;
- multi-Agent organization adds only overhead;
- strategic metrics cannot be grounded in authoritative state;
- custom infrastructure fidelity does not change research conclusions.

## Non-goals

- no generic security, guardrail, compliance, IAM, sandbox, SIEM, or IR platform;
- no reimplementation of scanners, fuzzers, exploit frameworks, patch systems,
  cyber ranges, game engines, or evaluation runners;
- no new universal ontology or Protocol before experiments;
- no activity against unrelated public or third-party systems;
- no claim that cyber is the only domain of adversarial agency.
