# Historical Reachability and Evidence Ecology — PPD Revision

> **World-model / research-method correction, not a History owner, memory service, universal innovation theory, or claim that synthetic/historical evidence can mint current Reality.** The central correction is methodological: naturally occurring Ordivon episodes are one valuable evidence regime, not the exclusive gate for serious research. PPD already permits active construction of discriminating pressure through external sources, historical trajectories, controlled intervention, measurement, representation changes, countermodels, simulation, formal possibility/impossibility analysis, synthetic worlds and first-principles construction.

## 0. Why this correction is necessary

A recent Historical Reachability closeout made an overly restrictive move:

```text
interesting historical-reachability synthesis
→ wait for a future natural owner-native consumer
→ only then test whether the lens matters
```

That is not consistent with Ordivon's current research method or its actual experiment history.

Current Computing already states in `PRESSURE-SELECTION-VALUE-OF-INFORMATION-AUDIT-20260826.md` that PPD has a broad pressure-construction repertoire:

```text
external sources
experiments / interventions
measurements
queries
representation changes
hypothesis / candidate generation
countermodels
first-principles possibility constructions
```

More decisively, HD0-HD9 already executed a 1760-2026 historical OOD programme precisely to expose Ordivon world-model priors to materially different worlds. The corpus contains 320 innovation/discovery trajectories, 96 Deep Anchors, source-first/theory-later coding, failed/abandoned routes, dormant discoveries, standards/infrastructure, externalities, independent coding, representation-depth ablation and historical replay controls.

Therefore:

```text
NaturalPressure
!=
OnlyAdmissiblePressure
```

and:

```text
DesignedPressure
!=
ManufacturedTruth
```

A designed probe earns only the claim class that its intervention, control, source and validation boundary support.

## 1. Evidence ecology, not an evidence hierarchy with “natural” at the top

Different evidence regimes answer different questions.

### E1 — owner-native / field Reality

Best for:

- current state/currentness;
- actual operational consequence;
- current authority/admission;
- ecological interaction with current infrastructure;
- realized recurrence under present conditions.

Claim ceiling:

```text
FieldConsequence
!=
UniversalMechanism
```

A natural episode can be richly confounded and historically contingent.

### E2 — designed intervention / DOE / matched-world experiment

Best for:

- causal discrimination;
- attribution among competing mechanisms;
- deletion/substitution effects;
- representation/environment treatment effects;
- response distributions under controlled factor changes.

Claim ceiling:

```text
ControlledTreatmentEffect
!=
UnboundedEcologicalGeneralization
```

The experiment is not invalid because the pressure was deliberately created; deliberate factor variation is the point of experimental design.

### E3 — synthetic world / simulator / executable counterfactual

Best for:

- mechanism existence;
- edge-case generation;
- policy/action comparison under identical initial state;
- intervention/deletion tests that are impossible, unsafe or expensive in current Reality;
- search-geometry and coordination mechanism studies.

Claim ceiling:

```text
SyntheticMechanismEvidence
!=
CurrentWorldFact
```

Transfer requires target-relative validation or an explicit model-to-Reality bridge.

### E4 — exploratory / heuristic model and simulation

Best for:

- possibility-space exploration;
- how-possibly constructions;
- identifying candidate mechanisms and parameter regions;
- generating new hypotheses and discriminators;
- revealing assumptions and sensitivity.

Claim ceiling:

```text
ModelPossibility
!=
PhysicalValidity
```

A model can improve search before it earns descriptive truth about Reality.

### E5 — formal / first-principles analysis

Best for:

- logical contradiction;
- impossibility or lower-bound candidates under explicit assumptions;
- decomposition of quantifiers, resources, interfaces and witness classes;
- proof that additional search in a fixed language/regime cannot solve a stated gap;
- construction of counterexamples and minimal sufficient conditions.

Claim ceiling:

```text
FormalResult(R)
!=
ActualWorldSatisfies(R-premises)
```

### E6 — source-locked historical trajectory

Best for:

- long-horizon path dependence;
- prerequisite/complement discovery;
- delayed/dormant capability;
- instrumentation and standards changing later search/coordination;
- scale-induced boundary changes;
- diffusion/realization stages;
- option value after local failure;
- lock-in, loss and correction.

Claim ceiling:

```text
HistoricalTrajectoryEvidence
!=
CurrentCapability
!=
RepeatableCausalLaw
```

Historical evidence is especially vulnerable to survivorship, hindsight and representation bias, which is why source locking, failed-route sampling, anti-cases and representation-depth audits matter.

### E7 — external mature science / engineering / institutions

Best for:

- importing already-solved mechanism space;
- identifying mature carriers and methods;
- testing whether an Ordivon problem is genuinely residual;
- widening the set of candidate solution families beyond current project history.

Claim ceiling:

```text
ExternalMatureResult
!=
AutomaticApplicabilityHere
```

External knowledge is positive solution-domain content to be transported and tested, not merely novelty-deletion pressure.

### E8 — archaeological / process lineage evidence

Best for:

- why an old route was admitted or rejected;
- what falsifier changed standing;
- reopen conditions;
- preventing duplicate dead routes;
- recovering how an environment/capability was previously formed.

Claim ceiling:

```text
HistoricalValidity
!=
Currentness
```

The correct principle is therefore:

```text
EvidenceEcology
>
SingleEvidenceRegime
```

PPD selects the evidence regime according to the uncertainty that must be reduced, not according to whether an event happened spontaneously inside Ordivon.

## 2. Historical Reachability is already strongly prefigured by HD0-HD9

HD0 chose **trajectory**, not famous invention or hero inventor, as the causal unit:

```text
pressure / anomaly / opportunity
→ available world model and competing routes
→ observation / instrument / hypothesis / prototype
→ falsification or decisive validation
→ enabling complements and constraints
→ diffusion / standardization / scaling
→ externalities / failures / revisions
→ second-order capability and research effects
```

That is already extremely close to a capability-basis transition representation.

HD0 also explicitly asks for every Deep Anchor:

- what could actors reasonably know at the time?
- which routes were serious competitors?
- what observation/instrument/prototype changed the frontier?
- which complements made the nominal invention usable?
- what failed or was displaced, and why?
- did the development lower future discovery cost or create entirely new search spaces?

Therefore the present problem does not require a new historical ontology. It requires a new **projection of an existing high-quality historical corpus onto reachability transitions**.

## 3. The strongest HD9 result already describes reachability-changing transition families

HD9 closed the 320-trajectory campaign by rejecting a universal-law interpretation and retaining seven typed structural families.

For Historical Reachability they can be re-read as follows, without changing their original standing.

### HR-H1 — Complementary capability formation

HD9 H1:

> Realized capability is often co-produced by components, infrastructure, skills, process and institutions; resolving one bottleneck can move the governing bottleneck.

Reachability interpretation:

```text
core idea / local method exists
!=
realizable target path exists
```

A target may become reachable only after acquiring a bundle of complements. The next blocker can migrate after each acquisition.

This directly opposes single-step thinking:

```text
current blocker removed
=> target reached
```

### HR-H2 — Staged realization and validation

Discovery, validation, production, deployment, diffusion, durability and evaluator standing are separable.

Reachability interpretation:

```text
EpistemicallyReachable
!=
TechnicallyRealizable
!=
OperationallyDeployable
!=
DurablyAvailable
```

A historical path can reveal which stage is the actual current blocker.

### HR-H3 — Observability expands the reachable question space

This is the strongest direct Historical Reachability witness in HD9:

> Instruments, measurements and evidence infrastructure can change which hypotheses are discriminable and future search cost/adjacency.

Thus:

```text
MoreObservationCapability
can change
QuestionReachability
```

not only answer quality.

This is a historical/civilizational analogue of the Open Interface and Representation research finding that an intermediate artifact can change future search geometry.

### HR-H4 — Representation and standards as coordination substrates

A representation/standard does not perform the physical end task, yet can make distributed production, comparison or interoperability possible.

Reachability interpretation:

```text
RepresentationChange
can create new compositional edges
without itself being the terminal capability
```

Standards can therefore be capability-basis transitions.

### HR-H5 — Scale-induced boundary dynamics

Scaling can move bottlenecks and bring new causal/value dimensions into the relevant problem.

Reachability interpretation:

```text
PathQualified(target, scale_1)
!=
PathQualified(target, scale_2)
```

A route that worked locally may become blocked by manufacturing, safety, logistics, authority, externality or coordination at scale.

### HR-H6 — Distributed causation and historical attribution

Innovation trajectories are frequently multi-actor and multi-stage.

Reachability interpretation:

```text
CapabilityHistory
!=
HeroEventHistory
```

The relevant object is the transition ecology: who/what supplied which missing relation, not who gets a single invention label.

### HR-H7 — Objective-relative frontier choice

Local value, generality, option value, metaproductivity and time-to-effect are separable.

Reachability interpretation:

A transition may be valuable because it changes future reachable work even when immediate objective gain is modest.

This is closely aligned with HD0 S7 Metaproductivity.

## 7. A formal lens: capability basis and basis-changing transitions

Let `B_t(g)` denote the target-relative capability basis available to a finite intelligence for target `g` at time `t`.

This is analysis notation, not a persistent schema. Depending on the target it may include:

```text
knowledge / methods
representations / standards
observable variables / instruments
skills / experts
software / compute
materials / machines / facilities
suppliers / logistics / institutions
interfaces / coordination relations
authority / safety / legal admission
resources / time
reconstruction / recovery routes
```

Let:

```text
Reach_g(B_t)
```

mean target-relative routes currently achievable under the declared boundary, and:

```text
Search_g(B_t)
```

mean routes/questions a finite consumer can realistically discover or discriminate under that basis and budget.

A basis-changing transition is:

```text
tau : B_t -> B_{t+1}
```

A historical transition is reachability-bearing when source-fenced evidence supports at least one material change such as:

```text
new target route opened
old route closed
blocker reclassified
new prerequisite/complement exposed
new observation/question became discriminable
coordination/composition edge became feasible
reacquisition/reconstruction cost changed
safe/authorized action set changed
bottleneck moved
option value survived target change
```

The full reachable set need not be enumerable.

The important recursive relation is:

```text
g notin Reach_g(B_t)
```

does not imply:

```text
there is no admissible sequence
tau_1 ... tau_n
such that
g in Reach_g(B_{t+n})
```

This is **candidate meta-reachability**, not a theorem that every target will eventually become reachable.

## 8. Transition operators suggested by combined HD9 + current Ordivon evidence

Use these as PPD candidate generators, not universal ontology labels.

### T1 — POTENTIATE / acquire prerequisite

A prior capability changes whether a later transition can occur.

Examples: precision machine tools enabling later machinery; measurement/instrumentation enabling later theory; missing materials/control delaying otherwise good representations.

### T2 — COMPLEMENT / compose a usable system

Combine mechanism + materials + process + skills + infrastructure + institution until the target is actually realizable.

### T3 — INSTRUMENT / expand observation basis

Create or acquire a measurement surface that makes previously inseparable worlds discriminable.

### T4 — REPRESENT / STANDARDIZE

Create a notation, interface, measurement standard, protocol or shared representation that lowers coordination/comparison cost and makes heterogeneous carriers composable.

### T5 — RECONFIGURE / RECOMBINE

Reuse a capability-production mechanism or artifact under a different target. Historical option value may appear here.

### T6 — EXTERNALIZE / ACCESS CIVILIZATION CARRIER

Use expert, vendor, laboratory, institution, market, remote instrument or contract manufacturing rather than reproducing the capability locally.

### T7 — VALIDATE / CROSS REALIZATION STAGE

Move from plausible idea to repeatable experiment, production, deployment, diffusion or durable service.

### T8 — CONSTRAIN / PRUNE

Remove unsafe, invalid or option-destroying actions; close falsified routes; preserve valid future completion paths.

### T9 — SCALE / MOVE BOUNDARY

Scale changes the relevant bottleneck, authority, externality, logistics or objective.

### T10 — LOSE / DECAY / LOCK-IN

Capabilities can disappear, become too expensive, be institutionally blocked, or trap search in a local trajectory. Historical reachability is not monotonic progress.

## 9. The revised PPD procedure: do not wait for Reality to volunteer the experiment

For a target that appears unreachable, unfamiliar or outside current practice:

```text
A. TARGET
State the exact consequence/evidence need and current boundary.

B. CURRENT CARRIER SEARCH
Who/what can realize an equivalent relation now?
local / buy / rent / remote / expert / vendor / institution /
contract manufacture / measurement / software / configure / compose.

C. HISTORICAL TRANSITION SEARCH
How did analogous capabilities become possible historically?
Which prerequisite, instrument, representation, standard, institution,
material, manufacturing process or complement changed the reachable set?
Which routes failed, stalled, went dormant, or became useful later?

D. BASIS RECONSTRUCTION
Represent the smallest target-relative B_t needed to explain the blocker.
Do not build a global capability graph.

E. CANDIDATE TRANSITION GENERATION
Generate several tau candidates, including negative operators:
add capability / change representation / instrument / standardize /
outsource / reconfigure / constrain / abandon / wait / decompose target.

F. DISCRIMINATING PRESSURE
Choose the cheapest admissible evidence regime that separates candidates:
formal counterexample, literature/history, source audit, simulation,
synthetic world, matched intervention, prototype, measurement,
expert/vendor test, or owner-native field experiment.

G. CLAIM-CEILING CHECK
Do not lift synthetic/historical/formal evidence into current capability.
Resolve current access, authority, safety, resource and Reality premises
before consequence-bearing action.

H. NEXT BASIS CHANGE
Select the smallest transition that improves qualified reachability,
discrimination or reconstruction enough to justify its burden.

I. RECONTACT
Observe consequence and revise the basis/path model.
```

This procedure is a research maneuver. It does not justify a `ReachabilityPlanner` service.

## 10. What exploration-process material deserves persistence?

There is no context-free future-importance oracle. Therefore do not score every intermediate idea by one predicted future-value scalar.

Use a deletion-oriented query instead:

> **If this causal delta disappears from ordinary recovery, can a plausible future consumer become materially worse at generating, rejecting, reconstructing or reopening capability paths?**

High persistence pressure exists when the delta records one or more of:

```text
load-bearing prerequisite / complement
route-opening instrument or representation
route-closing falsifier
reason a tempting route failed
condition under which a failed route can reopen
reconstructible capability-production method
coordination/standard/interface transition
bottleneck migration
option value under changed objective
lock-in / path-dependence warning
source/currentness boundary needed to interpret the transition
```

Low persistence pressure exists when:

```text
current authoritative state is sufficient for the future decision
and
history adds no route / blocker / prerequisite / reopen /
search-geometry / reconstruction information
```

This preserves the existing Atlas rule:

```text
ProcessMemory != RawTranscript
```

and adds:

```text
TerminalResultValue
!=
TransitionValue
```

No private chain-of-thought or verbatim scratch process is required.

## 11. Historical replay remains useful despite its negative benchmark result

HD9 correctly rejected modern historical replay as a clean test of independent scientific rediscovery because pretrained-model future knowledge leaked through temporal identity.

That negative result does **not** make historical replay useless.

It changes the admissible claim:

```text
HistoricalReplay
!=
ProofOfIndependentRediscovery
```

but replay can still test:

- whether a representation exposes the right blocker;
- whether an affordance manifest prevents future-tool leakage;
- whether the Agent proposes actions unavailable in the historical basis;
- whether different path representations change route selection;
- whether a Historical Reachability lens improves prerequisite/blocker decomposition relative to terminal-only history.

So the next historical experiments should be **mechanism/representation tests**, not claims that a modern model authentically became an eighteenth-century scientist.

## 12. Mature external-method subtraction

This correction does not claim that Ordivon invented active experimentation, simulation, model-based exploration or how-possibly reasoning.

Mature experimental-design practice deliberately changes factors and compares responses under controlled conditions. Modern simulation is used across science and engineering for prediction, explanation, heuristic exploration and parameter-space search, including cases where direct physical experimentation is infeasible. Philosophy-of-science work also distinguishes how-possibly models from how-actually-enough explanations: a possible mechanism can legitimately enlarge a hypothesis space without yet establishing the real mechanism.

The Ordivon-specific residual is therefore not a new philosophy of evidence. It is a **typed routing discipline**:

```text
uncertainty / blocker
→ choose an evidence regime with the right discriminating power
→ preserve its claim ceiling
→ combine with current owner truth before action
```

## 15. Falsifiers / next active research

This line should be attacked actively, not left waiting for accidental evidence.

### F1 — HD9 map already fully subsumes Historical Reachability

Recode Historical Reachability as a projection over H1-H7. If the new lens never changes route/blocker/prerequisite/reopen judgments beyond reading H1-H7 directly, the new label may be unnecessary.

### F2 — terminal-only history is sufficient

Construct matched historical packets:

```text
terminal conclusion + current standing
vs
source-fenced causal trajectory / reachability transitions
```

with the same target question. If finite consumers make the same qualified route/blocker/reopen decisions at similar burden, active lineage exposure is not earned for that query class.

### F3 — transition labels add no search value

Compare raw causal trajectories with a compact reachability-transition projection that does not add facts. If candidate route quality, blocker localization, historical-tool leakage and stop decisions do not improve, do not default-expose the projection.

### F4 — historical analogies mislead more than they help

Use anti-cases where historical similarity hides changed scale, authority, materials or causal regime. If transfer errors dominate qualified route gains, strengthen the transport gate or narrow use.

### F5 — simulations/how-possibly models create false confidence

Construct cases where several plausible basis-transition models reproduce a target. Require discriminating Reality contact before selecting among them. If consumers repeatedly lift possibility into truth, strengthen representation/non-claim boundaries.

### F6 — source-locked failed/dormant cases provide no additive option information

Ablate failed/dormant/standards/infrastructure cases from a historical packet. If future route generation and blocker reconstruction do not degrade, their active exposure can contract.

## 17. Post-experiment correction — keep the causal delta, delete the transition ontology

The HR0–HR4 programme was executed after the initial formulation above. It materially narrows Sections 8, 10, 15 and 16.

### HR1 — terminal-only vs causal-lineage was a ceiling control, not a positive result

HR1 froze 10 historical reachability questions with two treatments and two replicates:

```text
TERMINAL_ONLY
vs
CAUSAL_LINEAGE
```

The planned 40 DeepSeek v4 Flash trials produced:

```text
TERMINAL_ONLY   20 / 20 exact next action
CAUSAL_LINEAGE  19 / 20 exact next action
                + 1 Provider recovery/carrier error
```

The correct interpretation is **not** that terminal history is generally sufficient.

The candidate actions made the correct move too easy to recover from ordinary reasoning. The experiment therefore lacked discriminating power. HR1 is retained as a negative experiment-design result:

```text
HighAccuracy
!=
TreatmentInformationGain
```

Do not use HR1 to justify default lineage exposure or default lineage deletion.

### HR0 — the proposed T1–T10 transition classes failed as a stable representation

HR0 source-froze 18 existing HD trajectories and independently coded each twice using the T1–T10 classes from Section 8, with `OTHER` and `NONE` available so the model was not forced to fit every case.

One mechanical null result was recovered separately without rerunning the already committed observations. Effective final result:

```text
18 / 18 trajectory pairs comparable
exact transition-label-set agreement   3 / 18
mean Jaccard agreement                 0.565
```

The distribution was highly nonuniform:

```text
T7_VALIDATE_REALIZE_STAGE   28 assignments
T2_COMPLEMENT               13
T4_REPRESENT_STANDARDIZE    12
T1_POTENTIATE                8
T3_INSTRUMENT                6
T9_SCALE_MOVE_BOUNDARY       6
T5_RECONFIGURE_RECOMBINE     4
NONE                         3
T6_EXTERNALIZE_ACCESS        2
T8_CONSTRAIN_PRUNE           2
```
