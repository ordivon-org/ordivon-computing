## Standing

**MINIMAL FIRST SYNTHETIC ENGINEERING FLOOR — CLOSED**

This closeout is intentionally narrower than “engineering software complete.” It means Ordivon now has a current, recoverable first software substrate that can carry bounded engineering intent through structured artifacts and machine-checkable consequences across more than one provider.

The closure is supported by current canonical evidence rather than historical prose:

## Why this is enough to stop expanding the software floor

The original first-floor pressure was not “own every engineering tool.” It was to establish a small Agent-usable synthetic substrate with at least one credible authoring path, one EDA path, one solver path and one real cross-provider consequence bridge. Those deletion-essential obligations are now met under current source/provider evidence.

Continuing to add build123d/FreeCAD/OpenModelica/FEA/CFD or more EDA mutations merely because they exist would reverse the pressure-driven rule. They should return only when a concrete experiment cannot be expressed, checked, revised, or bridged through the current floor.

## PCB breadth is deliberately not expanded now

The current physical Laboratory plan provides a direct falsifier for premature PCB ownership: breadboard is the reversible exploration substrate, perfboard/protoboard is the stabilized intermediate substrate, and a custom PCB is earned only when repeated rebuilds, wiring error, connector density, electrical-layout sensitivity, or remote-PCBA economics make it consequential. The current active Laboratory Task does not yet contain such a concrete custom-PCB consumer.

Therefore the new KiCad P5 standing remains **bounded semantic schematic revision**, not “full schematic + PCB authoring.” This is a successful subtraction, not a missing benchmark.

## What remains open

The next important deficits are outside simple provider accumulation:

1. **Synthetic → physical** — compare predicted/design consequences with instrumented physical Reality rather than another synthetic oracle.
2. **Measurement information sufficiency** — choose observations/characterization that can actually discriminate the hypotheses an experiment is meant to test; more sensors or more relations are not automatically more informative.
3. **Physical E1 execution** — clear acquisition/site/safety/identity gates and build the first bounded physical falsifier loop.
4. **Pressure-driven specialist reopening** — component-complete board 3D, full-schematic simulation models, PCB layout, FEA/CFD and provider tournaments reopen only when a real downstream claim needs them.
