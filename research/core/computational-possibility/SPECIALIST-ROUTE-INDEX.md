# Computational Possibility — Specialist Falsification Registry v1

## 1. Role of this registry

Specialist routes are **derived-theory and falsification laboratories**. They are not Foundations, not a primitive numbering sequence, and not an automatic roadmap.

Each route is recorded by five questions:

1. What was the strongest attempted architecture attack?
2. Which current CP coordinates absorbed the hostile case?
3. What derived result or permanent distinction survived?
4. What is the route's current semantic status?
5. What concrete evidence would justify reopening it?

`NO_NEW_FOUNDATION` is a first-class successful result when hostile cases enrich theory and the current architecture survives.

Whole-owner architectural closure does not imply mathematical or literature exhaustion of any specialist field.

## 2. Status legend

- **CLOSED DERIVED** — the route reached local architectural saturation under its completed hostile tests; future literature growth alone does not reopen it.
- **PARTIAL HISTORICAL DERIVED** — useful route results survive, but the route itself was not completed; it has no inherited priority under current whole-owner closure.
- **SATURATION CONTROL** — a targeted whole-owner/control test rather than a specialist programme; it searched for deletion-essential residue and found none under current evidence.

A route may reopen only from a concrete CP architecture/boundary falsifier or an explicitly justified whole-owner reopen. Historical route numbering never supplies current admission.

## 4. D1 — Distributed / Information-Topology

### Strongest attack

Distributed computation appeared to require notions beyond ordinary existence: local views, communication topology, fairness/justness, reactive strategies, stochastic schedulers, adaptive Byzantine adversaries, dynamic networks, memory/randomization architectures and local-to-global compatibility.

### Reconstruction

The hostile cases remained representable by explicit:

- participant roles and local `InformationInterface` structure;
- interaction/environment/process structure;
- strategy/family witness grammar;
- memory/communication/probability constraints;
- source-owned task/game semantics;
- typed witness/contract translators for simulation and lower-bound transfer.

### Surviving results

- distributed solvability remains regime-relative witness/strategy existence;
- lower-bound transfer requires an explicit transformer/coverage relation and resource distortion;
- `ProofMethodBarrier != ComputationalLowerBound`;
- actual dynamic-network capability remains Network-owned;
- topology/sheaf/local-global characterizations can be powerful derived mathematics without becoming a CP Foundation.

### Current status

`D1 = CLOSED DERIVED`.

### Reopen condition

Reopen only if a concrete distributed case fixes the current source/role/information/process/witness/resource/obligation coordinates yet changes a genuine CP-owned solvability/limit truth, or exposes an explicit owner-boundary failure. New distributed terminology or literature volume is insufficient.

## 5. D2 — Derandomization / Pseudorandomness

### Strongest attack

Computational indistinguishability, pseudorandom objects, hitting sets, hardness-randomness implications, one-wayness/amplification, observer classes, interactive security reductions and quantum pseudorandomness appeared capable of defining an irreducible relation beyond witness existence.

### Reconstruction

The route reconstructed these as combinations of:

- observer/distinguisher witness classes;
- randomness/query/copy/ancilla information and witness architecture;
- source-defined error/security-game obligations;
- negative achievability for distinguishers;
- higher-order PRG/hitter/derandomizer constructors;
- explicit implication/translation and resource-distortion claims.

### Surviving results

- pseudorandomness is observer/regime-relative negative achievability, not a universal object property;
- constructive derandomizers and extensional class inclusions are distinct derived forms that may be theorem-equivalent;
- `ProxyModelBarrier != TargetRegimeComputationalBarrier`;
- proof/construction barriers do not establish target computational impossibility;
- Security owns game/threat/breach semantics; CP owns efficient witness-existence implications over those constituted games.

### Current status

`D2 = CLOSED DERIVED`.

### Reopen condition

Reopen only on a matched-world case holding target object semantics, observer class, access interface, error obligation, witness grammar and resources fixed while a genuine extra pseudorandomness/derandomization CP truth varies, or on a precise owner-boundary falsifier.

## 6. D3 — Proof Search / Automatizability

### Strongest attack

Proof-object existence and the ability to discover/automatize proofs appeared to separate `existence` from `discoverability` into two primitive CP relations.

### Reconstruction completed in Round 1

- proof validity/proof-object semantics remain Proof/Logic/source-owned;
- a proof checker, searcher or automatizer is a computational witness;
- discovering a witness can itself be represented as existence of a higher-order search witness;
- shortest-proof-relative automatability can be stated using source-defined proof validity/artifact benchmarks plus an outer search obligation.

### Surviving result

`WitnessExistence != WitnessDiscoverability` is a real distinction, but current evidence does not require a `Discoverability` or `Automatability` Foundation.

### Current status

`D3 = PARTIAL HISTORICAL DERIVED — ROUND 1 ONLY`.

The route was not completed. Its historical parent Algorithmics/AlgF0 continuation was superseded during canonical fork reconciliation. This partial status must not be upgraded to CLOSED merely because whole CP architecture later closed.

### Reopen condition

Proof Search may reopen only from a concrete current CP falsifier—for example, a deletion-essential discoverability/automatability truth not representable as higher-order typed witness existence with source-owned proof semantics—or another explicitly justified whole-owner reopen. Incomplete proof-complexity literature and the old D3 next-route selection do not supply current priority.

## 7. Infinite / Continuous / Limit Computation

### Strongest attack

Represented spaces, Type-2 computation, Weihrauch structure, SCI/towers, infinite/iterated limits, semidecision, partial/multivalued targets, exact versus approximate outputs, unavailable computable convergence rates and infinite-dimensional certified computation challenged finite/discrete algorithm intuition.

### Reconstruction

The route required explicit:

- source/target representations;
- oracle/sampling/information interfaces;
- name/realizer/tower witness grammars;
- output/evidence obligations;
- effective modulus/error/certification constraints;
- source/SCD-owned interpretation and validity semantics.

### Surviving results

- semidecision differs from decision through output/evidence semantics, not a second possibility primitive;
- partial/multivalued computation is ordinary realizer existence over source-defined relations/domains;
- exact represented output and arbitrary-precision approximation may coincide or differ depending on representation/obligation, so no universal Exact-vs-Approximate ontology is admitted;
- convergence without an effective rate is weaker when the obligation omits effective error access;
- certified approximation adds source-owned validity semantics plus an ordinary witness obligation.

### Current status

`InfiniteContinuousLimit = CLOSED DERIVED`.

### Reopen condition

Reopen only if a theorem-independent infinite/continuous/limit phenomenon cannot be represented through current representation, information, witness, process, obligation or resource coordinates while those existing coordinates are held fixed.

## 8. Meta-Computational Complexity

### Strongest attack

Quotation, universal evaluation, self-application, fixed points, hardness magnification, search-to-decision, dynamic/self-modifying systems and mutable computational rules threatened the requirement that a ComputationalRegime be theorem-independent.

### Reconstruction

A self-modifying computation is represented as a trajectory such as

`tau = (R0,s0,w0,R1,s1,w1,...)`

under an explicit outer transition/admission or whole-history semantics. Lower-level rules may be mutable state; their validity cannot be grounded merely by their own future declaration.

### Surviving results

- `SelfModification != SelfGrounding`;
- dynamic regimes are trajectory/process constructions, not a new existence relation;
- resource-sensitive fixed points remain existence/construction claims with explicit overhead;
- hardness magnification and search/decision relations remain implication/transformer structures;
- self-ratifying admissibility without an outer truth condition is underconstituted/vacuous rather than stronger computation.

### Current status

`MetaComputationalComplexity = CLOSED DERIVED`.

### Reopen condition

Reopen only if a concrete self-referential/dynamic computational system has valid theorem-independent computational meaning yet cannot be represented by explicit trajectory/evolution/admission semantics without theorem stuffing.

## 9. Sampling / Generative Distribution Complexity

### Strongest attack

Sampling appeared to replace value computation with generation of a probability law, while population/SMC methods, annealing paths, history-dependent samplers, perfect sampling, unbounded stopping and coherent quantum sampling challenged ordinary witness/output models.

### Reconstruction

The route used:

- source-owned target law/measurement semantics;
- output-law obligations such as exact or approximate distribution generation;
- randomness, oracle, copy and measurement interfaces;
- sampler, population/family and trajectory witness grammar;
- stopping/coalescence policies;
- expected/tail/worst resource profiles.

### Surviving results

- samplability is witness existence for an output-law obligation, not a separate CP relation;
- particle population, empirical measure, estimator and a single generated sample are distinct typed outputs;
- annealing/tempering paths are source/design structure whose choice may alter resource frontiers;
- perfect sampling is exact-law satisfaction plus stopping/resource obligations;
- coherent quantum sampling changes source/interface/witness structure, not the existential engine.

### Current status

`SamplingGenerative = CLOSED DERIVED`.

### Reopen condition

Reopen only if all relevant source-law, interface, witness/trajectory, output/stopping and resource coordinates are fixed while an additional CP-owned generative possibility truth varies.

## 10. Online / Causal / Sequential-Information

### Strongest attack

Online computation introduced non-anticipation, sequential revelation, adversarial/stochastic feedback, irreversible/recourse-limited actions, unknown horizons, advice/prediction, performative/endogenous environments, concurrent event-posets, distributed delays, strategic signaling and internally generated world-model predictions.

### Reconstruction

The route used:

- participant-specific information filtrations/revelation interfaces;
- event/process/causal-availability structure;
- action-transition and strategy/trajectory grammar;
- source/Game-owned environment, payoff and response semantics;
- benchmark/counterfactual obligations;
- switching/recourse/communication/regret resources.

### Surviving results

- online versus offline is an admissible-information/process distinction, not two existence relations;
- performative environment change is source-defined environment dynamics; CP owns computation relative to it;
- distributed online policies combine local information/process structure with communication resources; physical delivery remains Network/Runtime truth;
- strategic signaling semantics remain Game/Mechanism/source-owned;
- external advice changes `InformationInterface`, while self-generated predictions are internal witness state/computation.

### Current status

`OnlineCausalSequential = CLOSED DERIVED`.

### Reopen condition

Reopen only if source/role/event/interface/action/benchmark/resource structure can be held fixed while a genuine extra online/causal CP truth changes, or if a new nonclassical causal-availability form defeats the current theorem-independent process coordinate rather than merely refining it.

## 11. Total Search / Provable Totality and final saturation controls

### Role

These were whole-owner deletion/category controls, not an eighth specialist programme. They tested whether totality/provability or an overlooked category could reveal a deletion-essential relation or ninth independent axis.

### Result

No candidate survived the final matched-world/category-level saturation tests under current evidence. This supports the current zero-Foundation/eight-axis architecture but does not turn the control itself into a Foundation or specialist owner.

### Current status

`FinalResidualControl = NO_DELETION_ESSENTIAL_SURVIVOR`.

### Reopen condition

Only a concrete future matched-world or equivalent architecture/boundary falsifier can override this result.

## 12. Cross-route permanent controls

The specialist programme collectively established several permanent research controls:

- rich specialist mathematics does not imply a new primitive relation;
- a richer `ComputationalRegime` is allowed only when theorem-independent and non-stuffed;
- the same existential engine may range over programs, strategies, families, samplers, realizers, controllers, trajectories and higher-order transformers;
- unification of the existence engine does not erase distinctions created by representation, information, process, obligation, witness or resource coordinates;
- `ProofMethodBarrier != ComputationalLowerBound`;
- `ProxyModelBarrier != TargetRegimeComputationalBarrier`;
- `FormalAchievability != ActualRealization`;
- owner-specific semantics and actual facts remain source-fenced.

## 13. Future route-selection rule

There is currently **no default next specialist route**.

A future route begins only after a concrete whole-owner reopen/falsifier and a fresh information-gain comparison. It must not be selected by:

- historical D-number sequence;
- unfinished literature;
- an old Host READY/next-route state;
- disciplinary prestige;
- convenience or project symmetry.

The correct current posture is:

`Closed architecture -> open derived mathematics -> reopen only from falsification evidence`.
