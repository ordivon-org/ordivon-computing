# Computational Possibility — Project Formation Contract v1

## 1. Purpose

This contract turns the completed Computational Possibility research corpus into a durable independent research project without changing the canonical theory, reopening Foundations, selecting a new specialist route, or forcing a repository split.

Project formation is successful when a future researcher or Agent can recover the current project identity, theory, standing, evidence history, boundaries and reopen discipline from the project root without reconstructing stale conversations or treating Host continuity as domain authority.

## 2. Formation phases

### P0 — Constitution and theory entry surface

Materialize and freeze:

- project identity and referent;
- current standing and zero-Foundation discipline;
- constitutional laws and owner boundaries;
- canonical theory map;
- research/reopen controls.

P0 performs no fresh foundational research.

### P1 — Canonical theory presentation

Normalize the first-reading theory surface around:

`Computational Situation -> Admissible Witness Space -> Achievability -> Derived Structures`.

Existing detailed artifacts are referenced and reorganized for navigation; they are not silently rewritten into new claims.

### P2 — Genealogy and negative history

Preserve Broad Computing -> owner decomposition -> T -> Algorithmics -> AlgF0 -> Computational Possibility -> AlgF0 supersession as research genealogy. Historical admission and current standing must remain simultaneously recoverable.

### P3 — Specialist research registry

Expose specialist routes by semantic status and falsification role. Historical numbering is provenance only. A partial route remains partial; closure of the whole owner does not fabricate route-level literature completion.

### P4 — Applicability integration

Expose the existing theorem-transport bridge and thin spec/schema/fixture artifact as derived Engineering Consumption. Do not infer theorem truth, premise truth or owner authority from structural schema validity.

### P5 — Formation audit

A first-time reader should be able to answer the Project v0.1 recovery questions without consulting historical conversations.

## 3. Project v0.1 recovery questions

Formation v0.1 passes only if the project root answers all of the following:

1. What truth family does Computational Possibility own?
2. Why is it distinct from Semantics of Computational Descriptions?
3. Why are there zero numbered Foundations, and why is that a positive result?
4. What is the canonical computational-situation coverage schema?
5. How does witness existence generate achievability, impossibility, complexity, reduction and power?
6. Which specialist routes are closed, partial or historical, and what did they test?
7. What is the status of Proof Search / Automatizability?
8. What happened to Algorithmics and AlgF0?
9. When may the architecture reopen?
10. How may a formal claim apply to an actual target, and why do upper and lower transport use opposite directions?
11. Which external owners remain authoritative for semantics, execution, network, physical, orchestration, security and other imported facts?
12. What is semantic project independence, and why does it not currently require a standalone repository?

## 4. Research lifecycle

Future project-native investigations should follow:

`Question -> Proposition/Rival -> Reconstruction -> Hostile Cases -> Architecture Test -> Result -> Closeout`.

A specialist investigation must distinguish:

- **positive theory:** what computational structure the domain contains;
- **reconstruction:** how far the current CP coordinates express it;
- **hostile candidate:** the strongest plausible deletion-essential novelty;
- **falsifier:** whether a matched-world or owner-boundary case actually breaks the architecture.

A `DERIVED` result is first-class. `NO_NEW_FOUNDATION` is not a failed investigation.

## 5. Specialist-route admission

No specialist route is selected by numbering, recency, historical residue or disciplinary prestige.

A route may start only after a fresh whole-owner comparison considers at least:

- expected information gain;
- architecture-falsification value;
- current coverage depth;
- owner-boundary pressure;
- consumer relevance where appropriate;
- opportunity cost versus no-new-route control.

Historical D1/D2/D3 labels must not be extended as a current primitive sequence.

## 6. Identity and numbering discipline

Stable semantic names are preferred over project-wide Foundation numbering.

If future registries are introduced for research records, their identifiers must not imply Foundation standing, hierarchy, research order or admission. Historical AlgF identities remain immutable historical identities only.

## 7. Authority and provenance

- Owner-native CP corpus is semantic authority for current CP project statements.
- Git/artifacts provide exact durable bytes and provenance.
- Host provides continuity/recovery claims, not CP semantic authority.
- Atlas/Computer projections may reference CP truth but must remain source-fenced.
- External-owner facts used by CP remain owner-authoritative and version/scope qualified.

Project formation must improve recoverability without creating duplicated mutable truth as shadow authority.

## 8. Physical repository policy

During v0.1 formation:

- continue using the existing owner-native CP root under `ordivon-computing`;
- do not create a standalone CP repository merely for symmetry;
- do not move live main while unrelated dirty work exists;
- use isolated workspaces and durable refs for formation materialization when required.

A later repository split requires a separate evidence-based migration decision and must preserve exact provenance/currentness without dual owner authority.

## 9. Stop conditions

Formation work stops rather than expanding by inertia when:

- the v0.1 recovery questions are answerable;
- navigation reaches the existing canonical theory/history/artifact surfaces;
- no semantic inconsistency is found;
- no concrete consumer falsifier requires additional machinery.

Formation does not automatically trigger new research, new engineering, or a repository split.

## 10. Reopen conditions for this formation contract

Revisit the formation shape only if a concrete reader/Agent recovery failure, owner-authority conflict, navigation ambiguity, artifact-consumer falsifier, or durability failure names the missing or misleading surface. Convenience alone is insufficient.
