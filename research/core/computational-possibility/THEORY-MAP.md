# Computational Possibility — Canonical Theory Map v1

## 1. First view

The project is organized around one general pattern:

`Computational Situation -> Admissible Witness Space -> Witness Existence -> Derived Computational Structure`.

The core is not a numbered Foundation. It is a theorem-independent typed description of the computational situation plus ordinary typed/higher-order quantification over admissible witnesses.

## 2. Eight-axis computational-situation coverage schema

The current whole-owner saturation audit found no ninth independently varying CP axis under current evidence.

### Axis 1 — Source / Target Semantics & Representation

What the constituted objects, obligations and outputs mean, and how they are represented. Meaning/preservation remain source/SCD-owned; CP consumes the constituted structure.

### Axis 2 — Information / Revelation Interface

What information a witness may obtain, how, when, from whom, with what query/copy/advice/observation structure.

### Axis 3 — Witness / Strategy / Family / Trajectory Grammar

What counts as an admissible computational witness and how witnesses may be composed, indexed, randomized, staged, interactive, distributed, higher-order or history-bearing.

### Axis 4 — Interaction / Environment / Process / Causal-Availability Structure

How witness and environment evolve and interact: event/process grammar, roles, causal availability, adversarial/stochastic/endogenous evolution, synchronization and other declared process structure. This axis is not restricted to scalar time or classical event posets.

### Axis 5 — Obligation / Output / Benchmark / Quantifier Structure

What success requires: decision, search, realization, semidecision, approximation, sampling, certification, regret/competitive target, totality, probability threshold, family-level or other typed obligations and quantifier order.

### Axis 6 — Transformation / Transport

How witnesses, obligations, representations or regimes may be translated, reduced, simulated or otherwise transformed, including preservation and distortion assumptions.

### Axis 7 — Resource / Performance Structure, Composition & Aggregation

Declared resource/performance coordinates and their algebra: scalar/vector/preorder resources, expected/tail/worst/smoothed/amortized/cumulative forms, reusable/consumable/catalytic/convertible resources, aggregation and trade-off structure.

### Axis 8 — Actual Realization External Owner

Whether formal capabilities are actually available or realized is not CP-owned truth. Runtime, Network, World, Harness and other owners supply authoritative actual-state evidence. CP owns only the conditional formal claim and the derived transport burden.

## 3. Existential engine

For a constituted obligation `O`, computational situation/regime `R`, and declared constraint profile `Gamma`, the central derived notation is:

`Achievable_R(O; Gamma)`

meaning that an admissible typed witness exists under those declarations.

Historical `CExists_R(O; Gamma)` expressed the same idea. It remains useful derived shorthand but is not a Foundation because it is definitionally eliminable into ordinary existential quantification over admitted witnesses satisfying the obligation and constraints.

## 4. Derived theory from achievability

### Possibility

`Achievable_R(O; Gamma)`.

### Impossibility

`not Achievable_R(O; Gamma)` under the same declared inputs.

### Complexity and bounds

Complexity is constraint-indexed achievability. Upper/lower bounds and Pareto/frontier structures arise by varying declared resource/performance constraints rather than by introducing a universal scalar cost.

### Reduction / simulation / translation

A reduction or simulation is a typed higher-order transformer whose admissibility, access, preservation and overhead/distortion conditions are explicit. Reduction existence is itself an achievability claim.

### Computational power

For an explicit obligation universe `U`, a regime's power projection is the set of obligations achievable in that universe. Comparative power is inclusion/implication between such scoped spectra; no context-free universal power ordering is assumed.

### Hardness / completeness / degrees

These are induced structures over declared transformation/reduction relations and scoped obligation universes, not primitives.

### Uniformity / advice / preprocessing / dynamic and meta forms

These are represented by quantifier order, witness-family grammar, information interfaces, indexed resources, staged/trajectory semantics and explicit theorem-independent evolution rules.

## 5. Permanent distinction controls

Unification does not erase distinctions. The project preserves, where the declared situation makes them distinct:

- Decision != Semidecision.
- WitnessExistence != WitnessDiscoverability.
- Randomized != Deterministic.
- Offline != Online/Causal.
- Exact represented output != weaker convergence-only obligations.
- FormalAchievability != ActualRealization.
- ProofMethodBarrier != ComputationalLowerBound.
- ProxyModelBarrier != TargetRegimeComputationalBarrier.

The goal is to locate the coordinate responsible for the distinction rather than create a new existential primitive for every phenomenon.

## 7. Applicability / theorem transport

Formal claims cross into actual targets only through a claim-specific transport burden.

### Constructive / upper

`formal -> actual implementation/realization`.

### Impossibility / lower

`actual -> formal coverage/abstraction`.

### Quantitative variants

Upper applications require suitable domination of actual cost by the formal-to-actual mapping; lower applications require a suitable reflection relation so a hypothetical cheap actual witness would contradict the formal lower bound.

The canonical applicability record is organized around:

`ClaimScope + TargetScope + TransportBurden + PremiseEvidence + Verdict`,

with optional quantitative structure inside `TransportBurden`.

## 8. Architecture reopen test

A new CP coordinate or Foundation is justified only by a concrete case where all relevant current typed coordinates are held fixed but a genuine CP-owned truth still varies, or by an equivalent precise boundary/representation falsifier. Otherwise the correct response is refinement inside an existing axis or derived theory.
