# Computational Possibility Closure and Reopen

`WholeComputationalPossibilityClosure = ESTABLISHED` means local architectural saturation under the current deletion/representation tests. It does **not** mean mathematical or literature exhaustion.

Reopen requires a concrete falsifier such as:

- a matched-world case where a current frozen clause fails while its declared inputs are held fixed;
- a cross-owner anomaly naming the exact CP boundary clause that fails;
- a genuinely unrepresentable target, witness, information/interface, or regime form that cannot be expressed without circular theorem-stuffing.

Research does not reopen merely because time passed, a specialist subfield remains vast, or a numbered Foundation count is zero.
