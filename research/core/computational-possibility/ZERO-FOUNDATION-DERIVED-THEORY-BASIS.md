# Zero-Foundation Derived-Theory Basis

The Computational Possibility core is non-numbered and derived from explicit typed inputs rather than a primitive Foundation registry.

## Basis

1. **Source-constituted typed obligation/problem role** — relational, partial/promise, approximate, probabilistic, interactive/history-shaped, distributed, or higher-order as required.
2. **Theorem-independent typed ComputationalRegime** — witness/carrier grammar, allowed operations/transitions, information/access/representation interface, roles, randomness/adversary assumptions, state/history, indexing/uniformity/advice/preprocessing, composition/capability access, and allowed evolution laws.
3. **Imported interpretation/evaluation semantics** — source/SCD/shared-owner truth, referenced rather than re-owned.
4. **Ordinary typed or higher-order witness quantification**.
5. **Optional quality/performance/resource functionals** — scalar, vector, preorder, expected/tail, amortized/cumulative/regret, Pareto, or other declared forms.
6. **Explicit transformer/reduction/simulation/certificate profiles** when the theorem family requires them.

`InformationInterface` and `WitnessClosureGrammar` are explicit theorem-independent regime coordinates where load-bearing.

Anti-stuffing rule: target answers, theorem conclusions, or arbitrary capability labels may not be encoded into a regime merely to manufacture closure.
