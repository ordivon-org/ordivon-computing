# Derived Cross-Regime Laws

Canonical derived structures include:

- regime-relative achievability/existence notation;
- impossibility as negative achievability under the same declared typed inputs;
- complexity, bounds, and Pareto/frontier structure as constraint-indexed achievability;
- typed reductions and transfer;
- simulation/translation with explicit semantic preservation obligations;
- comparative computational power over an explicit obligation universe;
- induced hardness, completeness, degree, and order structures;
- uniform/nonuniform, advice, preprocessing/training, dynamic/amortized, sequential, and meta-computational variants as typed regime/index/witness constructions.

Useful notation such as `CExists_R(Ω;Γ)` remains derived shorthand. Utility does not make it a numbered Foundation.
