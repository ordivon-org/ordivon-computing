# Computational Possibility — Project Constitution v1

## 1. Canonical identity

**Project / semantic owner:** Computational Possibility.

Historical owner-line labels and the historical project name **Algorithmics** are provenance only. They do not define a sibling owner or a second current project authority.

Computational Possibility is a theory-first research programme. Its semantic independence does not depend on owning a standalone repository, service, runtime, product, or Foundation registry.

## 2. Referent

Computational Possibility studies formal, regime-relative computational achievability and impossibility over already constituted computational obligations.

Its canonical question is:

> Under an explicitly declared computational situation, does an admissible computational witness exist that satisfies the constituted obligation and declared constraints?

Witnesses may be programs, strategies, protocols, circuit/family objects, samplers, controllers, interactive or distributed procedures, approximation processes, trajectories, transformers, or higher-order/meta constructions, provided their grammar is explicit.

## 4. Constitutional laws

### CP-C1 — Regime relativity

Computational possibility is never asserted without a declared computational situation. Relevant source/target representation, information access, witness grammar, process/interaction structure, obligation/quantifiers, transformation assumptions and resource structure must be explicit where load-bearing.

### CP-C2 — Theorem independence / anti-stuffing

A ComputationalRegime or any equivalent coordinate must be theorem-independent. Target answers, theorem conclusions, unexplained capability labels, or self-ratifying admissibility rules may not be inserted merely to manufacture achievability or closure.

### CP-C3 — Owner separation

Computational Possibility reasons conditionally over imported truths; it does not re-own them. In particular:

- denotation, representation meaning and semantic preservation remain source/SCD/shared-owner truth;
- proof validity and proof-object semantics remain Proof/Logic/source truth;
- actual execution, resource availability and attempt lifecycle remain Runtime truth;
- actual reachability, topology, routing and communication capability remain Network truth;
- physical/substrate realizability remains World truth;
- concrete orchestration remains Harness truth;
- strategic/payoff/incentive semantics remain Game/Mechanism/source truth;
- authority/security truth and empirical/statistical validity remain with their respective owners.

References to such truths do not transfer authority to Computational Possibility.

### CP-C4 — Zero-Foundation discipline

No notion is admitted as a CP Foundation because it is useful, ubiquitous, mathematically rich, or convenient. A future Foundation requires a concrete CP-owned truth family that survives at least:

1. deletion-essentiality;
2. non-circularity / anti-stuffing;
3. broad cross-regime pressure;
4. source-owner separation;
5. a matched-world counterexample showing a genuine CP truth varies while the existing architecture is held fixed.

Absent that evidence, the correct result is derived theory, not ontology expansion.

### CP-C5 — Directional theorem transport

Formal computational claims do not automatically apply to actual systems.

- constructive/upper application requires a `formal -> actual` implementation/realization transport;
- impossibility/lower application requires an `actual -> formal` coverage/abstraction transport;
- quantitative claims additionally require explicit resource/performance domination or reflection relations.

Applicability remains target-, scope-, evidence- and currentness-sensitive.

## 5. Canonical theory posture

The current core is a **Zero-Foundation Derived-Theory Basis**. Ordinary typed or higher-order witness quantification over explicit theorem-independent coordinates is sufficient under current evidence.

Core derived families include:

- achievability and impossibility;
- constraint-indexed complexity, upper/lower bounds and frontiers;
- typed reduction, simulation, translation and transfer;
- comparative computational power over an explicit obligation universe;
- induced hardness, completeness and degree structures;
- uniform/nonuniform, advice, preprocessing, dynamic, trajectory and meta variants.

These are rich theory. They are not evidence that a numbered Foundation is missing.

## 6. Specialist research posture

Specialist routes are research/falsification laboratories, not primitive-layer sequences. Completion without a new Foundation is a successful result when the route enriches derived theory and the current architecture survives hostile testing.

No historical route inherits future priority. A new route must be selected from the current whole-owner frontier by information gain and falsification value.

## 7. Closure and reopen

Architectural closure may reopen only on concrete evidence, including:

- a matched-world falsifier with existing coordinates held fixed;
- a genuinely theorem-independent process/interface/witness/resource form not representable by the current architecture;
- a precise owner-boundary falsifier;
- a concrete applicability/artifact dogfood failure that identifies the exact failed CP clause.

Time passing, literature growth, project symmetry, zero Foundation count, or an unfinished specialist literature are not reopen conditions.

## 8. Semantic vs physical project independence

`SemanticProjectIndependence = YES`.

`StandaloneRepositoryRequired = NO` at Project Formation v1.

The shared `ordivon-computing` physical repository is a durability/navigation container, not a semantic owner. A future repository split may be considered only when actual independent work, release, tooling, governance or durability needs justify it without creating dual current authority.

## 9. Non-goals

This constitution does not authorize:

- a CP Foundation registry or CP0/CPF0 sequence;
- a new specialist route;
- a theorem engine, database, MCP surface, global registry or production gate;
- duplication of SCD or external-owner truth;
- physical repository migration;
- claims of exhaustive completion of theoretical computer science.
