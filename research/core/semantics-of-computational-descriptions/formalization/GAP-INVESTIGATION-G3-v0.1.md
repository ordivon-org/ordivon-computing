# SCD Gap Investigation G3 — Negative Closeout v0.1

Status: **negative research result / typed-relation classification**, not Foundation truth.

## G3 — Description Identity

Initial question:

> Does SCD require one irreducible `DescriptionIdentity` relation spanning carrier changes, alpha-renaming, behavioral equivalence, revisions, branches and currentness?

## Result

**Universal DescriptionIdentity gap rejected.**

The pressure decomposes into mature, purpose-specific relation families:

- names/binding/freshness/alpha-equivalence → nominal techniques;
- behavior/equivalence/refinement → framework- and observation-relative semantic relations;
- revision/derivation/specialization/alternate perspectives → provenance/version relations;
- exact stored-object equality → carrier/content-addressed identity;
- branch ancestry/current representative → lineage/currentness relations.

These relations need not coincide, compose across kinds, or define one quotient.

The names above are descriptive; their formal definitions remain framework-owned.

## Consequences

- Same bytes do not imply same semantic role.
- Different carriers may be alpha-equivalent or semantically equivalent.
- Behavioral equivalence does not imply historical/lineage identity.
- Revision/derivation does not imply semantic equivalence or current standing.
- Branches may share ancestry while having independent standing.
- Preservation/refinement does not require declaring source and target to be the same description.

K0.2 needs no identity/lineage primitive. `D` identifies the description object inside one semantic situation; cross-situation relations remain typed claims/correspondences/provenance/dependencies.

## Disposition

- G3 universal identity gap: **REJECTED**.
- Universal `DescriptionIdentity` primitive: **NOT ADMITTED**.
- Typed identity/equivalence/lineage discipline: **RETAINED**.

No SCDF/Foundation/closure standing changes.
