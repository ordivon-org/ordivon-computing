# SCD Gap Investigation G1 — Negative Closeout v0.1

Status: **negative research result / project-architecture classification**, not Foundation truth.

## G1 — Multi-Framework Semantic-Obligation Coordination

Initial question:

> Does a ComputationalDescription lifecycle that spans multiple semantic frameworks require a new SCD meta-semantics or coordination calculus?

## Result

**Independent formal gap rejected.**

After subtracting G2 currentness and G4 correspondence-composition pressure, the remaining mathematical theme is already covered by mature programmes for heterogeneous logical/semantic integration and theory linking.

Institution Theory was explicitly designed to support specification/programming independently of one underlying logical system, transfer results and theorem-prover artifacts across systems, glue signatures/theories, preserve theory structuring under institution morphisms, and combine multiple logical systems through duplex/multiplex institutions.

Hoare/He's UTP and Linking-Theories programme explicitly pursues links/unification across programming languages, paradigms, process algebras and implementation technologies.

Distributed information-flow/logical-environment traditions likewise model heterogeneous local logics and their systemwide semantic integration.

Therefore SCD does not gain a new universal semantics merely because one description lifecycle uses refinement, abstraction, process/interface, transformation, provenance and authorization suppliers together.

## SCD residue

The useful residue is **project-level responsibility-oriented semantic coordination**:

1. admit the ComputationalDescriptionRole and semantic scope;
2. classify the question by SCDF responsibility;
3. select/import an appropriate mature formal supplier;
4. preserve owner, authority, currentness and provenance boundaries;
5. compose correspondences only through framework/bridge-approved rules;
6. retain exact falsifier/reopen provenance;
7. never collapse the suppliers into one fake universal semantics.

This is already substantially represented by:

- Constitution v1;
- K0.2's thin obligation vocabulary;
- the SCDF responsibility matrix;
- AFCM currentness integration profile;
- TCC correspondence-chain discipline;
- canonical/applied falsification corpora.

## Novelty discipline

At current evidence, SCD's defensible contribution at this level is an **object layer / problem-setting / responsibility architecture / integration methodology** around admitted computational descriptions.

That may be practically and conceptually important, but it is not presently a demonstrated new mathematical logic, calculus or theorem family.

## Disposition

- G1 formal gap: **REJECTED**.
- Universal SCD meta-semantics: **REJECTED**.
- Responsibility-oriented coordination architecture: **RETAINED** as project architecture, not new formal primitive.
- G2/G4: remain rejected as independent formal gaps.
