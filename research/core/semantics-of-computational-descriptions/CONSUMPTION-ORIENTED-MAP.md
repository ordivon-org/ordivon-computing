# SCD Consumption-Oriented Map

Status: **EXPLANATORY / NON-AUTHORITATIVE CONSUMPTION AND NAVIGATION VIEW**.

This document reorganizes already-established Semantics of Computational Descriptions (SCD) standing for downstream use. It does **not** create a Foundation, alter any SCDF standing, define a new hierarchy, freeze TRAA, or introduce a production schema. Owner truth remains in the SCD core files and exact source-fenced research results.

## 1. Why this view exists

The immutable `SCDF1–SCDF10` registry is an identity/standing registry, not a research order and not the best order for consumption. The canonical SCD architecture is a typed responsibility hypergraph, so a consumer should begin from its decision/judgement need and recover only the relevant SCD responsibilities, boundary relations and claim ceilings.

The consumption order is therefore:

`Consumer question -> SCD owner boundary -> exact semantic judgement/relation -> applicable standing/support -> cross-owner bridge/firewall -> claim ceiling -> action/no-change decision`.

It is **not**:

`SCDF1 -> SCDF2 -> ... -> SCDF10 -> implementation`.

## 2. Stable owner identity and firewall

SCD owns model-relative constitutive semantic truth for admitted computational-description roles under applicable semantic contracts/models: meaning/behavior, observable projection, composition/context/open interfaces, comparison/refinement, abstraction/approximation, transport/preservation, admissibility/classification, specification satisfaction, predicate transformation, semantic dependency, and semantic evolution/derivation.

SCD does not own concrete Runtime realization/execution/lifecycle/resource truth, Computational Possibility possibility/impossibility/witness/reduction/comparative-power truth, Interlocus/Network actual capability, Harness orchestration, World physical causation/realizability, or Security/Normative/Human/source-domain truth.

A downstream consumer may combine owner-authoritative facts by explicit bridge, but shared repository location, shared vocabulary, successful execution, or convenient representation does not merge semantic ownership.

## 4. Consumer-facing responsibility views

These views are question-oriented projections over existing SCD responsibilities. They are not new Foundations or canonical schemas.

### V1 — Constituted semantic judgement

Use when the consumer asks whether a particular semantic claim is established, unresolved, invalidated, or otherwise supported under a declared semantic situation/qualification.

Recover at minimum:

- exact target judgement/claim;
- admitted computational-description role and applicable semantic contract/model;
- scope/context/qualification relevant to the judgement;
- owner/framework-admitted support or dependency witness;
- authority/currentness/applicability qualifications where they are constitutive;
- exact standing and claim ceiling;
- source references sufficient to recover richer evidence when needed.

Do not convert support presence, provenance presence, task completion, successful verification, or an intermediate standing into the target judgement without the required semantic bridge.

### V2 — Qualified semantic relation

Use when the consumer asks whether two descriptions/representations/states may be compared, refined, composed, transported, substituted, or related through a transformation.

Recover at minimum:

- source and target description roles;
- relation type actually owned by the applicable semantic theory;
- directionality (equivalence is only a special symmetric case);
- scope/context and preservation obligations;
- bridge/correspondence ownership and current applicability;
- explicit non-entailments outside the relation’s scope.

Do not replace typed carrier/alpha/behavioral/refinement/revision/lineage/correspondence/current-standing relations with one universal `DescriptionIdentity` or universal correspondence-composition law.

### V3 — Abstraction/substitutability adequacy

Use when a reduced Agent-visible or system-visible view is intended to substitute for a richer authoritative description for a concrete judgement/obligation.

Current TRAA work supports a bounded question:

> Under a semantic qualification, transformation contract, required judgement family and preservation scope, may this abstraction substitute for the richer authoritative description without changing semantics in an inadmissible direction or licensing unsupported higher-order judgements?

Important separations:

- `ExactProjection != SemanticSufficiency`;
- `SoundApproximation != ObligationSufficiency`;
- `ExactProvenance != DirectionalSemanticSoundness`;
- `LocalAdequacy != ContextualAdequacy`;
- `SemanticSubstitutability != ModelCognitiveReliability`.

TRAA remains a derived-theory candidate and must not be consumed as a frozen universal contract. A consumer should validate the smallest judgement-relative projection it actually needs and preserve richer provenance separately for audit/recovery.

### V4 — Cross-owner semantic firewall

Use when semantic standing is being combined with execution, capability, permission, selection, evidence or external outcome.

Preserve at least these non-entailments:

- `SemanticValidity != RuntimeRealizability`;
- `RuntimeSuccess != SemanticCorrectness`;
- semantic adequacy does not imply contract standing, computational derivability, capability availability, normative permission, Harness selection, Agent competence or Runtime/World success;
- owner-local support/evidence does not automatically create a cross-owner semantic bridge;
- a shared carrier or repository does not create shared semantic authority.

The expected consumer outcome is often **NO_CHANGE** when the downstream owner already maintains these boundaries correctly.

### V5 — Reopen / negative-history view

Use when deciding whether SCD itself should resume research rather than be consumed.

Current watchpoints:

- SCDF2/4/5 remain OPEN but are not selected merely by status;
- Reachability / Alias-Separation remains the strongest residual challenger to `FamilyMapLocalClosure`;
- Resource Usage / Ownership is primarily a typed SCD↔CP↔Runtime boundary watchpoint;
- Name Identity / Generativity remains a residual watchpoint, not a Foundation;
- K0.2 and whole-owner exhaustiveness remain falsifiable/unknown at their existing claim ceilings.

Reopen requires concrete pressure that cannot be represented or repaired by current owner standing; registry symmetry or a desire for completeness is insufficient.

## 5. What current SCD research has already deleted

Do not reconstruct the following as new generic SCD theory during consumption:

- universal SCD root / `SCDF0`;
- universal Semantic Continuity or Currentness primitive;
- universal `DescriptionIdentity`;
- universal correspondence-composition calculus;
- universal concrete `StateSpace` or `TraceSpace`;
- equivalence-only abstraction adequacy;
- truth-role labels as sufficient Agent inference control;
- generic support graph / proof obligation / provenance / warrant machinery as SCD novelty;
- universal `BridgeStanding` engine, support-closure ontology, or global standing service;
- automatic whole-Foundation reopen cascade.

Mature external/framework-owned mathematics remains a supplier. SCD should own only the responsibility/boundary residue that survives subtraction.

## 6. Consumption candidates after this reorganization

Actual consumption must be performed in separately scoped tasks. Candidate consumers are ranked by whether SCD can change a real decision, expose a missing distinction, or correctly certify `NO_CHANGE`.

### P0 — Agent-facing exact judgement standing

Target: a real Harness/Research consumer that must decide whether a higher-order semantic judgement is established from true supporting facts.

Why first: TRAA dogfood found real model- and morphology-sensitive illicit bridge completion, while generic reminders were not universally useful. The discriminating test is whether an exact judgement/currentness-scoped owner-qualified standing projection changes the decision without embedding the answer or creating new authority.

### P1 — Summary / handoff semantic sufficiency

Target: a real Research/Atlas/Handoff consumer where a compressed view must support safe continuation.

Known pressure: bounded reference-based preservation can be sufficient, while frontier-only summary may preserve `RoadmapDirection` but fail `FullSafeContinuationContract`. The consumer test should distinguish recoverable reference preservation from lossy semantic substitution.

### P2 — Cross-owner realization boundary

Target: Runtime/Harness/Interlocus consumers combining semantic validity with concrete realizability/capability/execution.

Expected role: primarily a negative control. Current owner architecture already separates many of these truths; SCD should not force refactoring when the consumer is already correct.

### P3 — Abstraction/transport decision

Target: a concrete representation/working-view transformation where directional approximation, contextual preservation or obligation sufficiency changes an actual choice.

Gate: do not instantiate this by symmetry. Require a current consumer whose decision differs depending on the SCD relation.

## 7. Consumption admission rule

For a named consumer `c`, consume an SCD result `r` only when at least one of the following is demonstrated:

1. **Decision delta** — `r` changes a justified consumer decision under the same evidence;
2. **Consequence repair** — removing `r` reproduces a concrete semantic/control failure;
3. **Boundary preservation** — `r` prevents an otherwise plausible cross-owner overclaim or unsafe inference;
4. **Recovery/persistence value** — `r` makes a required semantic standing recoverable without copying or centralizing owner truth;
5. **Correct NO_CHANGE** — applying `r` shows the downstream owner already embodies the needed distinction, and therefore blocks theory-shaped refactoring.

No global Research→Engineering pipeline, scalar SCD value score, universal schema, or owner-wide migration follows from this rule.

## 8. Reorganization closeout boundary

This document is complete when it lets a fresh consumer answer three questions without replaying the research history:

1. **What semantic question is actually SCD-owned?**
2. **Which exact current standing/relation and claim ceiling apply?**
3. **What must remain with another owner or remain unresolved?**

After that boundary, further work is consumption/validation, not additional SCD reorganization.
