# SCD Owner Boundary and Status

## Owner responsibility

For an admitted computational-description role under an applicable semantic contract/model, SCD owns model-relative constitutive semantic truth about:

- meaning and behavior;
- observable projection;
- composition, context, and open interfaces;
- comparison and refinement;
- abstraction and approximation;
- transport and preservation;
- static admissibility and classification;
- property/specification satisfaction;
- property/predicate transformation;
- semantic dependency;
- semantic evolution and derivation.

## Firewall

SCD does **not** own:

- actual Runtime execution, attempt lifecycle, persistence, or resource consumption;
- Computational Possibility possibility/impossibility, witnesses, reductions, complexity, or comparative power;
- Network topology, routing, forwarding, or actual communication capability;
- Harness task/run/tool orchestration;
- World physical realizability or causation;
- Security, Normative, Human, or other source-domain truth.

Admission of a `ComputationalDescriptionRole` and the applicability/authority of a semantic contract are boundary inputs, not hidden SCD Foundations.

## Status

SCD remains an active semantic owner with a ten-identity immutable Foundation registry. Local family-map closure does not imply whole-owner exhaustive completeness.
