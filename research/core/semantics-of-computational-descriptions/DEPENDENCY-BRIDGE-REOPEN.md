# SCD Dependency, Bridge, and Reopen Architecture

## Dependency discipline

Dependencies are claim-local and typed. A dependency does not imply that one whole Foundation is globally prerequisite to another.

## Bridge discipline

Cross-owner bridges reference the authoritative owner of each participating truth. A bridge does not transfer ownership merely because one owner consumes another owner's semantic predicate or preservation claim.

## Reopen discipline

Frozen claims reopen only through an exact counterexample or condition that identifies the clause that fails. Reopen propagation is asymmetric and scoped to affected claims; it does not automatically reopen an entire Foundation family or the whole SCD owner.

OPEN `SCDF2`, `SCDF4`, and `SCDF5` are not an instruction to continue research during repository materialization.
