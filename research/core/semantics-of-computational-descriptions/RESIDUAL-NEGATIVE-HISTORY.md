# SCD Residual and Negative History

Historical family candidates that do not currently map to dedicated SCDF identities remain preserved as challengers/watchpoints rather than being manufactured into new Foundations.

- **Reachability / Alias-Separation** — strongest known residual challenger to `FamilyMapLocalClosure`.
- **Resource Usage / Ownership** — lower-pressure watchpoint; much actual resource truth belongs to other owners.
- **Name Identity / Generativity** — historical admitted-but-unreconstructed family candidate; current status is residual watchpoint only.

Negative conclusions preserved here include:

- no `SCDF0`;
- no universal mandatory SCD root;
- no license to infer `WholeSExhaustiveComplete` from family-map local closure;
- no license to create a new SCDF for historical symmetry;
- no automatic whole-Foundation reopen cascade.
