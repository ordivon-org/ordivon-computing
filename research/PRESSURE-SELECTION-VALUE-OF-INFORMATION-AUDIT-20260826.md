## 3. Mature external subtraction

The generic question is already densely occupied.

### Optimal / Bayesian experimental design

Classical and Bayesian OED optimize experiment design relative to a declared model and objective/utility. Different criteria answer different questions: parameter-volume reduction, average variance, prediction variance, target quantity of interest, etc. `optimal` is therefore model/objective-relative rather than a context-free experiment ranking.

### Value of Information / EVSI / ENBS

VoI values additional information through the improvement it can produce in a downstream decision under uncertainty; expected net benefit of sampling additionally compares that value with the cost of collecting the evidence. This is stronger than `more entropy reduction is always better`, but requires a meaningful decision/utility model.

### Rational metareasoning / value of computation

Metareasoning values internal computation by its expected effect on the quality of the next external decision. This already owns the generic idea that finite reasoning/observation budget should be allocated by expected downstream decision value.

### Active learning / sequential exploration

Active learning, bandits, knowledge-gradient and information-directed sampling own important special cases where the query/action space, feedback model, objective or regret structure is sufficiently specified.

### Goal-oriented OED

Goal-oriented OED is a particularly important subtraction: reducing uncertainty about every model parameter need not be the relevant scientific objective. Experimental design can instead target a quantity of interest. This directly blocks any Ordivon claim that generic information gain alone is the missing research capability.

Conclusion:

```text
GenericPressureOptimizationTheory = EXTERNALLY_MATURE
IndependentResourceRationalPPDTheory = NOT_EARNED
```

The Ordivon residual is integration under owner-relative authority/currentness/consequence plus empirical proof of selection behavior, not a new optimization theory.

## 4. Selection regimes that survive subtraction

No universal scalar or fixed ordering survives. The useful discipline is **criterion selection before candidate optimization**.

### Hard-admission boundary

An observation/intervention that is unauthorized, unsafe, stale, evaluator-invalid or unrecoverable at the required consequence boundary cannot acquire authority merely by being highly informative.

```text
InformationValue != ActionAuthority
```

### Probabilistic decision regime

When probabilities and downstream decision utility are genuinely grounded, VoI/EVSI/ENBS can be appropriate.

### Probabilistic inference / QoI regime

When a model/prior and inferential or predictive quantity of interest are grounded, Bayesian/OED/EIG or goal-oriented OED can be appropriate.

### Set-valued uncertainty regime

When a finite set of live rivals exists but no justified probability distribution does, use discriminating partitions, counterexamples, worst-case elimination, set-valued diagnosis or destructive falsification rather than silently assigning uniform/arbitrary priors.

### Repeated online decision regime

When the problem genuinely has sequential reward/regret structure, bandit/information-directed criteria can apply.

### Internal-computation regime

When the choice is another reasoning/search/computation step, value-of-computation/metareasoning is the relevant mature abstraction.

### No-admissible-discriminator / unstable-evaluator regime

The correct next move may be to construct observation capability, repair the evaluator/infrastructure, preserve UNKNOWN, wait for natural pressure, or stop.

These are not Ordivon-owned algorithms. They are a routing discipline for not using the wrong mature criterion.

## 5. Closed-loop cost is not one universal number

Current Ordivon evidence repeatedly exposes heterogeneous costs:

```text
compute / model tokens
wall time / feedback latency
material and instrument use
Human attention
verification/evaluator work
environment setup
recovery/reconciliation
irreversible effect risk
opportunity cost
option value of waiting / reversibility
```

An owner/mission may legitimately supply a utility model that makes some of these comparable. Without one, summing them into `information-per-token`, `research-value`, or `tractability` would fabricate weights and erase responsibility.

Thus:

```text
CostVector != UniversalCostScalar
```

## 7. The actual capability decomposition

The audit now distinguishes at least three capabilities:

### PS-A — Local bounded pressure/probe selection

Given the live rival/candidate set, relevant evidence and owner constraints, choose an admissible next observation/intervention/stop decision.

Current standing: **strong**. Multiple owner-native mechanisms plus the 40/40 live campaign support this.

### PS-B — Open-world pressure discovery / evidence acquisition policy

Before the decisive evidence is in Context, decide which owner/source/anomaly/question to inspect first and when to stop inspecting.

Current standing: **unproven / negative bounded evidence**. C6 is the direct falsifier; Harness P4 demonstrates observation authority but not efficiency.

### PS-C — Prospective research-policy calibration

Before outcomes, predict which research bet will create frontier-changing evidence at acceptable closed-loop cost; after outcomes, update the research-taste prior from misses, surprises, false promotions and unexpectedly decisive low-ranked questions.

Current standing: **research hypothesis only**. TM0 is retrospective and no longitudinal calibration loop is established.

This is the central result:

```text
PressureGeneration
!= LocalPressureSelection
!= OpenWorldPressureDiscovery
!= ResearchPolicyCalibration
```

## 8. Why C6 failed while the new 40-trial campaign passed

The difference is not primarily intelligence or missing VoI vocabulary.

In the new campaign, the discriminating facts and candidate actions are already present. The Agent performs **semantic choice over a compiled local decision surface**.

In C6, the real problem precedes that surface:

```text
large owner-change world
→ which evidence should enter Context?
→ which source is worth inspecting?
→ has the evidence budget been spent on the wrong owners?
→ is a hidden cross-owner pressure still outside the current view?
→ when should inspection stop and assessment begin?
```

This is an acquisition/search-policy problem under partial observability and representation constraints. Better local decision doctrine cannot substitute for the missing evidence.

A concise formulation is:

```text
DecisionQualityGivenEvidence
!= EvidenceAcquisitionPolicy
```

Or in Ordivon representation terms:

```text
RelevantPressureExists
!= PressureReachableFromCurrentView
!= PressureSelectedForInspection
!= PressureSemanticallyRecognized
!= PressureAdmittedAsNextResearch
```

## 9. Method consequence

The current Method Canon's instruction to compare `marginal information gain and falsification value` is directionally correct but underspecified if read as a universal numerical rule.

The stable correction is small:

1. pressure construction does not decide pressure selection;
2. hard authority/safety/currentness/evaluator/consequence admission precedes optimization;
3. the information criterion must match the uncertainty/decision regime and target quantity/decision;
4. do not manufacture priors or scalar costs when owner evidence does not supply them;
5. stop/repair/environment-construction is a legitimate selection outcome;
6. local bounded selection is already strong enough that a permanent guide is not justified;
7. the remaining frontier is open-world evidence acquisition and prospective calibration, not a selector service.

The desired future test is not whether an Agent can recite VoI. It is whether its **research bets become better calibrated over time**.
