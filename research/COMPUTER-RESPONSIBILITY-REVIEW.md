# Ordivon Computer Responsibility Review

The current machine authority is [`computer-responsibility-map-v2.json`](computer-responsibility-map-v2.json). It is a **responsibility prior**, not a reform program and not product authority. Current owner-native evidence, exact source revisions, the research portfolio, and domain acceptance override it whenever they differ.

## Current responsibility set

Only ten Computer responsibilities remain active after the removal-first Existence Gauntlet and C1–C5 contraction:

1. `CR-01` — delegate mature mechanics to classical substrate;
2. `CR-02` — durable open-work identity and continuity;
3. `CR-03` — bind cognition to current authorized sources/revisions;
4. `CR-04` — explicit Effect uncertainty and reconciliation;
5. `CR-05` — separate evidence, independent verification, and accepted completion where consequence requires it;
6. `CR-06` — admit authority at the consequence boundary rather than interrupting routine private reasoning;
7. `CR-07` — bind pending work to exact Tool contracts and fail closed on drift;
8. `CR-09` — owner-native observation plus disposable projection rather than shadow authority;
9. `CR-12` — share only boundary contracts with real multi-consumer pressure;
10. `CR-17` — bounded experiment/self-reform discipline with an independent oracle, negative/null outcomes, and rollback where applicable.

These are responsibilities, not commitments to today's object names or storage mechanisms. Each should live at the lowest owner that can preserve the invariant.

## Removed from the active map

Eight older slots no longer receive permanent architecture seats:

- archived: `CR-08` semantic lowering, `CR-14` programmable/Prime cognition, `CR-15` TCG, `CR-16` generic Multi;
- localized: `CR-10` Harness cognition packaging, `CR-18` participant/commitment organization;
- deleted as specific negative placeholders: `CR-11` general Memory runtime, `CR-13` generic World layer.

Their historical experiments, negative results, and reopen conditions remain in Git, the portfolio, Knowledge, and closeouts. Removing the slots does **not** mean these ideas can never reappear. It means absence of evidence no longer earns a permanent active placeholder. A materially different owner-native failure can reopen a new question through the portfolio.

## What the contraction changed

- historical experiment executables moved out of the active tree while Git retained exact bytes;
- Content shrank from a four-package governance stack to a small managed-document metadata validator;
- the 784-line global research Method/checker/projection was replaced by a 122-line experiment-local declaration contract and checker;
- Protocol 0.3 remained immutable while current source became an explicit unreleased 0.4.0.dev0 candidate without `ordivon_semantics`; no consumer was upgraded automatically.
