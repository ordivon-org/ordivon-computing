## 4. Facility cart — DO NOT SELECT FAN YET

The site supports direct external exhaust, so QUICK 6601/6611 are outside the first-order order.

Before selecting facility hardware, measure:

```text
bench soldering source position(s)
hood shape / feasible hood-to-source distance
outlet location
duct route length
number/type of bends
feasible duct diameter
make-up-air path
cleaning/access constraints
```

Then compile:

```text
close-capture hood / partial enclosure
+ short smooth maintainable duct
+ pressure-capable fan selected against actual system resistance
+ safe outdoor outlet
```

Commission at the real soldering posture. Free-air m³/h alone is not an admission criterion.
