### SRE-02 — Genuine-Wait Rediscovery

Construct several renamed future-process problems in which (a) a current target-preserving substitute exists, (b) no substitute exists but the future target process remains reachable, (c) neither current substitute nor future target process is reachable, and (d) partial side research is still possible while final adjudication waits. Hide PPD Q1/Q2 terminology.

Expected structure: distinguish `wait for final target outcome` from `nothing useful can be done`; final genuine wait requires absence of a current target-preserving discriminator plus reachable future target-process support.

Primary capability: boundary rediscovery + action selection.

### SRE-03 — Apparatus-vs-Hypothesis Falsifier

Give a prospective experiment where treatment and sanity/control arms both fail because of a hidden output-contract/apparatus defect. The hypothesis is attractive and the raw treatment result appears negative. Score whether the Agent refuses efficacy interpretation, localizes the apparatus fault, preserves the original instances as contaminated/non-rerunnable evidence, and proposes a fresh prereg lineage rather than silently patching and retrying.

Expected structure: `Execution/ApparatusFailure != ScientificFalsification`.

Primary capability: research integrity + falsification discipline.

### SRE-04 — Standing-Geometry vs Support-Topology

Create an isomorphic data pipeline where a representation/constructor changes ranking/confidence geometry while candidate membership and independent ground-truth support remain unchanged. Include a second case where support changes but ranking barely does. Do not expose EO A/B vocabulary.

Expected structure: ranking/standing geometry and independent support topology are separable; representation change does not imply truth-support gain.

Primary capability: conceptual decomposition + evidence interpretation.

### SRE-05 — Same-Outcome / Different-Repair Coordinate Test

Provide matched cases with similar observed failure/output but different causal loci: representation, interface/grounding, currentness, validation, authority, resource, or objective mismatch. Require the Agent to propose the smallest discriminating probe before repair.

Expected structure: outcome similarity does not imply repair equivalence; correct coordinate separation should predict different probes/interventions.

Primary capability: problem formation + causal repair selection.

### New evaluation bias discovered

SRE-01 and SRE-02 are fundamentally `reason-from-supplied-evidence` tasks. They test structural inference, boundary recovery and conceptual rediscovery, but they do **not** yet test the full active research loop:

`form rivals -> choose discriminating probe -> contact world -> update -> choose next probe -> stop/reopen`.

Therefore `IndependentRediscoveryCandidate` must not be conflated with `AutonomousResearchCapability`.

The next discriminator should be interactive and budgeted. The Agent should receive multiple plausible rivals plus a domain Tool that reveals Reality only after the Agent chooses a probe. Probe budget must make redundant/supporting-only queries costly enough that discriminating-probe selection matters. This directly tests PPD-style pressure construction, experiment selection and revision rather than only answer reconstruction.

Current Harness already exposes a generic `DomainToolLoopRunner` with domain-owned immutable Tool catalog/grant and bounded Harness loop, so the active-discovery test can reuse existing mechanisms. No new benchmark service or generalized research infrastructure is earned.

`ReasonFromSuppliedEvidenceCapability > ActiveDiscriminatorSelectionCapability` on the tested model/scaffold.

Therefore the current research-capability gap is no longer vague. The highest-value frontier is **decision-bearing probe selection + state-consistent evidence update under finite budgets**, not more prose-level research methodology.

Do not repair this by injecting `choose alpha+beta` or by teaching the answer. 1. **Generic research-method prose is not the missing capability.** It did not reliably improve probe policy across AD-01..04 and was negative or neutral in multiple fixtures.
2. **Static representation is insufficient.** AD-02's initial partition worksheet did not establish guaranteed-policy improvement or terminal-state reliability.
3. **Dynamic current-state projection is a strong positive candidate.** The deterministic decision surface achieved full passes in two different adaptive matrices while deletion/control arms failed decisive action-selection steps.
4. The likely mechanism is not 'more information': the projection derives only from already supplied rival/probe predictions plus current observations. It changes **search/action geometry**, carries exact live standing across transitions, and externalizes finite combinatorial bookkeeping.
5. The effect is operation/currentness relative. It should not be generalized into a universal Representation service or static compressed card. This is consistent with the prior CECB negative result: generic cross-artifact compression/projection failed natural replication, while owner/operation-local earned projections remained admissible.
6. The pattern also converges with the 2026-08-27 Agent-Environment falsification result: semantic Environment Compiler was rejected; deterministic mechanical preflight/projection before Agent semantic choice survived. Here the analogous split is deterministic experimental-design geometry before Agent probe choice and scientific interpretation.

### Responsibility-placement candidate

`Deterministic carrier / solver`

- exact live-rival filtering from observations;
- partition geometry under candidate probes;
- worst-case remaining rival count;
- remaining-budget mechanical resolvability.

`Agent / research policy`

- form the semantic rival set and decide whether it is adequate;
- decide which mechanically represented probe is worth executing under task cost/risk/value constraints;
- interpret surprising/model-violating evidence;
- revise rival semantics or admit unknown/model-misspecification;
- choose claim ceiling, stop/reopen, and consequence.

`Environment / representation`

- expose the current decision-relevant geometry at the point of choice;
- recompile it after Reality changes standing;
- bind committed state to actual observation lineage.

This is a stronger fit to responsibility-relative primacy than asking the language model to perform exhaustive combinatorial experiment-design arithmetic internally merely because it can describe the principle verbally.

### Important nonclaims

- This does not establish general Autonomous Research: all AD fixtures supplied the rival set and full probe-prediction model.
- It does not establish a universal information-gain metric or globally optimal experimental-design solver.
- It does not establish cross-model robustness; current Harness has only a live DeepSeek provider adapter, and adding a second provider solely for benchmarking would change the apparatus under test.
- It does not reopen the closed generic Representation/CECB programme; the earned positive is dynamic, current, operation-local and decision-bearing.
- No canonical capability-map promotion is admitted yet.

### Next discriminating experiment

The next useful test is component ablation, not another broad representation benchmark. Split the successful decision surface into: exact live-state carrier only; static initial decision geometry only; dynamic decision geometry after each observation; and full treatment. This can identify whether the gain comes from current-state binding, combinatorial projection, dynamic recompilation, or their conjunction before any owner/Harness engineering proposal.


## AD-05 — component ablation of the decision surface

AD-05 separated the successful treatment into four arms on a fresh eight-rival adaptive matrix. Only `quartz` is a guaranteed first probe; hidden P6 has the guaranteed route `quartz=1 -> sable=1`.

### Baseline

`tes s -> vane` (actual `tess -> vane`) left `{P2,P6}` according to the model's own corrected reasoning, but the terminal structured state committed `{P4,P8}`. This is another direct `reasoning != committed standing` failure.

### Live-state only

The Tool observation supplied only the exact current live-rival set, with no decision-geometry metrics. The Agent chose `sable` first and happened to receive `1`, which uniquely identifies P6 in this hidden world. This is realized success but not a guaranteed policy: `sable` does not preserve identification for all possible initial rivals. More importantly, after reaching a unique standing in one probe, the Agent still spent its second probe on `zero`, which is globally non-discriminating. Exact state binding improved state conformance but did not establish experiment-policy or stop discipline.

### Static decision surface only

The prompt supplied the initial partition/worst-case/remaining-budget geometry but subsequent Tool observations contained only raw probe+observation. The Agent correctly chose guaranteed first probe `quartz`; after quartz=1, however, it chose `vane` instead of `sable` and ended with invalid terminal output. Static geometry improves first-step selection in this fixture but is insufficient for the adaptive transition.

### Dynamic surface

The full treatment supplied initial geometry, exact live state after observation, and recomputed geometry on that live state. It executed `quartz=1 -> {P5,P6,P7,P8} -> sable=1 -> P6` and passed E1-E8.

Pre-frozen adjudication standing:

- exact live-state binding: improves state conformance, not sufficient for guaranteed policy or stop;
- static decision geometry: improves first-probe selection, not sufficient for adaptive second-step selection;
- dynamic recompiled surface: full-pass conjunction candidate;
- component necessity is not yet individually proven because this is one stochastic-model ablation and hidden-specific lucky probes remain possible.

## Evaluation standing after historical forensics + SRE-01..03 + AD-01..06

### Strongly evidenced in the current programme

- research-process integrity: apparatus/effect separation, frozen prerequisites, invalid-result preservation, fresh-lineage repair, bounded claim ceiling;
- structural/mechanistic reconstruction from supplied evidence;
- independent-convergence candidates in historical Ordivon research and prospective answer-withheld structural rediscovery candidates;
- base-model capability is a major contributor and must be subtracted before Ordivon attribution;
- generic research-method prose alone does not reliably improve active experiment selection;
- dynamic, deterministic current decision-surface projection is a replicated active-research capability candidate on two adaptive matrices, with a third component-ablation full pass;
- explicit state/geometry projection can change actual Tool action sequence, not only prose quality;
- finite-rival optimization can preserve model-misspecification unknown rather than silently forcing closure.

### Still not established

- general Autonomous Research;
- open-world problem discovery without a supplied rival/probe model;
- autonomous rival generation and rival-set adequacy judgment;
- robust experimental-design performance under noisy/probabilistic observations, costs, side effects, irreversible probes or continuous parameter spaces;
- cross-model/provider robustness;
- natural real-world research-consumer consequence;
- long-horizon cumulative research advantage from prior Ordivon standing.

### Responsibility-relative interpretation

The evidence now disfavors an architecture where a language model is expected to internally perform every responsibility simply because it can verbally state the method. A narrower ecology is better supported:

`Reality / domain owner` owns probe semantics, evidence truth, constraints and model scope.

`Deterministic experimental-design carrier` owns exact finite bookkeeping that is mechanically derivable from the current explicit model: live-rival filtering, partition structure, worst-case branch size, remaining-budget reachability.

`Agent` owns semantic model formation, value/risk-sensitive choice among admitted actions, interpretation of contradictions, model revision, claim ceiling, stop/reopen and consequence.

`Harness` remains a carrier for bounded interaction/evidence and should not become the semantic owner of research policy.

This matches two existing Ordivon negative/positive boundaries rather than contradicting them:

1. the generic Media×SCD×Harness CECB representation candidate failed natural replication, so no universal Representation service was earned; owner/operation-local earned projections remained valid;
2. Agent-Environment falsification rejected a semantic global Environment Compiler but retained deterministic mechanical preflight/projection before Agent semantic choice.

The active-discovery result is therefore best understood as an **operation-relative mechanically derived research decision surface**, not as a general representation layer or a replacement for Agent research judgment.

### Phase decision

Do not keep manufacturing increasingly elaborate synthetic matrices merely to accumulate wins. The current synthetic programme has identified a real candidate mechanism and its main boundaries. The next promotion-bearing evidence should come from either:

- a materially different model/provider once the Harness naturally supports it; or
- a natural Ordivon research episode with explicit rival hypotheses, candidate evidence actions, finite cost/budget and independently observable consequence, where the decision surface can be inserted/deleted without changing owner truth.

Until then, keep this as detached research evidence. Do not mutate the canonical 28-row capability map, Book, Atlas or Harness product surface from synthetic evidence alone.
