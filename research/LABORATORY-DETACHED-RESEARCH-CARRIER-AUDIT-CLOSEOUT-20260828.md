## Reopen discipline

1. a modern current document/action has an unresolved source dependency on it;
2. a current consumer reaches a distinction whose derivation is missing from newer carriers;

4. a new domain physically activates one of the deferred F-family boundaries.

Do not recursively restore predecessors merely to satisfy historical navigation or document-count symmetry.
