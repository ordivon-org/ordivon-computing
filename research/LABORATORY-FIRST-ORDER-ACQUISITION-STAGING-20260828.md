## Transaction rule

A stage is not a bundle SKU. It may be partially transacted when its actual consumer needs only a subset. Conversely, completing R0 does not automatically trigger R1.

```text
consumer pressure
→ refresh exact transaction currentness
→ Human-authorized payment/order effect
→ arrival/object identity
→ Workstation/local binding where relevant
→ provider/fitness/safety admission
→ experiment consequence
→ next pressure
```

This ordering keeps acquisition subordinate to capability evidence rather than turning the approved portfolio into a completion checklist.
