## Reopen conditions

1. a current consumer cannot recover a standing that the lineage uniquely contains;
2. current owner evidence contradicts or cannot source-fence the retained standing;
3. a natural operation requires a historical prototype for which no current successor exists;
4. owner decomposition has changed so the historical responsibility may again belong locally;
5. durable publication/currentness transport becomes the blocking capability.

Otherwise preserve lineage reachability without forcing lineage embodiment into current main.
