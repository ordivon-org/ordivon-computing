# HD6 Final Report — 1970–1999 Historical Theory Dogfood

## Executive result

HD6 completes the sixth chronological wave of the frozen HD0 campaign. It expanded **130 candidate trajectories** (above the 120 floor rather than deleting ten post-discovery cases), froze the exact 40-case composition, source-locked all 40, mapped the unchanged S1–S10/F1–F7 only after that boundary, ran a fresh 12-anchor independent coder plus a scaffold-ablation audit, and executed 36 prospective replay trials over relational databases, cellular standards, lithium batteries and ozone policy.

The strongest conclusion is not six-epoch theoretical victory. R1–R8 recur again, but exact primary/independent agreement falls to **64/120 = 53.3%** and coarse applicability to **77/120 = 64.2%**. Removing repeated source-template boilerplate improves these only to **73/120 = 60.8%** exact and **81/120 = 67.5%** coarse. Reality recurrence is therefore materially stronger evidence than exact taxonomy recurrence.

## Pass A — 130 → exact 40

HD6 deliberately keeps the larger 130-case discovery pool. The exact frozen roster remains 12 Deep Anchors + 16 breadth + 3 failed/abandoned + 2 parallel + 2 delayed + 2 standards + 2 safety/externality + 1 complement.

Deep Anchors cover microprocessor/PC ecosystems, Unix/C, relational databases, TCP/IP, the Web, cellular/GSM, GPS, recombinant DNA governance, PCR, MRI, lithium-ion and ozone/Montreal. ## Primary frozen-theory coding

Primary Pass B covers 40/40 cases without changing a frozen definition. Anti-cases mostly narrow quantifiers: relational abstraction does not remove physical execution; a stable interoperability standard can be optimal inside a coordination regime; temporary containment can preserve long-run searchable space; safer battery architecture can beat locally maximal energy density; a failed general program can retain subsystem option value.

No primary direct contradiction is recorded.

## Independent coding and confirmation-bias audit

The fresh independent coder produced 120 valid judgments using 82,450 Provider tokens.

```text
primary ↔ independent exact:   64/120 = 53.3%
coarse applicability:          77/120 = 64.2%
raw independent CONTRADICTS:   0
```

S3 remains unusually stable (11/12 exact, 12/12 coarse), S4 is 10/12 exact and 12/12 coarse, and S10 is 11/12 exact/coarse. S1 and S9 initially appear as 12/12 independent SUPPORTS even though primary coding is much narrower.

That ceiling triggered a post-freeze scaffold ablation. Repeated generic fields were removed while the case-specific pressure/world/routes/observations/tests/complements/rivals/summary stayed fixed. The ablation yielded:

```text
exact:                 73/120 = 60.8%
coarse applicability:  81/120 = 67.5%
S1 SUPPORTS:           10/12 (down from 12/12)
S9 SUPPORTS:           10/12 (down from 12/12)
```

Source scaffold therefore affects measurement, but it explains only part of the large disagreement. HD6 rejects any monotonic “more epochs → stable classifier” story.

### Historical-memory leakage

Leakage becomes easier to see in the modern epoch:

- At the 1973 relational cutoff, unscaffolded/affordance proposals jump quickly to cost-based optimizer and even hash/merge-join details.
- At the 1980 battery cutoff, unscaffolded trials repeatedly identify the later carbon route; one explicitly names LiCoO2, graphite/petroleum coke and an 18650-style cell.
- At the 1984 ozone cutoff, several trials target Antarctic/polar-spring chlorine/ClO signatures before the ozone-hole publication.

The affordance manifest keeps raw actions inside contemporaneously available action classes, but does not remove future-specific target knowledge inside those classes. Thus **action-class admissibility ≠ epistemic temporal cleanliness**.

## Anti-case result

No adjudicated direct contradiction emerges. Six historical epochs without a clean contradiction now increases the obligation to attack the codebook, not confidence in universality. HD6’s low coder agreement, scaffold sensitivity and replay leakage are themselves reasons to resist premature convergence claims.

## World-model update

HD6 adds no stable law. It sharpens these non-collapse boundaries:

- abstraction can be more powerful while depending on hidden physical execution;
- interoperability can be a system-level source of value rather than a local performance compromise;
- containment/governance can increase long-run searchable space under uncertainty;
- local peak performance can lose to lifecycle safety/repeatability;
- action availability, action-class compliance and epistemic cleanliness are distinct;
- source representation/scaffold can alter theory applicability measurement;
- repeated historical recurrence is not the same thing as stable taxonomy or universality.

## Disposition

No frozen-theory mutation. No residual promotion. Experiment-specific Provider runners are archived by digest and contracted during repository closeout. HD7 receives unchanged hypotheses plus a stricter warning: later epochs make pretrained-history leakage stronger, so historical replay should be interpreted mainly as harness/calibration evidence rather than counterfactual scientific discovery performance.
