# EX2 final closeout — from fixed axes to sparse responsibility questions

## Result

EX2 is complete. It rejected the initial five-axis Explanation Unit as a whole-family ontology and retained a narrower method:

> Ask the concrete responsibility relation that is uncertain now, name its exact subject, compare against owner-native/classical baselines, preserve negative proof boundaries, and leave unrelated relations unstated.

That method became fully stable in the World × Finance pressure test, but a closed whole-family target vocabulary failed transfer across Security, Game, Human, Studio, and Web. EX2 therefore retains a research method, not a new ontology or service.

## EX2-A — fixed five axes rejected

The inherited axes were semantic choice, durable structural state, physical truth, external-effect truth, and sharing disposition. On 24 World × Finance cases × six paired replicates:

| representation | axis exact | whole-case exact | critical external-effect exact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| compact responsibility | 352/720 = 48.89% | 3/144 = 2.08% | 85% |
| explicit five-axis | 352/720 = 48.89% | 11/144 = 7.64% | 80% |

Classification: `REJECT_OR_REVISE`.

The row itself was wrong. It forced latent questions and conflated state semantics with persistence carrier, provider occurrence with Runtime execution and Finance derivation, and semantic home with classical delegation and shared promotion.

## Whole-family owner-native audit

After the World × Finance method stabilized, EX2 re-read current owner-native evidence and produced `family-relations-v1.json` as an experiment-local deletion/existence map:

- **Computing** — cross-project synthesis and promotion/deletion reasoning; never current product truth.
- **Host** — durable semantic work, commitments, verification, and Task-level consequence admission.
- **Harness** — bounded Agent Run, structural cognition, Provider/Tool lifecycle and recovery; current Gemini evidence strengthens opaque Provider continuation outside Agent cognition.
- **Runtime** — exact trusted-local physical execution and recovery; execution success is not external or semantic success.
- **World** — narrow external correlation/trajectory/evidence/reconciliation seam; providers/source/destination domains retain native occurrence/current-state truth.
- **Finance** — owner-capital semantics, research validity, venue interpretation, effect identity, reconciliation and causal accounting.
- **Security** — adversarial-autonomy laboratory for contested authority, information, physical/world truth, recovery and evaluator integrity; recent IF3 evidence rejected mandatory finalization/readback ceremony as a generic law.
- **Game** — authoritative game-world/rule/action-admission semantics plus playability research; registered Station Zero v2 remains current while accepted v3 is still an unregistered target.
- **Human** — problem-first human-life research under slow, ambiguous, individual and high-consequence outcomes; frameworks never grant authority over a person and no automatic successor taxonomy is justified without new trajectory pressure.
- **Studio** — cross-medium expression/production and grounded creative research; source facts remain owner-native and Agent-observer knowledge transfer does not prove human comprehension.
- **Web** — source-bound public navigation/publication/static projection; source change creates a review obligation, not an automatic public edit, and browser/build success is not human comprehension.

This map is useful audit evidence, not a promoted whole-family ontology.

## Whole-family transfer — formal rejection

Large multi-answer Tool schemas were themselves falsified by Provider serialization behavior, including concatenated extra JSON. Those incomplete campaigns remain apparatus evidence only and are excluded from semantic totals.

The final formal family gate reduced the wire shape to **one relation query per fresh Provider call**, removing that serialization artifact.

| representation | query exact | case exact | infrastructure | domain | negative laws | shared promotion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| compact family | 303/324 = 93.52% | 86.67% | 87.50% | 86.11% | 100% | 100% |
| role-pure family relations | 310/324 = **95.68%** | **88.33%** | 83.33% | **91.67%** | 98.61% | 90% |

Classification: **`REJECT`**.

Some family-gate errors still expose benchmark-boundary ambiguity. For example both representations systematically chose Runtime rather than the frozen Classical-substrate oracle for exact Web browser execution evidence. Per preregistration, post-hoc diagnosis does not alter the score, and fixing that item would still not make all failed family thresholds pass.

The correct scientific disposition is to stop abstraction rather than design a fifth ontology.

## Retained method

1. Reality / recurring pressure.
2. Stronger owner-native or classical/domain baseline.
3. Residual missing responsibility.
4. Counterfactual deletion failure.
5. Only the question-relative responsibility relation currently uncertain.
6. Exact subject identity.
7. Explicit negative proof boundary.
8. Evidence, applicability, and reopen condition.
9. Feynman/public projection only afterward.

The stable grammar includes semantic judgment, state-contract ownership, persistence carrier, native fact versus local execution, mapping/derivation, reconciliation identity, consequence admission, semantic home/classical delegation/shared promotion, and negative implication boundaries. EX2 does **not** freeze a universal answer vocabulary for them.

## Rejected directions

Do not promote a five-axis row, one primary owner, a closed family graph schema, `RelationGraphService`, `ExplanationService`, ontology server, global currentness database, project-name-as-generic-role vocabulary, or typed/causal jargon as mandatory public/Agent prose.

Shared promotion also does not follow merely because multiple domains consume the same Runtime/Harness mechanism.

## RSI consequence

EX2 itself exercised the deeper RSI loop:

```text
shared world model
→ explanatory hypothesis
→ owner-native pressure test
→ falsification
→ representation contraction
→ fresh held-out test
→ local stability
→ whole-family transfer
→ global falsification
→ narrower retained method
```

The recursive core is improvement of the method by which the system decides what distinctions are real and what abstractions deserve to exist. World × Finance demonstrated a stable local method; whole-family transfer then prevented that local success from becoming a premature global law.

## Closeout

EX2 is closed. `family-relations-v1.json` remains recoverable as the owner-native audit hypothesis map for this exact experiment basis, but it is not promoted as current family authority. `method-disposition-v1.json` owns the EX2 research conclusion.

The next phase must not begin by enlarging the relation vocabulary. It should first identify a concrete explanation/comprehension failure that remains after EX2. If compact causal prose already preserves the required responsibility facts, no new ontology work is justified.
