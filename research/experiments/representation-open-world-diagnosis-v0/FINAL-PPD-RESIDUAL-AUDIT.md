## 1. Problem after all subtraction

Original question:

> Can Representation itself be intelligence capability?

Current corrected question:

> When a finite observer/system changes the way owner-qualified Reality is represented, what exactly changed in its effective work situation, what actual/formal capability claim changed, what causal locus deserves credit, and what hidden lifecycle/currentness/recovery obligations were introduced?

The scientific object is therefore not a new intrinsic `RepresentationCapability`. It is a family of controlled interventions on an already constituted finite-intelligence system.

## 2. Pressure

The residual discipline is forced by recurring pressures:

- finite search/attention/context budgets;
- non-equivalence of information availability and effective access;
- representation-dependent operator/search costs;
- model/provider-specific elicitation effects;
- source/currentness/authority boundaries;
- compressed/derived views becoming stale shadow truth;
- responsibility migration to compiler/tool/environment;
- common-mode failures among apparently diverse representations;
- open-world failure attribution and incomplete diagnostic models;
- safe reconfiguration/recovery under semantic obligations;
- temptation to attribute every performance change to the visible representation layer.

None of these pressures by itself establishes a new owner or primitive.

## 5. Final G/R/A disposition

`gra_nonorthogonality_probe.py` is the decisive contraction.

The same bounded success can be produced by:

- G-only locality/reordering;
- R-only direct lookup/operator grammar;
- A-only external filtering.

A realistic change-highlighting intervention combines A and G.

Therefore:

`OutcomeDelta != MechanismIdentity`.

`G/R/A != OrthogonalAxes`.

`OneIntervention can span G/R/A`.

Keep G/R/A only when they help generate a specific competing intervention; the primary scientific record is the actual intervention and deletion control.

## 6. Final SCD × Harness disposition

Generic preservation/reconfiguration is mature theory. Harness already parameterizes Context substitution/OPUR by externally supplied use obligations U.

`scd_harness_deletion_probe.py` shows source-owner U and identical SCD-labelled U yield identical mechanical preservation decisions.

Thus:

`SemanticObligationPresent != SCDCausallyNecessary`.

Default null:

`source/domain contract + mature preservation/refinement + Harness operational reconfiguration`

is sufficient until falsified.

SCD causal promotion requires a **unique derived semantic discriminant** whose deletion changes a real decision and which generic/source-owner controls cannot match.

No current natural workload clears that gate.

## 7. Final Media disposition

Media retains an important owner responsibility: structured observer mediation / artifact-observer encounter.

But generic interface/work-domain theory is mature, and Media-specific capability credit requires deletion against strong generic task-fit representation baselines.

No evidence currently shows Media is deletion-essential to the semantic-reconfiguration seam.

## 8. Final Harness disposition

Harness owns real bounded operational realization: Run-local Context, Tool/action exposure, Context substitution, OPUR reconfiguration, reconciliation and recovery mediation.

But a treatment carried through Harness is not automatically Harness causal contribution. Direct Provider/source controls remain mandatory whenever they can realize the same treatment.

## 9. Final theory statement

The strongest defensible statement is:

> **For finite observers, representation participates in the distribution of search, inference, operation, memory and recovery responsibility across observer, artifact and environment. Changing that distribution can materially alter situated system capability under bounded resources. However, capability itself remains target-, boundary-, condition-, resource- and environment-relative; representation-specific causal credit requires an explicit intervention, mature-prior subtraction, owner-qualified evidence, competing explanations and deletion-positive consequence.**

This is a synthesis/discipline, not a new primitive or exclusive literature novelty claim.
