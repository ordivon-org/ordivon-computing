## 10. Statistical / epistemic boundary

There are only three paired semantic replicates. All three favor projection, but an exact two-sided sign test over only three independent replicate-level directions is `p = 0.25`.

The 120 field judgements are not 120 independent experimental samples and must not be treated as such for inferential significance.

The result is retained because it passes a **predeclared engineering/research falsifier** on a natural workload with a large repeated effect and an independent cost reduction, not because a conventional large-sample significance threshold has been met.

A second materially different consumer/operation is therefore still load-bearing.
