## Programme-level result

The research line began with a risk of two opposite collapses:

```text
natural owner-native event
= only real evidence
```

and:

```text
historical / synthetic / formal possibility
= current Reality truth
```

Both are rejected.

The surviving evidence model is:

```text
uncertainty / blocker
→ choose the evidence regime with the right discrimination
→ respect the regime's claim ceiling
→ combine with current owner-qualified Reality before consequential action
```

The surviving historical representation is not a transition taxonomy. It is a minimal causal delta when deletion of that delta would impair future route generation, rejection, reconstruction or reopening.

## Explicit non-admissions

No evidence from HR0–HR4 admits:

- History owner;
- Reachability owner/service/planner;
- T1–T10 persistent transition ontology;
- global capability or transition graph;
- future-importance scalar;
- universal requirement to retain process lineage;
- natural-event-only evidence hierarchy;
- automatic authority transfer from historical/synthetic/formal evidence;
- guarantee that human-realized targets eventually become Ordivon capability.

## Closeout

**HR0–HR4: COMPLETE for the present question.**

Reopen only for a new falsifier, materially different consumer task, human/independent historical adjudication challenge, or a concrete owner workload where deletion of the causal delta changes route/stop/reopen decisions.
