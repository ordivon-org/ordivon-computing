## Judgment

Historical PF0 correctly names `build-first` as a possible failure mode, and older Ordivon history contains periods of broad speculative internal infrastructure. But this **current fixed window does not establish recurrent Internal-Origin Bias**. The contemporary method already strongly pressures internal mechanisms with mature external theory/providers/artifacts and also protects owner-native residuals when external substitution would erase responsibility.

Therefore:

```text
InternalOriginBias = HISTORICALLY PLAUSIBLE / CURRENTLY NOT ESTABLISHED AS RECURRENT M0 FAILURE
```

Do **not** create `MO-F11 Internal-Origin Bias` now.

The more current failure pressure is subtler:

```text
External capability exists
but
relation-to-consumer is not represented / current / authorized / proven
```

That is better explained by existing discoverability, currentness, sedimentation and capability-realization failures than by a new origin-bias taxonomy.
