# HD7 Coverage / Bias Audit — pre-source-lock

## Frozen decision

The 120-case Pass-A candidate pool is broad enough to freeze the exact 40-case HD0 composition without adding a +10 extension block. The exact roster is not a winner list: it explicitly carries three failed/abandoned programmes, two parallel-discovery cases, two delayed/dormant routes, two standards/infrastructures, two safety/externality corrections and one under-recognized complement.

## Coverage corrections made before freeze

- Computing was initially overrepresented by mobile/cloud/data/ML; the exact Deep Anchors therefore reserve six of twelve positions for genomics/biomedicine, energy, commercial space and astronomical instrumentation.
- Energy is not represented only by renewables: solar scale-up and shale scale-up are both Deep Anchors, allowing materially different scaling/institution/externality regimes.
- Biology is not represented only by successful products: CRISPR, checkpoint blockade, HPV public-health diffusion, RNAi delay, mRNA dormancy, iPSC parallelism and CAR-T early proof occupy distinct causal roles.
- Space is not represented only by a heroic launch vehicle: COTS is treated as an institutional/procurement trajectory and Kepler as an instrumentation/observability trajectory.
- Low-glamour infrastructure is explicitly retained through LTE, Git and warehouse-scale datacenter complements.
- Safety corrections are represented outside the earlier chemical/nuclear accident set through Deepwater Horizon and Fukushima, both within the epoch.

## Known limits

- The pool remains richer in US/Europe/Japan institutional sources than Africa/Latin America; this is recorded rather than repaired with token cases that would not survive the source contract.
- Agriculture is represented in the candidate pool (Golden Rice, Bt cotton, drought-tolerant programmes, precision agriculture) but no agriculture case enters the exact 40 after the HD0 composition trade-off. HD8 must re-audit this omission rather than silently treating HD7 as globally balanced.
- Modern-history pretrained-memory leakage is expected to be severe. Replay is calibration evidence only; it cannot be interpreted as clean counterfactual discovery competence.

## Freeze boundary

No frozen LW0 verdict or prior historical residual label was used to select source-native facts. The five anti-cases were selected before Pass B to pressure scope/quantifier assumptions rather than post-hoc manufacture falsifiers.
