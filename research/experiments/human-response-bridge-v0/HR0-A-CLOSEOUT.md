# HR0-A Human-Response Bridge Closeout

HR0 started from a narrow unresolved claim left by Studio R6 and the FD5–FD6 documentation programme: Agent/browser evidence can establish one observer consequence and one encounter, but it cannot establish a human consequence. The first task was therefore not to search for a better explanation style. It was to determine whether Ordivon already had enough owner-native equipment to cross the observer-class boundary without inventing a new human-experiment platform.

## Disposition

**HR0-A is complete. The bridge apparatus exists, no new cross-owner service was justified, and no human-response result is claimed.**

The surviving composition is:

```text
Computing preregistration / synthesis
+ Studio R6 intervention and oracle
+ Web encounter / viewport evidence
+ Harness surrogate Provider authority
+ Human methodology / privacy boundary
→ sealed human-ready pilot packet
```

No `HumanResponseService`, participant database, analytics platform, universal survey schema, new preference engine, or mandatory human approval gate was added.

After the apparatus passed, its three executable helper scripts were also removed from the active Computing tree. Their exact preregistered bytes remain in Git history (`7f850e3` for v1 and `77070bb` for v2), while the already generated private owner-pilot packet remains available. This keeps optional future calibration recoverable without turning completed HR0-A research into permanent current apparatus.

## Why HR0 reused R6 instead of inventing new treatments

R6 had already frozen a useful same-fact intervention. Each fictitious mechanism contains the same seven factual blocks; only reveal order changes. The initial no-scroll viewport is fixed at 1080×1050. That made observer class the smallest genuinely new dimension. Adding causal diagrams, interactive disclosure, delayed recall, trust, preference, or a different medium in the same experiment would have confounded the observer-class question.

HR0 therefore inherited the three R6 orderings and kept diagram/time/trust/preference work out of scope.

## v1 was correctly falsified before human use

The first 12-run Harness surrogate preflight completed mechanically with zero Provider failure, but exposed an experimenter error in the v1 scorer. v1 had changed the factual target to `O4` whenever required evidence was absent from the initial viewport. That incorrectly collapsed two consequences that R6 had deliberately separated:

- factual answer accuracy;
- evidence discipline / unsupported inference.

A substantive answer may happen to be factually correct while still unsupported by visible evidence. A disciplined `O4` may be epistemically correct while not equal to the hidden factual oracle. Treating those as one score destroyed comparability with R6.

HR0 followed its own stopping law: no human response was collected, v1 was marked falsified, and the sealed H1–H3 bytes were **not** regenerated.

## v2 scoring law

v2 was preregistered at `77070bb78497088f7ad143da619cbaf10c3e186e`. It changes response interpretation, not stimulus bytes.

Three consequences now remain separate:

1. **factual accuracy** — selected option equals the mechanism oracle;
2. **unsupported inference** — a substantive option is selected without all required evidence in the viewport;
3. **epistemic decision accuracy** — when evidence is sufficient, choose the factual answer; when it is insufficient, abstain with `O4`.

Confidence is interpreted as confidence that the selected response is justified by shown evidence, so confidence calibration is evaluated against epistemic decision accuracy rather than hidden factual truth alone. No single aggregate “human understanding score” is created.

A dual synthetic dry run proved the distinction. An always-oracle responder reached 100% factual adaptation but made unsupported inferences and lost epistemic accuracy. An evidence-disciplined responder reached 100% epistemic accuracy and zero unsupported inference while factual adaptation fell where the necessary facts were not visible.

## Fresh v2 Harness preflight

After v2 preregistration, Harness executed 12 fresh structured Runs: 2 preflight mechanisms × 3 reveal orders × 2 replicates. All six admitted DeepSeek Flash credential scopes participated.

- 12/12 structured conclusions completed;
- 0 Provider/equipment failures;
- no H1–H3 human stimulus was sent to the Provider.

Descriptive preflight consequences were:

| Variant | Factual adaptation | Factual comprehension | Factual perception | Unsupported inferences | Epistemic decision accuracy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| explicit-chain | 100% | 100% | 100% | 0 | 100% |
| fragmented | 75% | 50% | 0% | 9 | 62.5% |
| evidence-delayed | 100% | 100% | 0% | 0 | 100% |

These are **surrogate apparatus results**, not human-response evidence. They show why the consequence split matters: the delayed condition can be perfectly disciplined while failing factual perception because the trigger fact is not exposed, and the fragmented condition can reach factual answers while making unsupported assertions.

## Human pilot readiness

A private owner-pilot packet has been generated with three encounters and 18 total questions. It contains no oracle, seed, required-evidence hints, or alternative-variant text, and is stored with mode `0600`.

No human answer has been collected.

The project owner has already seen the experiment hypothesis and variant-family discussion. Therefore that packet is permanently classified **non-naïve**. If used, it can test instructions, interaction burden, response capture, and scoring, but it cannot become blinded observer-class effect evidence. One person also remains one evaluator cluster regardless of the number of questions answered.

A later naïve human study requires actual independent participants plus whatever consent/review is appropriate for the real jurisdiction, institution, risk, and intended publication/deployment. HR0 does not invent those facts in advance.

## World-model update

HR0 adds one useful causal distinction without adding a new service:

```text
encounter occurred
≠ observer had relevant evidence
≠ observer selected the factual answer
≠ observer was justified by the visible evidence
≠ observer knew that their answer was justified
≠ population-level human effect
```

This is the human-facing version of the same Ordivon law already seen elsewhere: **proof, meaning, authority, and consequence stay scoped.**

## Rejected in HR0

- rebuilding Web's archived preference apparatus as an always-on platform;
- creating a generic participant or analytics database;
- adding diagram/prose/reveal/interaction changes before isolating observer class;
- treating the project owner's informed pilot as naïve human evidence;
- treating repeated questions from one person as independent participants;
- publishing identifiable/raw participant data into Git;
- repairing the v1 scorer silently after seeing Provider output;
- regenerating sealed H1–H3 merely because v1 measurement was wrong;
- collapsing factual correctness and evidential justification into one score.

## Next pressure

The apparatus is now ready for two materially different continuations:

1. **non-naïve owner pilot** — cheap end-to-end calibration of the human interaction/response/scoring path; it can expose friction but cannot establish a human treatment effect;
2. **naïve independent-observer study** — only this can begin addressing whether the R6 reveal-order consequence transfers to humans. Population inference still requires a new sampling/analysis preregistration.

Diagram versus prose, delayed recall, trust, preference, and interactive disclosure remain later experiments. They should open only when a concrete human consequence justifies changing another dimension.
