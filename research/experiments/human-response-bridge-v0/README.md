# HR0 Human-Response Bridge

## Question

Studio R6 showed that the same facts, exposed in different reveal orders, changed one **Agent-observer** consequence. That result deliberately stopped before a human claim. HR0 asks the next smaller question:

> Can the R6 same-fact reveal-order intervention be transferred to a human observer without changing the factual mechanism, task family, initial-viewport evidence rule, or owner boundaries?

HR0 is an observer-class bridge, not a search for the best explanation format. It does **not** compare diagrams, interactive disclosure, prose styles, delayed recall, trust, preference, or aesthetics. Those are separate interventions or outcomes and are excluded until this bridge establishes that a human-response measurement is worth further work.

## Owner split

- **Computing** owns this cross-project preregistration, synthesis, and disposition.
- **Studio** owns the inherited R6 intervention semantics, fictitious mechanism family, questions, oracle, and typed consequence distinction.
- **Web** owns exact browser assignment/exposure and initial-viewport evidence when rendering is used for apparatus verification.
- **Harness** owns surrogate Provider execution; Studio and Computing do not gain a new secret-loading path.
- **Human** owns the methodological/privacy boundary for any actual participant evidence.

No `HumanResponseService`, participant database, analytics platform, universal survey schema, or permanent experimentation backend is introduced.

## Phases

### HR0-A — apparatus preflight

Before any human response:

1. freeze the contract;
2. generate a sealed novel human stimulus set and publish only commitments;
3. mechanically verify equal full fact sets and the inherited reveal-order transforms;
4. verify browser initial-viewport evidence on separate preflight mechanisms;
5. use Harness on those **preflight-only** mechanisms to catch broken or ambiguous task wiring.

Surrogate results cannot select, rewrite, or tune the sealed human mechanisms after their commitment.

### HR0-B — human pilot

A participant receives only one assigned variant of each novel mechanism and the same bounded questions. Responses are stored privately and minimally. The first participant may be used to test apparatus usability. A participant who already knows the hypothesis or variant identity is explicitly **non-naïve** and contributes only apparatus/local-calibration evidence.

HR0 makes no population-level human claim regardless of pilot count. A future inferential study requires a new preregistration with an independent-evaluator sampling and analysis plan.

## Outcomes

Primary: causal **adaptation accuracy** on the inherited R6 Q4–Q6 questions.

Secondary:

- immediate comprehension accuracy (Q2–Q3);
- perception accuracy (Q1);
- per-question confidence as descriptive confidence-calibration evidence.

Guardrail: choosing a substantive answer when the assigned initial-viewport evidence does not establish it counts as an unsupported inference.

These outcomes remain separate. HR0 does not average them into one human-understanding score.

## Explicit exclusions

- delayed recall — changes the time dimension and belongs to a later experiment;
- causal diagram versus prose — changes representation as well as observer class;
- preference / trust / affect — different human consequences;
- population inference from one person or repeated questions;
- identifiable participant data in Git;
- mandatory human approval for ordinary Studio/Web work.

## Human evidence boundary

The public repository stores protocol, commitments, aggregate/mechanical receipts, and bounded conclusions only. It does not store names, emails, IP addresses, demographic profiles, private free text, or raw identifiable participant records. Formal research or external recruitment must separately satisfy the consent/review requirements of its actual jurisdiction, institution, risk, and publication/deployment plan.

## Reopen conditions

Broader human-response infrastructure is justified only if repeated real experiments require a shared contract that owner-local files plus Runtime/Host continuity cannot express. Diagram, delayed-memory, trust, preference, or interaction treatments should open only after a named unresolved human consequence survives HR0 or another real production decision.
