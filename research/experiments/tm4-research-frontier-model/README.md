# TM4 — Research Frontier Model Closeout

TM4 is the assimilation closeout for TM0→TM3. It introduces no new live mechanism.

The result of the mainline is a narrower model of scientific research taste:

> **Research taste is a learned, revisable prior over which research operator policy is likely to convert the current evidence topology into trusted frontier-changing evidence within the current budget, authority, and recovery constraints.**

This is not a new primitive, law, scheduler, Skill registry, or universal score. It is an Agent-held / research-held Representation about research Dynamics.

TM4 records:

- the prospective calibration trajectory from TM1–TM3;
- supported evidence-topology/operator relations;
- still-unvalidated topology classes;
- the remaining stopping/value-of-information gap;
- a minimal `ResearchBet` record for future prospective calibration.

No change to `core/foundations.md` or the canonical Agent-First Research Method is made in this closeout. A future ordinary research campaign must first demonstrate that the compact frontier representation improves real work when used prospectively rather than as another prompt layer.
