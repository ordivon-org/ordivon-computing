## Pass-A anti-case policy

HD3 intentionally includes cases where narrow specialization, serendipity, null results, local-only systems or initially wrong interpretations nevertheless produced major value. These cases are frozen as historical sampling pressure before any theory verdict is visible; their purpose is to test whether later theory over-rewards generality, deliberate planning, metaproductivity, or clean model–reality separation.
