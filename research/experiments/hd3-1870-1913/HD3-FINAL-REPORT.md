HD3 completes the third chronological out-of-distribution wave of the historical RSI/world-model campaign.

## Deep-Anchor findings

### A01 — electric power as a system

Generation technology, distribution architecture, load type, transmission distance, motor capability, finance and installed infrastructure jointly select the viable system. Pearl Street local DC can be genuinely useful while Niagara-scale AC solves a materially different world. Local success does not imply universal architecture.

### A02 — telephone network

A speech transducer is not yet a switched network. Transmitter quality, exchange/switching, line engineering, operating practice and long-distance infrastructure are complements. Priority is distributed and later network narratives compress component and organizational invention.

### A03 — wireless telegraphy

Hertzian-wave evidence did not itself create transatlantic service. Detectors, tuning, antennas, power, operating practice, finance and system integration mattered. Rival routes remained live; first long-range signal did not instantly establish the later radio architecture.

### A04 — automobile plurality

Steam, electric and internal-combustion vehicles were all real routes with different infrastructure, range, control and operating-cost worlds. A better abstract technology did not dominate independently of roads, fueling/charging, reliability and use case.

### A05 — Wright/Langley flight research

Inherited aerodynamic tables failed to predict the Wright gliders. The productive move was to improve observability—build measurement apparatus, generate local aerodynamic data and then revalidate at full-scale glider conditions before adding power. Langley's successful powered models followed by full-scale crashes provide a direct scale/generalization control.

### A06 — Koch bacteriology

Pure culture, staining/microscopy, animal transmission and reproducible pathogen isolation formed an experimental system. The method increased causal discriminability, but no single postulate wording or one laboratory representation should be projected as a universal ontology.

### A07 — diphtheria antitoxin

Serum therapy required potency standardization, manufacturing, safety, distribution and later regulation. Biological activity, product consistency and institutional safety are separable. A working therapy can reveal a new safety frontier only after diffusion.

### A08 — X-rays

Serendipitous observation and useful medical intervention preceded a mature physical mechanism and safety model. This is a frozen anti-case against any rule that useful local action must wait for complete ontology. It simultaneously demonstrates why first usefulness is not net or lifecycle validation.

### A09 — Michelson–Morley

A high-quality null result remained stable while its theoretical role changed across ether-repair and later kinematic frameworks. Observation and evaluator interpretation have different histories. The experimenters can produce excellent evidence without assigning its eventual semantic meaning.

### A10 — Haber–Bosch

Laboratory chemical closure did not make industrial scale-up an implementation chore. Hot-hydrogen metallurgy, pressure equipment, catalyst abundance/poisoning, purification, heat/recycle loops and continuous-operation reliability created a new research frontier. Scale itself changed the bottleneck topology.

### A11 — regression/correlation/biometrics

Statistical representations can open new quantitative comparisons while also becoming reified beyond what their assumptions justify. A useful metric is neither raw reality nor automatically causal explanation.

### A12 — Ford production system

Moving flow plus specialization is deliberately retained as an anti-case to vague preference for generality/flexibility. A strong flexible stationary baseline can lose badly on a stable standardized high-volume objective. The gain remains scoped: throughput can improve while worker experience, adaptability and turnover worsen.

## Anti-case audit

### X-rays

Useful local intervention can precede complete causal ontology and mature safety. This narrows over-strong prerequisites for action while preserving the distinction between local usefulness and net/general success.

### Michelson–Morley

Evidence value and evaluator interpretation diverge. A durable observation can survive multiple explanatory world models.

### Ford specialization

The independent coder's raw `S4=CONTRADICTS` is rejected as a quantifier/scope error. A fixed method can be optimal inside one stable regime. S4 should forbid unconditional extension across changing conditions, not require constant operator switching.

Ford is still a real anti-case against treating flexible/general/metaproductive methods as intrinsically superior.

### Koch tuberculin

A major true causal discovery does not transfer authority to a different consequence domain. Bacteriological expertise did not validate a human therapeutic claim.

### Wegener

Present missing mechanism/non-tractability does not imply zero future option value. Later evidentiary change also cannot be projected backward as present proof.

No anti-case establishes an adjudicated direct contradiction. This keeps falsifiability pressure active.

## What HD3 changes in the RSI world model

No frozen law is rewritten. Seven operational conclusions strengthen:

1. **Reality recurrence and classifier recurrence are different.** R1–R8 cross a three-epoch threshold while exact S-label classification remains imperfect.
2. **Anti-cases should narrow quantifiers, not manufacture contradictions.** Ford breaks an S1 coding ceiling and narrows S4 without forcing false theory rejection.
3. **A stable regime can favor a fixed specialized operator.** Conditional policy is about world/regime dependence, not perpetual switching.
4. **Scale-up can create a new research frontier.** Laboratory closure does not imply industrial closure.
5. **Evidence can outlive its evaluator.** Observation quality and current interpretation are separable.
6. **World affordance and historical epistemic state are different boundaries.** An action can be physically admissible while the model's reasoning leaks future knowledge.
7. **Structured evidence has its own validity boundary.** Process success and semantic readability do not imply schema-conformant evidence.

A useful current decomposition is:

```text
Research competence
  = proposal quality
  + selection quality
  + World action availability/admissibility
  + epistemic/frontier cleanliness
  + evidence-structure validity
  + later outcome verification
```

These terms are not a scalar score and can fail independently.
