## Geographic / institutional profile

The epoch remains Europe/United-States heavy, reflecting the surviving institutional/industrial source base and the selected transformations. To prevent those systems from treating the rest of the world as exogenous background, the roster explicitly includes:

- plague bacteriology in Hong Kong and vaccination in Bombay/India;
- yellow-fever experiments in Cuba and vector-control infrastructure in Panama;
- global submarine telegraph operations;
- international electrical, time/meridian and radiotelegraph coordination;
- global medical X-ray diffusion and radiation injury;
- cross-national Hall–Héroult and Tesla–Ferraris parallel invention.

No geographic quota is imposed. The source lock must keep colonial authority, local populations and coercive/public-health consequences inside the causal boundary where historically material.

## Frozen anti-cases

HD3 deliberately freezes five cases whose historical shape could cut against an over-generalized modern research doctrine. Their role is not to prove any theory wrong in advance; it is to ensure Pass B cannot avoid them after seeing outcomes.

1. **HD3-A08 — X-rays:** a serendipitous observation produced enormous useful capability before the physical ontology and safety model were mature. This attacks the assumption that high-value discovery must begin from a correctly framed discriminating question.
2. **HD3-A09 — Michelson–Morley:** a high-quality null result was interpreted by the experimenters as a failed ether-detection experiment; later theory changed the meaning of the same observation. This attacks simple equations between evaluator interpretation and evidence value.
3. **HD3-A12 — Ford moving assembly:** extreme specialization and reduced local worker/task flexibility can dominate a more general craft-production baseline on the declared production objective. This attacks any unqualified preference for generality/metaproductivity.
4. **HD3-F03 — Koch tuberculin:** exceptional validated success in bacteriology did not transfer authority to a therapeutic claim. This attacks hero/expert authority transfer and tests whether a true world model in one domain can still create overreach nearby.
5. **HD3-D02 — continental drift:** weak immediate closure and missing mechanism can coexist with large future explanatory value. This attacks any stopping rule that equates present non-tractability with zero future option value.

## Replay freeze

Four Deep Anchors are fixed before source lock as historical prospective-replay cases:

- `HD3-A05` — flight research and scale-up;
- `HD3-A09` — ether-null / relativity frontier;
- `HD3-A10` — Haber laboratory synthesis → Bosch industrial scale-up;
- `HD3-A12` — mass-production specialization.

HD3 inherits the HD2 three-treatment distinction:

```text
curated live routes      → selection competence
no live routes           → proposal competence + future-tool leakage risk
no live routes + exact historical affordance manifest
                         → world-bounded proposal competence
```

Effective capability compliance will be externally adjudicated; model self-attestation is not acceptance evidence.

## Anti-confirmation constraints

During Pass A:

- source collectors may not use S1–S10/F1–F7 labels;
- source collectors may not use HD1/HD2 R1–R8 residual names;
- source collectors may not use HD2-only residual labels;
- serious local/specialized routes must not be rewritten as defective merely because a broader route later wins;
- accidental discoveries must not be backfilled with a fictional prior research plan;
- null/failed experiments retain the experimenter's contemporary interpretation separately from later historical meaning;
- institutional/economic success is not treated as proof of scientific correctness;
- retrospective future value does not retroactively make a declared-objective failure successful.

## Verdict

The 40-case roster and five anti-case pressures are accepted and frozen for source lock. No sample mutation is permitted after theory coding begins except if a case fails the historical source contract or proves causally duplicative with another frozen trajectory.
