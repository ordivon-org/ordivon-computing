## Mechanism observations

- **MO-M01 Permissive Exploration** — Weak/UNKNOWN project, Foundation or product standing can coexist with bounded search, rival construction and falsification when the investigation's own consequence authority is satisfied. Limit: exploration can itself be high-consequence; ethical/external/resource authority is independent.
- **MO-M02 Problem / Representation Challenge** — Question identity, candidate basis, evaluator/search space and owner decomposition can themselves be wrong and can be revised after evidence. Limit: most work occurs under stable frames; reframing is a minority mode.
- **MO-M03 Falsification + Typed Negative Knowledge** — Failed branches, falsifiers, absorbed novelty claims and superseded mechanisms can retain durable why-not/provenance/reopen value. Limit: preserved but undiscoverable material still causes rediscovery; raw bytes may be inadmissible to retain.
- **MO-M04 Typed Standing + Promotion** — Usefulness, ownerhood, Foundation status, route currentness, novelty, engineering need and effect authority repeatedly diverge; content can survive at lower standing after higher-standing failure. Limit: standing can itself be stale, wrong, undiscoverable or overbroad.
- **MO-M05 Standing-Mediated Commitment** — Stronger semantic/engineering/effect commitments are often withheld until contemporaneous standing supports them, while weaker exploration continues. Limit: does not prevent implementation bugs; explicit standing can overauthorize.
- **MO-M06 Human–Agent Joint Judgment** — Human steering is load-bearing in sequence/intent corrections, but Human-Agent agreement is not truth and can itself overcommit. Limit: contribution shares are difficult to reconstruct without private reasoning.
- **MO-M07 Durable Memory / Currentness / Recovery Separation** — Historical survival, current authority, recovery ability and live capability are distinct; continuity can remain true after semantic currentness has moved. Limit: owner-unknown discovery remains incomplete; zero-trace loss is censored.
- **MO-M08 Engineering / Effect Consumption Boundary** — Supported research does not automatically authorize refactor, deployment, trade, deletion or other external effects; NO_CHANGE is a valid consumption outcome. Limit: overly narrow gates can suppress fragile options; cheap reversible prototyping still needs room.
- **MO-M09 Verification + Recoverability** — Mechanical mutation errors are a distinct failure family; tests, exact state fences, receipts, reconciliation and snapshots reduce repair uncertainty. Limit: recoverability is not universally desirable.
- **MO-M10 Survivorship + Workload Selection** — Memorable surviving correction stories overstate prevalence; balanced/fixed-window sampling shows stable-frame work dominates and zero-trace loss is underobservable. Limit: selection bias does not explain every within-episode demotion/currentness effect.

Stable observations:

```text
Exploration breadth != promotion authority
Evidence != result != usefulness != currentness != ownerhood != engineering/effect authority
Negative != useless
Historical continuity != semantic currentness
Mechanical correctness != semantic standing
No-op / defer / demote / derived-only can be progress
```

## Current interaction map

- M01 ENABLES M02: low-cost inquiry keeps alternative framings alive before durable promotion.
- M02 EXPOSES representation failure.
- M03 COMPENSATES_FOR anti-rediscovery failure only when future discovery paths exist.
- M04 INHIBITS premature promotion.
- M05 DEPENDS_ON M04 and INHIBITS silent escalation; it can still fail without mechanical correctness.
- M06 can still fail through explicit overcommitment.
- M07 COMPENSATES_FOR stale continuity and EXPOSES owner-unknown discovery failure.
- M08 INHIBITS explicit overcommitment by separating supported research from irreversible effects.
- M09 COMPENSATES_FOR implementation error.
- M10 CONFOUNDS naive prevalence claims about all other mechanisms/failures.

No mechanism above is claimed sufficient for research quality, and no interaction is a required execution sequence.
