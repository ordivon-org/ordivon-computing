## Candidate mechanism — Challengeable Environmentalization

Observed mechanism candidate, not workflow rule or architecture:

A durable/current Standing sometimes earns **consumer-relative constitutive placement**: repeated reconstruction burden or downstream consequence justifies compiling the smallest useful part of that standing into a concrete future consumer's starting conditions — for example a default, routine, first-look, semantic surface, guard, Tool visibility or responsibility placement. This is distinct from saying the standing is true, important, promoted, current or engineering-actionable.

The mechanism is selective. `KEEP EXPLICIT`, `KNOWLEDGE ONLY`, `NO_DEFAULT`, demotion or `NO_CHANGE` are valid when no concrete consumer burden/consequence earns a default. Placement preserves owner-native truth, currentness and authority; a default does not mint stronger authority. Material changes in capability, owner/currentness, consumer, hidden dependency or observed consequence can challenge, narrow, rebind, demote or remove the placement without rewriting historical validity.

Broad organizational memory/learning/routines, infrastructure, distributed cognition and function-allocation theory are mature external solution content. This candidate claims only a recurrent Ordivon methodology observation under current owner/currentness/authority boundaries.
