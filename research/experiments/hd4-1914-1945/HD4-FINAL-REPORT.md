## Anti-case results

### Coarse can be frontier-optimal
Chain Home is a direct counterexample to prestige-by-sophistication. Long wavelength, fixed infrastructure and coarse resolution were not globally superior, but they were deployable quickly and useful once coupled to reporting, plotting and fighter control. A later cavity magnetron changed the feasible action space. More precision was not automatically the right current bet.

### Predictive convergence does not require one ontology
Matrix and wave mechanics converged on shared predictive content while mathematical representation and interpretation remained plural. One representation need not defeat all others for a scientific framework to become productive.

### Useful intervention can precede molecular closure
Insulin became life-saving before molecular structure and modern pharmacology were known. The missing pieces were not optional: purification, biological potency standards, manufacturing and dose control converted local effect into reproducible therapy.

### Generality/autonomy are not prerequisites for value
Bombe/Colossus/ENIAC-era computation was narrow, manually configured and embedded in human workflows. High operational and metaproductive value did not require autonomous hypothesis generation or one universal architecture.

### Correct formulation is not present tractability
Richardson's numerical weather prediction could contain a productive mathematical formulation while remaining operationally useless because computation, initialization, filtering/balance and error structure were missing. Correctness, tractability and decision value are separate.

No anti-case yields an adjudicated direct contradiction to the frozen S1–S10. Four epochs without one remains a falsifiability pressure, not proof of universality.

## World-model update

HD4 strengthens six operational distinctions:

- sophistication/generalization is not automatically current frontier value;
- time-to-decision/time-to-effect can be part of the objective itself;
- a scientifically correct representation can still be presently intractable;
- a useful intervention can precede mature ontology without removing the need for safety/production/standardization evidence;
- proposal competence can coexist with outcome-memory leakage, and explicit World affordances can reduce that leakage without choosing the route for the Agent;
- semantic readability and admissible structured evidence are distinct evidence states.
