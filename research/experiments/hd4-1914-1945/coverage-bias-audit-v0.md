# HD4 Coverage / Bias / Anti-case Audit — pre-source-lock

## Mechanical result

- discovery pool: **120 unique candidates (C001–C120)**;
- frozen roster: **40 trajectories** in the exact HD0 composition;
- Deep Anchors: **12**; replay candidates: **4** (`A03`, `A05`, `A07`, `A09`);
- anti-cases frozen before theory coding: **5**;
- no candidate ID is reused across frozen trajectories.

## Contraction decisions

The first-pass seed set was heavily tempted toward physics, weapons and Anglo-American wartime winners. The frozen roster deliberately contracts that bias by retaining agriculture (hybrid corn, Lysenko), public-health/drug production, quality/statistics, food logistics, standards, environmental/occupational harm, delayed theories and failed infrastructure. War deployment is treated as one high-pressure validation environment rather than a universal success label.

The roster also separates nominal discoveries from complements: insulin treatment is separated from insulin standardization; penicillin discovery/clinical validation is separated from fermentation-scale complement; radar network operation is separated from magnetron/Tizard transfer; nuclear proof-of-concept is separated from industrial material production.

## Geographic reading

The period is structurally concentrated in Europe and North America because much of the selected scientific-industrial archive is there, but the pool includes Soviet, international, African/South-American public-health, and transnational standards/material-supply trajectories. HD4 does not force geographic equality that the source universe cannot support. Colonial/resource and wartime power asymmetries must remain visible in source-native consequence fields rather than being treated as neutral diffusion.

## Winner-bias controls

Explicit controls include Richardson numerical weather prediction, rigid airships, Lysenkoism, delayed macromolecular theory, delayed continental drift, tetraethyl lead and radium-dial harms. Parallel invention is represented through turbojets and electronic television. Anti-cases are frozen before Pass B so they cannot be invented after seeing theory scores.

## Replay choice

A03 radar, A05 fission/chain reaction, A07 penicillin and A09 cryptanalysis/computation were selected because each contains a real pre-outcome decision frontier with multiple materially different routes and complements. They also differ in domain, observability, scale and institutional form. De-identification cannot eliminate pretrained historical memory; future-theory/identity leakage is an explicit validity dimension.

## Gate

Roster freeze authorizes source collection only. No S1–S10/F1–F7 verdict, residual-cluster mapping or promotion decision is permitted until all 40 trajectories are source locked.
