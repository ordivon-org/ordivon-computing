# EX3–EX7 — Causal comprehension under action pressure

This programme starts where EX2 stopped. EX2 rejected both a universal five-axis record and a closed whole-family relation ontology. EX3–EX7 therefore ask whether any additional explanation structure earns existence by changing **decisions and counterfactual predictions**, not by making a taxonomy look cleaner.

Five independent surfaces are frozen before the first live call: A=EX3 failure discovery, B=EX4 representation ablation, C=EX5 intervention/deletion, D=EX6 de-identified transfer, E=EX7 final compression. The owner-native fact basis remains external to Computing; these corpora are experiment-local oracles and do not become product authority.

The experiment is allowed to end with deletion. If strong compact prose is already at ceiling, or if extra structure does not survive intervention and drift, EX7 must contract rather than invent a richer schema.
