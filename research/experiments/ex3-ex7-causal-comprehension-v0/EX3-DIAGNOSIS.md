# EX3 diagnosis — explanation-failure discovery

EX3 asked a deliberately prior question before testing richer representation: after current owner-native causal boundaries are already expressed in strong compact natural language, do fresh Agents still make materially wrong actions often enough to justify another explanation structure?

The preregistered A surface covered 17 responsibility families spanning external-effect occurrence, semantic owner versus persistence carrier, Run versus semantic completion, provider continuation versus cognition, message delivery versus knowledge, historical versus current state, Finance assurance and credential identity, context/search non-monotonicity, consequence ordering, durable versus physical currentness, Human framework authority, observer class, encounter versus comprehension, projection versus authority, classical delegation, and capability versus permission.

Formal result: **102/102 exact actions, 100%, with zero critical unsafe errors, zero unresolved trial failures, 69,891 Provider tokens, and 102 physical Provider calls.** Every family was 6/6.

The preregistered explanation-deficit gate required either overall compact accuracy below 97.5% or a repeated critical unsafe choice in at least 2/6 replicates for any family. Neither occurred. EX3 therefore **falsifies the measurable explanation-deficit hypothesis on this pressure set**.

This is not evidence that explanation never fails. It means a new mandatory representation cannot justify itself by claiming that already-correct compact owner-native prose is insufficient for these action boundaries. Any richer candidate now bears the burden of proving value on a different consequence class such as intervention, deletion, drift transfer, or human comprehension.

Disposition: **CEILING / NO DEFICIT FOUND.** Do not manufacture harder labels or weaken the baseline to create room for an ontology.
