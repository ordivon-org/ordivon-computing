# EX4 diagnosis — representation ceiling ablation

Because EX3 found no baseline deficit, EX4 could no longer be interpreted as a repair experiment. Its valid role became a ceiling ablation: on a separately frozen B surface, does any richer representation change action correctness enough to earn mandatory existence, or does it merely consume more context?

Four treatments were frozen before EX3 live calls:

- strong compact responsibility prose;
- compact prose plus the exact seven-question responsibility scaffold;
- compact prose plus typed relation notation;
- a compact causal card.

Formal result: **all four treatments scored 102/102 exact actions with zero critical unsafe errors.** There were 408 accepted decisions, 408 physical Provider calls, zero unresolved failures, and 293,385 Provider tokens in total.

Token burden for the same perfect action result:

- compact prose: 70,620 tokens;
- causal card: 72,031 tokens (+2.0% versus compact);
- typed relation notation: 74,291 tokens (+5.2%);
- exact-question scaffold: 76,443 tokens (+8.2%).

The preregistered EX4 promotion rule required a >=3 percentage-point gain on EX3-deficit families, elimination of repeated critical errors, and <=1 point overall regression. EX3 produced no deficit families, so no treatment can satisfy the improvement limb. Perfect ties therefore do not establish representation benefit.

Disposition: **CEILING_EQUIVALENT; COMPACT PROSE DOMINATES ON BURDEN.** Typed relations, question scaffolds, and causal cards may remain experiment-local reasoning aids, but EX4 provides no evidence for mandatory Agent-facing serialization, ontology, or service. EX5 must test whether intervention/deletion counterfactuals expose value that direct action selection did not.
