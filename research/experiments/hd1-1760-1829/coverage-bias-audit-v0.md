# HD1 Coverage and Bias Audit — roster freeze

## Expansion before contraction

HD1 first expanded to **105 candidate trajectories**. The pool intentionally included precursors, rivals, diffusion systems, standards, failed routes, delayed discoveries and consequences. Closely coupled candidates were then merged when they were parts of one causal trajectory instead of being counted as separate famous inventions.

The frozen roster contains exactly 40 trajectories and satisfies the HD0 composition contract: 12 deep Anchors, 16 breadth/transformative, 3 failed/abandoned, 2 parallel, 2 delayed/dormant, 2 standards/infrastructure, 2 safety/externality corrections and 1 under-recognized complement.

## Geography

HD1 is unavoidably concentrated in Britain and continental Europe because the sampled epoch is the first industrial revolution, but the roster deliberately prevents an all-British heroic narrative. It includes France, Italy, Denmark, the United States, India and explicitly transregional knowledge/effect trajectories.

Four cases are especially important anti-Western/anti-hero controls:

- `HD1-A07` begins with variolation practices documented across Asia, India, parts of Africa and the Ottoman world before Jennerian vaccination;
- `HD1-A12` treats the cotton gin inside an Atlantic cotton/slavery system rather than as an isolated American machine;
- `HD1-B14` begins with Andean cinchona knowledge before French alkaloid isolation;
- `HD1-B16` puts Indian textile production and world-market competition inside the same industrial-revolution reality rather than treating Britain as a closed world.

No numeric equality quota is forced on 1760–1829. The full 320-case study retains the HD0 post-1900/post-1945 geographic constraints.

## Winner-history controls

The roster contains explicit non-winners:

- Fairmount steam pumping is retained because the city abandoned the high-tech steam route for waterwheels after explosions and high operating cost;
- early steamboat routes are retained separately from Fulton's later durable service;
- Babbage's 1820s Difference Engine is retained as an ambitious incomplete integration effort, not rewritten as a completed computer;
- Avogadro and Cayley test delayed tractability rather than immediate success;
- early factory legislation tests rules without adequate enforcement;
- the Davy safety lamp tests success that exists only relative to a prior lethal operating world.

## Hero-inventor compression controls

Several famous names were deliberately merged into distributed trajectories:

- Watt is analyzed with Newcomen, Boulton, machine-tool precision, customer economics and factory adoption;
- textile mechanization spans Hargreaves/Arkwright/Crompton and factory organization;
- electrochemistry spans Galvani, Volta, Nicholson/Carlisle and Davy;
- vaccination begins before Jenner and continues into diffusion logistics;
- railways span high-pressure steam, adhesion experiments, infrastructure and competitive trials;
- William Smith is paired with the Greenough rival map and source/field constraints.

## Source-selection bias

Nobel does not cover this epoch and is not used. IEEE/ASME/Science Museum/Smithsonian landmark collections are used as candidate indexes, not a population. Deep Anchors must still satisfy HD0's independent-source bundle. Quantitative economic-history sources are used to expose diffusion, labor, trade and complement effects that museum object histories often omit.

## Why these 12 Anchors

Anchor status was assigned for **structural discriminability plus source richness**, not fame. Together the 12 expose:

- efficiency versus diffusion/complements;
- machine versus production system;
- observation/instrument versus theory;
- standard versus realized interoperability;
- local technical success versus global social consequence;
- rival methods and evaluator design;
- delayed diffusion and institutional disruption;
- metaproductive infrastructure.

Four provisional replay candidates are `A03` precision machine tools, `A05` electrochemistry, `A08` longitude and `A09` geological mapping. `A11` Leblanc is held as the first alternate because it is structurally rich but easier to identify from its unusual soda-ash context.

## Freeze rule

The roster is now frozen before any S1–S10/F1–F7 coding. A later substitution is admissible only if a case cannot satisfy the historical source contract or if the title accidentally duplicates another trajectory's causal unit. Substitution must preserve the composition bucket and record the exact reason; it cannot be made because a case gives an inconvenient theoretical result.
