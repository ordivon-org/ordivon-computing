# KiCad Target Standing Adapter v0.1

Projects provider-observed and source-derived standing needed by a bounded contract/probe research question:
- electrical ERC standing;
- target-node net membership and exact current net name;
- source-level target pin coordinate candidates and directly incident wire witnesses where derivable.

It deliberately does **not** propose a mutation, label, operator family, owner judgement or discriminator. Its purpose is to prevent an Agent from repeatedly spending open-ended cognition reconstructing deterministic KiCad representation/currentness facts before doing the actual research task.

The adapter is fixture-scoped v0.1; geometric pin extraction is not yet a universal KiCad parser and must remain explicitly bounded by independent provider/netlist verification.
