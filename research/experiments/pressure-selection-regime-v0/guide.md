# Compact Pressure-Selection Guide v0

Use this only as a non-authoritative reasoning aid. It does not select for you.

1. **Hard admission first.** A probe/action that lacks required authority, safety, currentness, evaluator validity or consequence/recovery boundary cannot become preferred merely because it is informative.
2. **Name the target consequence of learning.** Prefer evidence that can change the target decision, standing, ownership placement, admissible frontier or future experiment cost; entropy reduction about irrelevant variables is not automatically valuable.
3. **Match criterion to uncertainty geometry.**
   - Grounded probabilities + decision utility: Value of Information / EVSI / net sampling benefit may apply.
   - Grounded probabilistic model + inference/QoI target: Bayesian/OED or expected information gain may apply; target the relevant quantity of interest.
   - Finite live hypotheses but no justified probabilities: use discriminating partitions, counterexamples, worst-case elimination or set-valued diagnosis rather than inventing priors.
   - Repeated online action with regret/exploration tradeoff: bandit/information-directed criteria may apply.
   - Internal computation/search step: value-of-computation/metareasoning may apply.
4. **Closed-loop cost is a vector.** Consider execution, verification, delay, material, human, recovery, effect risk and opportunity cost. Do not force them into one universal scalar unless the owner/mission supplies a justified utility model.
5. **Preserve option value and reversibility.** A slightly less informative reversible probe can dominate an irreversible or authority-expanding one.
6. **Stop is an action.** If no admissible observation can change the current decision enough to justify its cost, or evaluator/infrastructure instability dominates candidate differences, lawful abstention / repair / wait-for-natural-pressure can be the best next move.
