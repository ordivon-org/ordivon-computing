# HD2 Coverage / Bias Audit — 1830–1869

## Freeze boundary

This audit occurred after expansion to 120 candidates and before any S1–S10/F1–F7 coding or HD1 residual mapping. The first draft roster was mechanically audited and then contracted once before source lock.

## Composition

The final frozen roster satisfies the HD0 composition contract exactly:

| Deep Anchors | 12 |

The 40 trajectories draw on 94 distinct candidate IDs. No candidate ID is counted in more than one frozen trajectory.

## First-draft bias found and corrected

The first 12-Anchor draft put four Deep Anchors in `biology_medicine_and_public_health` and none in agriculture. That would over-weight high-consequence medical cases with unusually explicit evaluators and interventions.

Before source lock:

- the combined Semmelweis→Lister trajectory was removed from Deep Anchors;
- Semmelweis became a delayed-adoption control;
- Lister became a safety-correction control;
- Mendel retained the second delayed slot;
- Lawes→superphosphate→Rothamsted long-term field experimentation became Deep Anchor A09;
- the vacated breadth slot became the Peruvian guano/global fertilizer trajectory.

This was a sampling correction based on coverage, not a response to theory results.

The full 320-case campaign, not each epoch, owns final domain coverage.

## Winner / failure control

The roster does not isolate only mature winners. It includes:

- South Devon atmospheric railway — operating deployment abandoned after coupled sealing/maintenance problems;
- Babbage Analytical Engine — rich design without a completed operational system;
- Great Eastern — exceptional technical scale without a sustainable original commercial model;
- Mendel and Semmelweis — evidence/ideas with delayed recognition or adoption;
- serious parallel routes in vulcanized rubber and Neptune prediction;
- railway gauge conflict before standard constraint;
- safety/regulatory cases where written rules or technical corrections must still acquire effective consequence.

Major Deep Anchors also contain their own failures/rivals, especially the Atlantic cable, Bessemer steel, photography, anesthesia and cholera/public-health trajectories.

## Geographic / institutional control

HD2 remains historically Europe/North-America heavy, but the roster deliberately includes world systems that change the causal boundary:

- Indian railway and Indo-European telegraph infrastructure;
- U.S. transcontinental railroad with Chinese/Irish migrant labor rather than railway machinery alone;
- Peruvian guano and global fertilizer demand;
- Atlantic submarine communication as a multinational ocean system;
- Darwin/Wallace evidence gathered through global fieldwork;
- gutta-percha as a Southeast Asian material substrate for European/American cable systems.

No equal geographic quota is imposed for this epoch. These cases prevent European invention narratives from silently treating colonial/material/labor worlds as exogenous background.

## Institution / standard control

The roster contains explicit institutions instead of treating them as passive context:

- 1846 British railway gauge constraint;
- 1865 International Telegraph Union;
- patent-pool governance in sewing machines;
- Public Health Act/local sanitary governance inside A08;
- Pacific Railway Acts and financing/land authority inside A12;
- Rothamsted as durable experimental infrastructure rather than one fertilizer product.

## Replay freeze

Four Deep Anchors are frozen as historical prospective-replay candidates before outcome-oriented theory coding:

- `HD2-A02` — Atlantic cable;
- `HD2-A04` — induction→generator;
- `HD2-A06` — Bessemer steel;
- `HD2-A08` — cholera/public-health intervention.

The replay design will hide names/dates/outcomes where possible and score research-control quality separately from route-direction accuracy. Pretraining-memory leakage remains a validity threat.

## Anti-confirmation constraints

During Pass A:

- collectors may not use S1–S10/F1–F7 labels;
- collectors may not use the names of HD1 residual clusters;
- a famous outcome must retain real rivals, failures, complements and externalities;
- URL count may not be interpreted as independent evidence-lineage count;
- historical ambiguity stays ambiguous rather than being resolved for theory convenience.

## Verdict

The corrected 40-case roster is accepted for source lock. No further sample mutation is permitted unless a frozen case fails its source contract or is proven to duplicate another trajectory causally.
