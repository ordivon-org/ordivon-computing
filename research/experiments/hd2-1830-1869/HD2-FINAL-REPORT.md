# HD2 Final Report — 1830–1869 Historical Theory Dogfood

## Executive result

HD2 is complete at the research/evidence layer.

The wave expanded **120 candidate trajectories**, corrected one sampling bias before source lock, froze an exact **40-case roster**, source-locked all 40 histories, reconstructed 12 Deep Anchors, coded the frozen S1–S10/F1–F7 only after Pass A closed, ran an independent coder audit, and executed three historical replay treatments totaling 36 final live Provider calls.

The main result is more discriminating than HD1:

1. all eight post-hoc HD1 residual structures recur in independently collected HD2 history, giving them **two-epoch recurrence** but still no right to promotion;
2. S3 gains a new temporal/lifecycle boundary: first success or first service is not durable validation;
3. S5 gets several genuine non-Agent cases in information-rich institutions, while still remaining non-universal;
4. the primary coding ceiling `S1=40/40 SUPPORTS` survives an independent Deep-Anchor attack (`12/12`), making S1 unusually stable but not statistically universal;
5. independent exact coding agreement is still only **80/120 = 66.7%**, with S6 remaining badly unstable;
6. a raw independent `S3=CONTRADICTS` on Atlantic Cable was identified as a **verdict-polarity error**: a historical actor violating the guardrail supports why the guardrail exists; it does not contradict the guardrail's truth claim;
7. historical replay decomposes research competence into route selection, route proposal, historical/world affordance, and external compliance verification.

The frozen hypotheses file remains byte-identical throughout HD2.

## Deep-Anchor source-native findings

### A01 — telegraph systems

The electric telegraph was not one instrument. Cooke–Wheatstone and Morse/Vail show competing end-to-end systems in which signaling interface/code, transmitter, receiver, wire/insulation, operators, railway/public lines, finance and company administration jointly produced service. A patent or laboratory effect was not yet a network.

### A02 — Atlantic cable

The trajectory is failure-rich: laying failures, a 1858 cable that briefly communicated and then failed, a 1865 near-complete break, and the 1866 durable deployment/recovery. First message, complete physical span, weak-signal observability and durable service are different events. Cable, insulation, weak-signal receiver, payout machinery, ship and recovery practice co-evolved.

### A03 — thermodynamics

Joule's quantitative work, independent conservation arguments, Kelvin's temperature representation, Clausius' thermodynamic reformulation and Joule–Thomson refinement form a distributed theory/measurement transition. Better measurement did not merely add confidence; it made previously invisible real-gas effects observable.

### A04 — induction to dynamo

Faraday's induction discovery and Pixii-style permanent-magnet generation were not yet the later high-output machine architecture. Self-exciting dynamos used residual magnetism plus generated current to strengthen the field, turning a physical principle into a machine-level amplification route.

### A05 — photography plurality

Daguerreotype, calotype and wet collodion optimize different objectives: detail, copyability, exposure/workflow and legal diffusion. No single early process dominates every objective, and later wet collodion recombined advantages while imposing a severe immediate-processing constraint.

### A06 — Bessemer steel

Spectacular air-blown decarburization did not mean reliable steel across feedstocks. Early brittleness exposed composition/feedstock conditions; Mushet-type correction knowledge and open-hearth competition turned the problem into industrial process control. Phosphorus remained an explicit later boundary.

### A07 — anesthesia

Private early use, failed public demonstration, successful public validation, rapid reproduction, agent rivalry and delivery/dose-control engineering are distinct. Wells' failed nitrous-oxide demonstration did not prove the route dead; Morton's public ether demonstration mattered partly because it was legible/reproducible to an independent clinical community.

### A08 — cholera/public health

Useful intervention and correct causal ontology were asynchronous. Chadwick's miasmatic framework helped mobilize sanitation; Snow supplied more discriminating water evidence; the 1848 Act created incomplete/permissive authority; physical sewer/water systems translated concern into consequence. A map, pump intervention or Act alone was not the public-health system.

### A09 — Rothamsted

Lawes' soluble phosphate product and factory are separate from long-term field science. Persistent plots, treatment records and retained physical samples created an evidence substrate whose option value grew as future methods/questions appeared. Some early experiments were discontinued, showing long-lived infrastructure need not mean literal immutability.

### A10 — Darwin/Wallace

Independent convergence, priority governance, evidence compression and open-ended attack all matter. Natural selection reorganized many observations without one decisive benchmark, while missing/incorrect heredity ideas remained. Broad explanatory productivity did not validate every auxiliary claim.

### A11 — spectroscopy

A new instrument/representation opened otherwise unreachable observation: characteristic lines supported element discovery and inference about celestial composition. The instrument changed the set of empirically addressable objects, not merely the speed of an existing test.

### A12 — transcontinental rail–telegraph

The hard problem was not inventing locomotion. Federal finance/land authority, staged certification, uniform through-route requirements, telegraph coordination, physical construction and migrant labor were causal components. Machine-only history silently removes authority, labor and land from the artifact's actual production function.

### S3 adds lifecycle generalization

HD1 narrowed generalization to the declared applicability scope. HD2 adds another axis:

> a successful moment is not necessarily durable performance.

The 1858 Atlantic cable communicated before failing; the atmospheric railway operated before abandonment; storm warnings were deployed, suspended and later reinstated; Bessemer conversion visibly worked before feedstock-quality boundaries were understood.

`initial success ≠ temporal/lifecycle generalization`.

### S5 gains genuine historical cases without becoming universal

HD1 had almost no direct non-Agent support. HD2 contains stronger information-system-like cases:

- Rothamsted treatment histories plus physical sample archive;
- Nightingale decision-grade statistical records and explicit critique of unusable data collection;
- atomic-weight coordination/classification;
- international telegraph regulations and stable inter-network representations.

Yet 33/40 remain N/A in the primary coding. The result is a scoped expansion, not a universal law.

### S6 remains weakly measurable

Only 5/40 primary cases support it directly and 34 are N/A. Historical narratives often show additional complexity and later value but do not provide a strong simpler baseline with comparable cost/accounting. Great Eastern also demonstrates why objective/horizon binding matters: poor original commercial fit can coexist with large later cable-laying option value.

## Independent-coder audit

A fresh provider context received only one locked Deep Anchor plus exact frozen S1–S10. It did not see primary verdicts and was explicitly warned not to map generic analogy to S1.

Result:

```text
exact agreement:              80 / 120 = 66.7%
coarse applicability:         91 / 120 = 75.8%
provider tokens:              34,381
```

This does **not** represent monotonic measurement convergence from HD1: exact agreement improved from 55.0%, but coarse applicability fell from 79.2%.

S1 is unusually stable:

```text
primary whole roster: 40 / 40 SUPPORTS
independent Anchors:  12 / 12 SUPPORTS
```

This survives a stronger anti-metaphor attack, so S1 is now a high-confidence recurring structural pressure. But the ceiling still cannot be converted into a population frequency: the trajectory methodology itself focuses on claims, observations, representations and realized consequences and may make this distinction especially visible.

S6 remains unstable: only 3/12 exact agreement and 4/12 coarse applicability agreement.

### Verdict-polarity failure

The independent coder returned `S3=CONTRADICTS` for Atlantic Cable because short-cable success failed to generalize to Atlantic conditions.

This is a coding-polarity error. The frozen S3 is a guardrail saying an improvement must survive materially different conditions before general progress is asserted. A historical violation of that guardrail is precisely evidence for the failure mode S3/F2 describes; it does not make S3 false.

Raw output is retained, but adjudicated direct contradictions remain zero.

Therefore historical taxonomy now has at least three measurement risks:

```text
codebook elasticity
+ sampling/scaffold effects
+ verdict-polarity ambiguity
```

## Historical replay — three treatments

Four pre-registered cases were used: Atlantic cable, self-exciting generator, Bessemer steel and cholera/public health. Three replicates each.

### Treatment 1 — curated routes

The pre-registered replay supplied historically plausible live routes and asked the Agent to select/test.

```text
12 calls
60 / 60 epistemic-control points
12 / 12 route-direction hits
22,943 Provider tokens
```

All four cases selected the useful historical direction. But this is a ceilinged selection test, not proposal competence.

### Treatment 2 — unscaffolded proposal

A post-hoc ablation removed `liveRoutes`. The Agent had to invent alternatives itself.

```text
12 calls
12 / 12 core historical strategies generated
12 / 12 route-direction hits
60 / 60 epistemic-control points
9 / 12 historically admissible top routes
24,898 Provider tokens
4 schema-repair retries
```

The epistemic structures remained strong, but future-tool leakage appeared:

- two Atlantic-cable answers selected time-domain reflectometry-style fault localization;
- one Bessemer answer selected a later basic/dephosphorization route;
- one additional non-top cholera route imported modern chlorination/cluster-RCT language.

Thus:

> good epistemic operator ≠ world-admissible intervention.

### Treatment 3 — explicit historical affordance boundary

The final ablation still withheld route choices but supplied a frozen `availableCapabilities` manifest for each historical World.

```text
12 calls
12 / 12 core strategies generated
12 / 12 historically admissible top routes
10 / 12 exact capability-manifest compliance
12 / 12 model self-reported compliance
27,236 Provider tokens
```

Known future-tech leakage was removed. However two Atlantic-cable runs introduced simulated ocean-depth hydrostatic-pressure testing even though that capability was absent from the manifest.

The model's own compliance note claimed full compliance in all 12 runs.

Therefore:

> self-reported capability compliance ≠ effective capability compliance.

The strongest replay decomposition is now:

```text
Research competence
  = proposal quality
  + selection quality
  + world-state action availability/admissibility
  + external verification of actual capability use
```

The action space is part of the World, not an optional prompt decoration.

## Provider / apparatus failure evidence

HD2 produced two function-calling failures before closeout:

1. the first primary replay runner lost an incomplete run after the provider returned malformed JSON tool arguments; because per-replicate progress had not yet been durable, the incomplete run was excluded and the final 12 trials were rerun fresh;
2. the first independent-coder run durably stored A01–A04, then failed on malformed tool JSON for the next uncommitted case. After adding bounded schema repair, A01–A04 were reused exactly and only A05–A12 were sampled.

The methodological result is simple:

> required function calling ≠ valid structured output.

Durable per-trial evidence plus bounded repair is necessary if Provider schema failures must not erase or silently resample completed trials.

## What HD2 changes in the RSI world model

No frozen law is rewritten before HD9, but five operational conclusions become stronger:

### 1. Reality recurrence is now more credible than taxonomy recurrence

The same eight residual structures recur across two independently collected epochs. Exact S-label coding remains unstable. This strengthens the hypothesis that Reality has lower-dimensional recurring pressures while our current classifier remains provisional.

### 2. Generalization has at least scope and time dimensions

An improvement must be tested inside its declared scope and for the lifecycle duration implied by the claim. `works once` is not `works durably`.

### 3. Action availability is part of the World

An Agent can propose a scientifically excellent test that is physically unavailable in the current World. Historical replay made this observable through future-tool leakage.

### 4. Capability claims need external binding/verification

Even with an explicit capability manifest, model self-attestation overclaimed compliance. This recurs with Ordivon's broader rule that representation/claim/authorization cannot be collapsed into effect.

### 5. Failure can preserve option value

Research/project evaluation must bind its declared goal while keeping future reuse value as a separate dimension. A failed original objective does not imply zero future capability; future optionality also does not retroactively make the original project successful.

## HD2 disposition

HD2 is complete without changing S1–S10/F1–F7.

It hands HD3:

- eight two-epoch residuals that now deserve deliberate anti-case selection;
- three one-epoch residuals to watch without priming source collectors;

- a warning that self-reported capability compliance is not verification;
- a stronger apparatus requirement: durable per-trial evidence before long Provider experiments.
