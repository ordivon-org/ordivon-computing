# FD1 internal exemplar review

The best Ordivon writing already exists in different owners. FD1 treats these documents as empirical design evidence, not as templates.

| Exemplar | Strong move | Why it works | Do not universalize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Computing `knowledge/agents/causal-responsibility-explanation.md` | starts from the next wrong action, gives one compact causal paragraph, then negative proof boundaries | responsibility is reconstructed in ordinary language before any vocabulary | the seven diagnostic questions are not a mandatory README checklist |
| Game `docs/PRODUCT.md` | Problem → Model → Boundary → concrete Situation → Player role → Core loop → non-goals → acceptance | a new reader can predict what play means and what models may not control | product/game narrative structure is not appropriate for Runtime reference docs |
| Human `README.md` and economy report | concrete question, current answer, reader-specific paths, explicit evidence limits and reopen rule | numbers stop being a ladder; readers can enter by purpose and stop when they have enough | Human's research/report structure should not be copied into engineering owners |
| Security `docs/research-boundary.md` | opens with a decision, immediately narrows the rule with a counterexample, separates planes and evidence | the constitutional boundary is understandable without reading C1-A through C1-N | Range-specific five-plane explanation is domain-local |
| Studio `docs/review-consumption.md` | one concrete review loop, two evidence planes, one bounded revision, explicit list of what was not established | physical evidence and judgment context remain distinct while the story stays concrete | C5/C6 phase language should not lead a new Studio reader |
| Web editorial system | states communication failures, then public sequence, reading map, current boundary, and owner links | explanation and publication can be strong without stealing technical authority | public editorial questions are not a product-authority schema |

## Internal convergence

Across these documents, the recurring successful sequence is not a fixed heading list. It is a causal movement:

```text
real pressure or question
→ retained judgment / model
→ concrete journey or counterexample
→ responsibility and non-responsibility
→ current scope / evidence limit
→ reader-specific next path
```

A document may omit or reorder pieces when its reader job differs. What matters is that the reader need not reconstruct the causal boundary from research genealogy or inventories.
