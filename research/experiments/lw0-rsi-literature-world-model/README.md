# LW0 — Ordivon RSI Literature World Model v0

LW0 is not a bibliography. It binds external research claims to evidence, boundaries, replication status, Ordivon priors, and falsifiers.

The first cohort intentionally spans materially different research families: recursive self-improvement, research agents, Harness/context/state, evaluator integrity, memory/security, tool authority/discovery, world models, execution/state verification, Finance, Game, Web and scientific-frontier selection.

Two coding layers are kept separate:

1. **source-native claim/evidence/boundary** — what the paper/project itself actually establishes;
2. **normalized structural motifs / failure anti-laws** — Ordivon research shorthand used only for cross-source recurrence analysis.

A normalized motif is not a new primitive or shared product layer. It earns confidence only when independent research groups in materially different domains repeatedly force the same semantic distinction.

## Higher-order hypothesis under test

> Robust successful structures may converge toward a small recurring set of causal invariants, while failed implementations exhibit high surface diversity.

LW0 explicitly does **not** assume the stronger claim that failure causes are chaotic. A key falsifier is that diverse-looking failures may themselves collapse onto a smaller recurrent set of anti-laws.

## Evidence tiers

- `project_report_multitask_heldout`: official project report with held-out/multitask evidence, but not an independent publication/replication.
- `preprint_multidomain_or_transfer`: one research group with multiple domains/models/held-outs/transfer.
- `preprint_controlled_or_ablation`: one research group with controlled benchmark/ablation evidence.
- `same_group_followup`: follow-up from the same research lineage; useful but not counted as an independent group for convergence.

Independent support is computed at the **motif** level using unique research groups and domain families, not by counting benchmark rows.
