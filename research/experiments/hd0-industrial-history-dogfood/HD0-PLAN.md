# HD0 — Collection and Analysis Plan

## 1. Purpose

This is a large historical OOD dogfood of the current Ordivon world model, not a history encyclopedia.

The main question is whether structures inferred from modern Agent/RSI experiments survive contact with 250 years of materially different scientific and industrial worlds.

The study begins around the First Industrial Revolution and ends in 2026. A tiny 1650–1759 prelude may be used only to calibrate terms such as instrument, institution and mechanization.

## 2. Why the unit is a trajectory

A famous-invention list creates exactly the wrong ontology. Important technologies often behave like general-purpose technologies: their consequences depend on continued improvement, complementary inputs and diffusion across downstream sectors. Steam power, electrification and computing are canonical examples in the innovation literature.

Therefore each case is reconstructed as a trajectory from pressure through validation, complements, diffusion and correction. The inventor name is metadata, not the causal unit.

## 3. Scale

The frozen main target is 320 trajectories:

```text
8 epochs × 40 trajectories = 320
```

Each epoch contains:

```text
12 deep Anchors
16 additional transformative/breadth cases
 3 failed or abandoned routes
 2 parallel / near-simultaneous discoveries
 2 delayed-adoption / dormant-discovery cases
 2 standards / enabling infrastructures
 2 harmful-externality / safety / regulatory corrections
 1 under-recognized complement / maintenance infrastructure
= 40
```

Ninety-six deep Anchors receive the strongest source contract. Thirty-two de-identified Anchors become historical prospective-replay holdouts.

The 320 target is a floor and a discipline. An epoch may receive a +10 extension block only when the current block exposes substantial new contradiction/residual structure or a serious geographic/domain coverage hole. Stop extending after two consecutive +10 blocks each add <10% new residual/contradictory structural information.

## 4. Chronological waves

### HD1 — 1760–1829

Mechanization, textile machinery, steam efficiency, precision machine tools, iron processes, chemical revolution, early electrochemistry, vaccination, gas/urban infrastructure, early locomotion, metric/metrology systems.

### HD2 — 1830–1869

Railway networks, electric telegraph and cables, electromagnetism/generators, thermodynamics, Bessemer-era steel, anesthesia, germ theory/sanitation, evolution, periodic classification, photography and industrial precision.

### HD3 — 1870–1913

Electric grids, AC/DC competition, telephone, wireless, internal combustion/automobile, synthetic chemistry, Haber-Bosch, refrigeration/cold chain, X-rays, electron/atomic models, early quantum theory, powered flight and assembly-line precursors.

### HD4 — 1914–1945

Mass production, statistical quality control, aviation/aerodynamics, vacuum-tube electronics/radio, radar, quantum mechanics, relativity maturation, nuclear fission/chain reaction, insulin/antibiotics, modern rockets, computation/cryptanalysis and synthetic materials.

### HD5 — 1946–1969

Transistor, integrated circuit, stored-program computing, information theory, laser, molecular biology/DNA, vaccination programs, plate tectonics, Green Revolution, containerization, operations research and space systems.

### HD6 — 1970–1999

Microprocessor/PC, relational data systems, TCP/IP and Internet, Web, cellular systems, GPS, recombinant DNA/PCR, MRI/CT, lithium-ion, semiconductor manufacturing, Earth observation/climate/ozone institutions, production/logistics reforms.

### HD7 — 2000–2014

Genome sequencing, CRISPR discovery, smartphone/mobile ecosystems, cloud/distributed computing, MapReduce/data systems, deep-learning resurgence, solar/wind scale-up, shale/horizontal drilling, modern immunotherapy, HPV/public-health platforms, early reusable-launch programs and decentralized digital money.

### HD8 — 2015–2026

Transformers/foundation models/agents, AlphaFold/protein modeling, diffusion/generative media, CRISPR therapies, mRNA platform deployment, GLP-1 therapeutics, reusable launch at operational scale, modern battery/LFP/EV systems, fusion ignition experiments, single-cell/spatial biology, large-scale renewable/storage integration and contemporary AI-science loops.

The listed topics are seed pools, not preselected winners. Final 40-case epoch rosters are frozen only after bias/coverage review.

## 5. Domain frame

Twelve domains prevent a pure computing/physics history:

1. energy and power;
2. materials, chemistry and industrial processes;
3. manufacturing, machines and production systems;
4. transport and logistics;
5. communications, electronics and computing;
6. mathematics, statistics, control and information;
7. physics, measurement and instrumentation;
8. biology, medicine and public health;
9. agriculture, food and biotechnology;
10. earth, environment and climate;
11. aerospace, navigation and space;
12. standards, institutions, organization and infrastructure.

Empty epoch/domain cells are allowed. Artificially manufacturing an 18th-century counterpart merely to complete a matrix would violate the experiment.

## 6. Source discovery and source truth

Discovery pools include NobelPrize.org, IEEE Milestones/History Center, ASME Engineering Landmarks, ACS National Historic Chemical Landmarks, Smithsonian and Library of Congress collections, NASA/CERN/NIH/CDC/WHO and other owner-institution archives, patent/standards archives and peer-reviewed science/technology/economic history.

Landmark/prize programs are **indexes**, not truth authorities or sampling frames. Nobel begins only in 1901 and explicitly recognizes selected achievements; ASME and ACS curate historically significant engineering/chemistry landmarks; IEEE Milestones spans electrical/computing plus several adjacent technical fields. These programs are excellent candidate finders but structurally winner-biased unless paired with rivals and failures.

Every decisive historical claim binds the exact source and its contemporaneous/retrospective status.

## 7. Two-stage anti-confirmation coding

### Pass A — history first

Collectors know the study topic but do **not** assign LW0 motifs. They reconstruct source-native facts:

```text
pressure
available theories / capabilities
serious candidate routes
instrument / evidence
validation or failure
complements
scaling / diffusion
institutions
externalities / corrections
second-order effects
```

The case becomes `source_locked`.

### Pass B — theory coding

A separate pass maps only locked evidence to the frozen S1–S10 and F1–F7 vocabulary.

Every code can be:

```text
SUPPORTS
NARROWS
CONTRADICTS
NOT_APPLICABLE
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
```

A case is allowed to support none of them.

Any recurrent structure not represented by LW0 goes to `unmappedHistoricalStructures`; it must not be retrofitted into a frozen label.

## 8. Historical prospective replay

History creates hindsight leakage. Thirty-two Anchor cases therefore receive an additional replay:

1. choose a cutoff before decisive validation/diffusion;
2. construct a packet from evidence available by that cutoff;
3. remove unnecessary names, dates, places and famous product labels;
4. freeze an Agent ResearchBet on bottleneck, routes, discriminating test, complements and likely failure mode;
5. reveal later outcome evidence;
6. compute prediction error.

This is not perfectly blind because a pretrained model may remember famous history. De-identification and less-famous cases reduce leakage but cannot erase it. Suspected leakage is evidence, not something to hide.

## 9. Historical questions asked of every deep Anchor

Each Anchor should answer at least:

- What real pressure/anomaly made this worth doing?
- What could relevant actors reasonably know at the time?
- Which routes were serious competitors?
- What observation/instrument/prototype changed the frontier?
- Was there a cheap discriminating falsifier, or did evidence remain ambiguous?
- Which complements made the nominal invention actually usable?
- How long separated discovery, validation, deployment and broad diffusion?
- Which standards/institutions/authority boundaries mattered?
- What failed or was displaced, and why?
- Did scaling introduce new externalities or correction loops?
- Did this development lower the cost of future discovery or create entirely new search spaces?
- Which later mythology falsely compresses a distributed trajectory into one heroic event?

## 10. Special analyses

### Parallel invention

Cases such as telephony, calculus-like priority disputes, flight, radio or semiconductor developments can test whether enabling conditions make independent convergence likely. Priority must be represented as competing Claims, not flattened into one inventor.

### Dormant discoveries

A valid idea that waits decades for materials, instruments, institutions or complementary infrastructure tests whether `correct idea` is sufficient for tractability.

### General-purpose technologies

Steam, electricity, semiconductors/computers and candidate later GPTs are analyzed for pervasiveness, continuous improvement, downstream complementarities and diffusion lag rather than headline invention date.

### Measurement / instrumentation

Microscopy, spectroscopy, X-rays, electron microscopy, sequencing, satellites and other instruments test the hypothesis that better Observation can out-leverage direct solution search.

### Standards / invisible infrastructure

Metrology, interchangeable parts, voltage/frequency standards, shipping containers, TCP/IP, semiconductor process standards and similar cases test whether low-glamour coordination substrates create metaproductive capability.

### Safety / negative externalities

Lead/asbestos/CFCs, industrial accidents, radiation, pharmaceutical safety, environmental regulation and similar trajectories test whether successful local optimization can later fail under a broader domain truth.

## 11. Data plane

Do not create a Historical Research Service.

Use ordinary files plus deterministic tooling:

```text
source manifests / trajectory JSON
→ JSONL / Parquet
→ DuckDB queries
→ recurrence / contradiction tables
→ Agent interpretation
```

The existing RSI Lab may be used for mechanical experiment matrices, frozen evidence packs involving Ordivon owner evidence, contraction verification and deterministic analysis. It never decides which historical case matters or whether a theory succeeded.

## 12. Measures

Do not produce `greatnessScore` or `innovationScore`.

Track vectors:

- epistemic delta;
- technical capability delta;
- generalization/domain breadth;
- diffusion breadth and latency;
- complementary-dependency structure;
- metaproductivity / future research capacity;
- validation latency;
- safety/externality burden;
- organizational/institutional transition;
- path dependence / lock-in.

The cross-case synthesis uses recurrence and partial order, not one scalar.

## 13. Success and falsification

The campaign succeeds scientifically even if LW0 fails.

Especially valuable outcomes are:

- a famous historical success that contradicts a modern invariant;
- a repeated historical structure absent from LW0;
- an anti-law that disappears outside modern Agent systems;
- a supposedly universal success motif that applies only after a particular institutional/technological era;
- a failed technology that obeyed all of our current priors yet still failed for a missing variable.

If 320 trajectories produce no serious contradiction, run a confirmation-bias/codebook audit before strengthening the convergence thesis.
