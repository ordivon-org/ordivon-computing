# D — Human comprehension / retention / transfer transport gate

Status: **system-level transport analysis first; direct Human A/B dormant. No Human outcome evidence exists.**

## Current Human-owner boundary

`ordivon-human/research/agentic-capability/EVIDENCE-TRANSPORT.md` is current authority for this question. Its default path is:

```text
external Human evidence
→ mechanism and moderator boundary
→ Ordivon structural transport analysis
→ natural Ordivon dogfood
→ bounded residual experiment only if still decision-relevant
```

Therefore measurement availability is not enough to justify a new direct Human study. This Representation Effect programme supplies the Ordivon-specific system falsification stage first.

## External prior being transported

Learning science already supplies population-level reasons to separate:

- immediate performance from delayed retention;
- passive re-exposure from active retrieval/reconstruction;
- near/far transfer from verbatim recall;
- instructional support effects by prior knowledge and task/element interactivity.

These priors do not establish an Ordivon-specific Human treatment effect. They define moderators and outcomes that a residual study would have to preserve.

## Transport questions before any Human experiment

1. Do Agent/system experiments show a representation effect large enough to create a plausible Human decision difference?
2. Is the effect about taxonomy, source search geometry, persistent trace formation, or some other mechanism?
3. Does current Ordivon Human work naturally require dense simultaneous Result classification often enough for the representation to matter?
4. Does the candidate representation alter what the Human must internalize, or can it remain operation-specific external support?
5. Is there a known moderator—prior knowledge, task complexity, delay, interruption, recovery without aid—that makes population evidence insufficient for the target use?
6. Would competing representation policies lead to a real change in Book/Media/Human environment design?

If these questions do not leave a consequential transport gap, do not run direct Human A/B.

## Natural dogfood first

The preferred next Human evidence is ordinary Ordivon work in which a Human naturally needs to distinguish Result, Achievement, Improvement, Consumption or Benefit. Record only what the work already requires:

- whether explicit Result/Value support was requested or naturally consulted;
- whether the Human could reconstruct the needed relation without it;
- whether a source/evidence card was more useful than a taxonomy;
- later recovery when the aid is unavailable;
- correction of an intentionally or naturally encountered category error;
- interaction time and subjective burden only as secondary measures.

Do not force every ordinary task into a learning test.

## Residual direct-comparison protocol — admit only if transport gap survives

If natural dogfood leaves two genuinely competing policies, use a pre-registered Book-only vs candidate-representation comparison with:

- immediate comprehension containing positive admissions and overclaim controls;
- delayed unaided reconstruction after an unrelated task;
- near and far transfer cases not seen during study;
- seeded-error verification and aid-removal recovery;
- confidence calibration, not only correctness;
- reading/interaction time and subjective effort as representation costs;
- counterbalancing or between-subject assignment appropriate to carryover risk;
- frozen oracle, exclusion and stopping rules.

LLM personas are not Human evidence. Agent context-interference results are not Human memory results. A short laboratory effect is not automatically professional-practice standing.
