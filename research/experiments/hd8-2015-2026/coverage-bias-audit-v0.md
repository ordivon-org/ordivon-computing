# HD8 Coverage / Bias Audit — pre-theory

## Candidate contraction

The discovery pool contains exactly **120** source-native candidates across science, engineering, standards, institutions, infrastructure, failure and safety correction. The exact roster is frozen at **40** before any S1–S10/F1–F7 or R/N residual mapping.

## Composition

The HD0 composition is preserved: 12 Deep Anchors + 16 breadth transformations + 3 failed/abandoned + 2 parallel + 2 delayed + 2 standards/infrastructure + 2 safety/externality corrections + 1 under-recognized complement.

## Bias controls

- Modern digital/computing and biomedicine are intrinsically overrepresented in 2015–2026 candidate visibility; the exact roster contracts them with energy, manufacturing, public health, environment, transport, astronomy and aerospace cases.
- North-American/European institutions remain overrepresented in primary Web-accessible documentation. This is recorded as a source-access/geography limitation rather than disguised by nominal quota filling.
- Corporate sources are used only where they are the direct operational authority for the event (for example SpaceX reflight or an explicit product shutdown); decisive cross-domain claims remain bounded to what those sources establish.
- Failures are first-class: Loon, Theranos and Kemper are retained alongside 737 MAX and Volkswagen correction cases. These failures span commercial viability, diagnostic measurement/representation failure, industrial-route economics and deliberate evaluator evasion.
- Standards and institutions are causal trajectories rather than background metadata: 3GPP, W3C, NASA Commercial Crew and WHO genomic surveillance are represented directly.
- Contemporary AI is not allowed to dominate the sample. Transformer/foundation models are one Deep Anchor; GPT-4 is one breadth/control; AI compute is a complement.

## Anti-case freeze

Five anti-cases are frozen before theory mapping: A03 Transformer/foundation models, A07 PV+battery/grid, A11 NIF ignition, F01 Loon, and F02 Theranos. They attack over-simple claims about generality, local component improvement, physical proof, technical/commercial success, and visible evaluator/representation validity.

## Replay boundary
