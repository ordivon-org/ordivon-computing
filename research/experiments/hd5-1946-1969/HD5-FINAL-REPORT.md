## Anti-case synthesis

### Principle != platform
The transistor effect did not automatically produce a scalable electronics platform. Surface passivation, purity, controlled diffusion/masking, yield and batch repeatability become new research objects. Platform claims require a different evidence chain from device physics.

### Efficacy != manufactured product safety
The Salk field result and the Cutter failure can both be true. One evaluates biological efficacy under trial manufacture; the other exposes industrial process/lot-release failure. A passed evaluator cannot silently inherit authority over the next lifecycle phase.

### Directional truth != present tractability
Continental drift-like direction can remain low-tractability until ocean-floor observations create discriminating predictions. Later tractability does not retroactively give earlier researchers later evidence.

### Directness != whole-system simplicity
Lunar-orbit rendezvous introduces an extra rendezvous operation yet reduces landed/ascent mass enough to close the lunar objective under finite launch capacity and deadline. Architectural complexity must be evaluated at the whole-system objective boundary.

### Spend/subsystem progress != viable mission
The nuclear-aircraft program demonstrates that sustained investment and real component progress do not imply whole-system closure under mass, shielding, safety and mission constraints.
