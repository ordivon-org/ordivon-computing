## Anti-bias decisions

The roster deliberately prevents a postwar-US-computing winner narrative. It includes Japan (Toyota/Shinkansen), Soviet Union (laser theory, nuclear power, polio deployment context), Mexico/India/Pakistan/Philippines (Green Revolution), Britain/Canada/Atlantic ocean science (plate tectonics), international WHO/ISO/INTELSAT institutions, and failed programs (nuclear aircraft, Mohole, Brabazon).

The 12 Anchor set spans communications/computing, biology/medicine, earth science, energy, aerospace, logistics, manufacturing, agriculture and physics. Mathematics/control and standards remain represented in breadth/control rather than being artificially promoted to Anchor status.

Five anti-cases were frozen before theory coding: semiconductor manufacturability, polio efficacy-versus-manufacturing safety, plate-tectonics tractability, Apollo architecture choice, and nuclear-aircraft whole-system failure. Their role is to attack later interpretation, not pre-decide a theory verdict.

Replay candidates were frozen as A01 semiconductor, A04 polio, A05 plate tectonics and A07 Apollo to span device/manufacturing, public-health evaluation, observational science and large systems engineering.
