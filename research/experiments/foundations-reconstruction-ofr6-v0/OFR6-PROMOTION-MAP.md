## Owner-local causal evidence

All ten OFR3 causal cases remain `OWNER_LOCAL`. They are exact historical/current owner evidence used to support or falsify shared intersections; they are not copied into a central theory store.

## Contracted shared forms

The following active shared forms are rejected while their historical evidence remains in Git:

- **D1 — winner-only as causal theory**: OFR4 holdout falsified recovery of rival/falsifier/counterfactual/boundary. Preserve as negative control evidence only.
- **D2 — causal-kernel/minimal-chain as universal canonical packet**: OFR4 compressed treatments failed frozen semantic non-inferiority; do not keep an active canonical packet shape.
- **D3 — two-independent-call ATLAS_PROGRESSIVE product**: OFR5 failed physical reliability and failure-adjusted cost gates; retain only research evidence.
- **D4 — INDEX_ONLY as causal theory**: OFR5 supports it as locator only; causal-role/ref fidelity was insufficient.
- **D5 — CENTRALIZED_LAWS as owner-local causal history**: OFR5 strongly falsified exact case/history/ref recovery and showed overgeneralization.
- **D6 — model-selected per-role hydration as default public abstraction**: OFR5 models systematically over-hydrated and did not earn effective-cost savings; exact case-level hydration remains research pattern.
