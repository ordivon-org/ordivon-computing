# Working Foundations

This file contains the smallest current foundations from which the project’s architecture and research direction can be regenerated. Explanations, counterexamples, and sources live in [`../knowledge/`](../knowledge/) and [`../studies/`](../studies/).

## A0 — Begin from operational reality

Foundation models are the current probabilistic cognition baseline. Their architectures and capabilities will change, so the Core preserves stable system responsibilities rather than one model family, prompt pattern, or harness workaround.

## A1 — Inherit the classical substrate

Operating systems, isolation mechanisms, databases, networks, version control, compilers, model runtimes, and durable workflow engines remain authoritative for their declared physical and deterministic contracts. Agent-native semantics are constructed above them unless evidence proves a lower contract insufficient.

## A2 — Models produce proposals under selected context

Models interpret, infer, plan, generate, compare, and revise from a bounded context. Their outputs are useful probabilistic proposals, not automatically durable state, authorized actions, external observations, verified facts, or completed work.

## A3 — Open goals lower through revisable work

Natural language can express purpose before the execution path is known. A persistent Agent system progressively lowers a Goal into Tasks, Attempts, Claims, Effects, verification, and executable actions while allowing new evidence to revise the work frontier.

## A4 — Work outlives model and process episodes

A Task is not identical to a conversation, model session, process, Job, or Tool call. Durable work state preserves current purpose, dependencies, Attempts, uncertainty, world bindings, Artifacts, and next admissible work across replacement and interruption.

## A5 — Context is a compiled view, not durable truth

Parameters, KV state, conversation history, task state, durable knowledge, Artifacts, and external world state have different identities and lifetimes. Context is the bounded selected view supplied to one cognitive episode; critical authority and execution history must not depend on remaining inside it.

## A6 — Authority binds purpose to consequence

Credentials and operating-system permissions determine physical reach. Agent Effect admission may additionally bind principal, delegated purpose, target and version, operation, consequence envelope, budget, policy, expiry, and revocation state.

## A7 — Effects are first-class commitments

An Effect preserves the stable intended observation or change. Its identity is separate from a concrete Dispatch, backend Job, and declared idempotency behavior. Lost responses remain explicitly uncertain until reality is reconciled.

## A8 — Evidence mediates knowledge admission

Observation, Artifact, Claim, Verification, and Fact are distinct roles. A model statement or successful Tool call does not become accepted system truth without the evidence and authority required by the domain.

## A9 — Humans retain purpose and consequence ownership

Agents can expand search and execution throughput, but people remain responsible for goals, value judgments, and consequences. Human attention is scarce and should be requested at meaningful decisions rather than every low-level action.

## A10 — New layers require unowned non-bypassable responsibility

A concept becomes an Agent-native layer only when a stable contract owns an invariant not already provided below, bypassing it causes a real failure class, and the abstraction creates leverage across materially different workloads. Research interest alone does not imply implementation or a new repository.

## A11 — Knowledge grows through evidence and deletion

Claims evolve through primary sources, explicit reasoning, prototypes, failure traces, reproducible experiments, benchmarks, and sustained use. Core statements must survive counterexamples and deletion tests; unstable mechanisms remain in Studies, Knowledge, or Research.

## Research cycle

```text
observe
→ distinguish classical responsibility from Agent pressure
→ formulate a falsifiable question
→ construct the smallest useful artifact
→ run against reality and a strong baseline
→ revise or delete the abstraction
→ preserve the result at the lowest sufficient layer
```

The Core remains small enough to load, challenge, and reconstruct as one coherent model.
